## Perspective coverage

| Target | Coverage condition | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Recorded inventory | Every item in a named source snapshot is represented | Items absent from that source |
| Finite construction space | Every assignment satisfying declared finite rules has been examined | Missing dimensions, values or rules |
| Declared case coverage | Every case in a fixed domain receives the required distinction or contribution | Cases and adequacy requirements outside that domain |
| General perspective space | Every admissible perspective is captured under a justified identity relation | No such complete basis is established here |

## Perspective construction

| Operation | Input | Transformation | Result | Continuation condition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Target substitution | A matter and its current interpretation | Hold the surrounding situation fixed; replace what is under examination | A different determination with a distinct required contribution | Find what result would differ between the targets |
| Bearer substitution | A response attributed to one entity | Assign the candidate configuration to a component, interaction or larger system; specify the observable each attribution explains | Competing bearer attributions | Identify a change that separates their predictions or explanatory coverage |
| Distinction construction | Two cases currently treated as equivalent | Identify a difference that changes an intended consequence; state its possible values and conditions | A new candidate dimension with a separating case | Check whether the difference was already encoded elsewhere |
| Role substitution | A subject with one current use | Change what the result is used for while retaining its content | A different relevance relationship | Trace the changed next operation |
| Scope variation | A fixed interpretation and declared domain | Change a boundary, time span or unit; preserve which assumptions still hold | A qualified extension or limitation | Identify a case the changed scope newly admits or excludes |
| Temporary adoption | A candidate with a specified contrast | Use it provisionally to construct the next question or operation | A concrete consequence of the candidate | Separate usefulness of the trial from truth of its premise |
| Assumption substitution | A current assumption and a specified alternative | Retain the matter; derive the consequential differences under the alternative | A branching interpretation | Seek a distinguishing observation or argument |
| Perspective composition | Contributors with defined results | Specify which result supplies which later input and under what conditions | An arrangement with inspectable dependencies | Check incompatibility and order dependence |
| Condition creation | A desirable perspective inaccessible under current conditions | Construct an encounter, record, resource or operation that supplies a missing condition | A route to making the perspective usable | Attempt the condition-producing operation or establish why it is unavailable |
| Exclusion reconsideration | A candidate excluded for a recorded reason | Recheck the reason against the actual intended contribution | Retention, reinstatement or a narrower justified exclusion | Preserve both the original exclusion and its changed scope |
