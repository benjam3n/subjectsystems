## Perspective bearer attribution

| Condition | Operation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| A hormone, gene, cell or agent response is described without locating the responsible configuration. | Fix the response distinction; compare candidate bearers; specify which components each contains; vary or remove a component in the stated causal account; identify which attribution explains the retained and lost distinctions. | A scoped attribution or competing attributions with a distinguishing intervention. |

| Case | Finding | Neighbor | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Holding hormone identity fixed while varying the receiving configuration separates ligand identity from the whole response arrangement. | One causal episode can support different bearer attributions for different explanatory targets. | System identity specification | Assigning consciousness, goals or all downstream effects to the named component. |
