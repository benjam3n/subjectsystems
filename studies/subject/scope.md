A subject is a specified matter of consideration. Its scope consists of the matters admitted by its definition.

| Relation | Definition |
|---|---|
| Subject scope containment | Every matter admitted by the child is admitted by the parent. |
| Subject scope exclusion | The two scopes admit no identical matter under the same interpretation and conditions. |
| Subject scope coverage | Every matter in the stated domain is admitted by at least one scope. |
| Subject scope collision | One fully specified matter satisfies two definitions intended to exclude each other. |
| Subject scope gap | An in-domain matter satisfies none of the available definitions. |
| Subject name equivalence | Different names identify the same specified matter. |
| Subject qualification | A condition narrows the matter identified by a name. |
| System composition | Contributors are connected through specified interfaces, dependencies, conditions, and operations. |
| Study result dependence | A later result requires a specified contribution from an earlier study. |

Question creation constructs a requested distinction, referents, assumptions, and resolution conditions. It is contained in question specification. Question record modification can preserve or change the question identified by the record.
