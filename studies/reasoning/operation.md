| Operation | Material changed | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Distinguish previously merged cases | A representation that treats different cases alike | Different cases can warrant different next work |
| Change a role | A claim, constraint, method, or result and its current role | A conclusion can be questioned; a method can be investigated; a constraint can be varied |
| Hold alternatives open | A candidate set before selection | Several possibilities remain usable without premature synthesis |
| Adopt a temporary assumption | A possibility and its conditional context | Consequences can be developed without asserting the assumption as fact |
| Withdraw a specific exclusion reason | A rejected candidate and the reason for rejection | Candidates excluded solely by that reason are reopened; independent reasons remain |
| Change a criterion | A standard applied to candidates or outcomes | A prior verdict or next choice may change while the artifact itself stays the same |
| Change a frame | The same subject represented through a different organizing account | New distinctions, questions, or operations may become available |
| Exclude an anticipated continuation | A substantive purpose and one candidate continuation | Another contribution must preserve the purpose while departing from the excluded continuation |
| Follow an incidental connection | An observation outside the current route | A trial can reveal an unanticipated question or useful relation |
| Propagate a correction | A changed premise, interpretation, or criterion and its dependent uses | Affected work is reopened while independent support remains |
| Compose operations | The output and conditions of one operation and the input needs of another | Order, compatibility, and retained distinctions determine what the composition can do |
| Develop a missing operation | A needed transformation unavailable in the current repertoire | A new method becomes a candidate for use and further study |
