# Optional working forms

These are prompts for retaining consequential work when a form helps. They are not mandatory admission gates, required per-turn output, or a fixed theory of inquiry.

- [Custom system](custom-system.md): local purpose, operations, result, criteria, and source changes.
- [Subject development](subject.md): a branch, its actual questions, source contributions, and what changes.
- [Worked transition](case.md): a real operation and its consequence, including uncertainty and failure where relevant.

Use a simpler or different representation when it preserves the distinctions the work needs.
