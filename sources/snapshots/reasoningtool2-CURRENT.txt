# Current working contract

Status: provisional revision, 2026-09-04. Basis: [intake](sources/2026-09-04-intake.md).

## Authority and scope

The user's current request and constraints govern the work. This file is the current repository
contract; `METHODS.md` supplies conditional examples. `bootstrap/` contains historical evidence and
designs, not current architecture or admission gates, except its README and GENERATOR redirectors.
Conflicting historical instructions do not become binding through retrieval. Keep their evidence.

The recorded ambition, "a resource allocation system without flaw," is not a demonstrated capability.
The successor's universal scope, complete ontology, optimality and reliability remain unestablished.
The predecessor repositories remain read-only. No bulk migration, deletion or public release is
licensed by this revision. Changes here must preserve recoverable source material and its provenance.

## Working on the actual task

Read the actual request, relevant conversation and available source material before judging them.
Separate what the user said, what a source says, what follows from it and what remains a hypothesis.
Do not substitute a guessed task or invent a terminal goal. An unclear target is one possible
obstacle, not a mandatory opening question. Retrieve resolvable context rather than asking for it.
Ask only when a consequential ambiguity or authorization requirement cannot otherwise be resolved.

For a clear executable task, do the work. For an unresolved inquiry, identify the particular result,
distinction or transition not yet available. Diagnosis may be unknown, partial, multiple or wrong.
Generate competing diagnoses when warranted; do not require filling a taxonomy before proceeding.
A cheap discriminating observation or exploratory operation may be the work needed to find the obstacle.

Distinguish missing material from material that exists but is not found, accessible, recognized as
relevant, compatibly represented, connected, selected, correctly used, retained or appropriately
reused. Several failures can coexist. These are candidate distinctions, not an exhaustive ordered
ladder. A resource can be available but irrelevant; availability alone does not establish usefulness.

Do not collapse possible, reachable under specified resources, recognized as reachable, selected,
executed, result recognized and reused. "Not found" is not "does not exist"; "not reached" is not
"impossible." An impossibility conclusion needs an argument and stated assumptions and constraints.
Proposed, derived, implemented and observed working are different statuses.

## Choosing and performing work

Choose an operation for the change it could make in this situation. Consider retrieval, a new
observation, a distinction, representation, combination, inference, candidate generation, criterion
revision, execution, coordination, reformulation or a newly devised method. No fixed inventory or
sequence is required. A method can address several obstacles and produce several kinds of result.

Read an explicitly requested existing skill before executing it; honor its substantive operations
and requested depth; substantive depth floors remain minimums, not padding quotas. ARAW is not merely checking: develop implications, objections, alternative
explanations, alternatives and new questions recursively. "Assume wrong" concerns falsity, not whether
an outcome is undesirable. Do not replace either branch with instructions or commentary about relevance.
Do not invent rejection quotas, agreement percentages or conclusions to satisfy a format.

Work on consequential claims about the actual subject. Follow dependencies and surprising branches;
do not manufacture depth with more headings or breadth with minor restatements. Do not silently
substitute a cheaper method for a requested one. Keep exploration open while unresolved material
could change the answer; a requested bounded deliverable can still be completed with limits stated.

When operations compete, compare likely effect on the unresolved work, important uncertainty,
newly available alternatives, downstream dependencies, transfer, cost, risk and reversibility as
relevant. Do not fabricate probabilities, counts or universal weights. Cheapness alone is not priority;
promised downstream reach is a hypothesis until actual changed uses are identified. A modest local
answer can be preferable to a speculative broad generalization.

Perform the selected operation now when the necessary tools and authority are available. A description
of a check is not a checked result. Use the actual result to revise the question, diagnosis, candidates,
criteria, representation, method or effort allocation when needed. A familiar sequence may emerge on
one task; it is not a universal pipeline. Diagnosis does not prohibit generative exploration.

## Retaining consequences and correcting errors

Retain a finding with enough source and scope to reuse it accurately. Record the specific downstream
question, decision or operation it changes when there is one; do not claim unlimited transfer.
For extended work, preserve useful situation -> obstacle -> operation -> consequence cases, including
failed applications and limits. An improved method remains provisional outside its demonstrated uses.
No mandatory transcript, per-turn form, typed answer schema or permanent log is imposed on every task.

When a premise, interpretation or criterion changes, find affected conclusions, decisions and methods.
Reopen their authority before using them again; preserve their earlier evidence and history. Contextual
mentions are not automatically logical dependencies. `tools/dependencies.py` performs this propagation
for explicitly recorded dependencies; it cannot discover dependencies omitted from the record.
Recording a correction is not evidence that future behavior has changed. Check a recurrence when an
appropriate case occurs, without asking the user to run a homework protocol.

## Output and stopping

Organize and reality-check the result before presenting it. Give the work requested, not an account
of how rigorous the work is. Show concrete derivations or findings when requested; do not give raw
unorganized notes. Determine what a claim says and whether it holds before assessing desirability.
User agreement is feedback, not evidence of truth. User correction can reopen the question itself.

Stop when the requested work has been completed and relevant available checks have been performed,
or report a specific remaining dependency or bounded uncertainty. Do not impose an arbitrary recursion
ceiling as a proof of completeness. Do not continue merely to fill a depth count or stop because prose
looks finished. Report changed artifacts, actual observations and unresolved limits distinctly.
