## Study

| Subject | Question | Result | Technique |
|---|---|---|---|
| Preference hypothesis creation | Infer which selected aspects explain a preference across materially different candidates. | Produce a preference hypothesis and a contrasting candidate that could refute it. | Unspecified |
| Design candidate construction | Construct new candidate forms that retain selected aspects while changing other dimensions. | Produce a candidate with explicit preserved aspects and actual new differences. | Unspecified |
| Resource configuration feasibility | Determine whether one specified candidate can satisfy its requirements under actual constraints. | Give a feasibility verdict whose limiting conditions can change the next iteration. | Unspecified |
