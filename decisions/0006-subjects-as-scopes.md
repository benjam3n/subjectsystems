# 0006 — Subjects as scopes of consideration

Date: 7 September 2026.

Standing: Implements the user's request to define what each subject actually includes and excludes, following the clarification of what a subject is. The requirement is user direction; the particular definitions are the working derivation produced here.

## Correction

A subject is what is being considered. Processes, properties, relations, and questions can be subjects. The earlier audit's distinction among entity kinds did not by itself decide which things qualify as subjects. The relevant boundary is the matter included in a scope.

An unrestricted Questions subject and unrestricted Creation subject both contain question creation. Choosing one home does not fix that overlap. Define their actual scopes and the relation represented by the hierarchy.

## Implementation

The repository now contains 28 defined scopes within 11 parent scopes, with precise matters, inclusions, exclusions, examples, and next substantive results. Parents mean the union of the stated children. Further investigations such as question creation, inferential validity, and improvement are located as contained scopes rather than forced peers.

All 39 earlier labels have an explicit disposition. Planning and Strategy are merged in their shared course-structure sense. Knowledge and other ambiguous labels are split into determinate senses. Research, Science, and Philosophy are retained as broad programmes whose methodological aspects can be studied without absorbing all their contents. Promoting remains withdrawn and previously excluded roots are not reinstated.

All ten written local designs have their actual contributions mapped, with generic target-construction gaps explicit. All 21 source families have development directions at their existing source standing. No new executions or effectiveness results are inferred from those mappings.

## Boundary finding

An initial broad intervention-effect scope collided with instruction uptake and experiential change. The implemented R2 is narrowed to remedy adequacy: whether a specified effect, if obtained, addresses the formulated problem. The fact that the effect occurs is a distinct target inquiry. This changes the result that an R2 system must justify.

Constructed cases support several other exclusions and identify an unrepresented target-specific interface contract. A first model and tested distinctions are established; universal coverage and a proof over every possible matter are not.

## Consequences

The current subject entry points use `subjects/scopes/`. Legacy paths retain earlier findings, design operations, and provenance, with current interpretations linked. There is no further mass relocation. Structural checks continue to check navigation and immutable source identity, not semantic perfection.

Future work must use the stated determinations rather than a noun, source name, primary purpose, or closest fit. A genuine single-matter collision requires repair. A new matter outside coverage requires a scope, not a catch-all.

This extends and corrects [0005](0005-non-overlapping-subjects.md) on the meaning of subject while retaining its rejection of shared-work grouping. The earlier audit remains useful evidence of collisions; it is no longer the endpoint of the work.
