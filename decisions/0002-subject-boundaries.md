Standing: mixed; see the distinctions below. Recorded 7 September 2026.

Subsequent correction: [0005 — Non-overlapping subjects](0005-non-overlapping-subjects.md) rejects the functional grouping introduced by 0004 and withdraws Promoting. Connections and Improvement remain proposals requiring precise boundaries; neither earlier placement settles their admission.

## Established direction

The starting relevance map is retained with Education and Collaboration removed as top-level entries. Their useful contents are not discarded. The user also requested reconsideration of Integration, Evaluation, Automation, and Self-improvement.

There are 37 retained entries in the resulting starting list. Promoting retains its original wording but not the earlier assistant's unconfirmed marketing interpretation. Its distinction from Prompting remains unresolved.

Connections and Improvement are visible as proposed roots so their content can be developed without presenting them as settled user decisions. The other retained roots are likewise not declared a final ontology.

## Consequential tests of the arrangement

An alternative arrangement should be judged through a concrete difference: a question it exposes, a false equivalence it prevents, a contribution it makes retrievable, or a use it preserves. Education is not excluded from the entire project, and rejecting Evaluation as a root does not demonstrate that evaluation and judgment are fundamentally different activities.

Work whose proper place is unresolved can remain visible with the uncertainty stated. It need not be forced into an existing category before being investigated.
