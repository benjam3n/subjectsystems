# 0001 — Name and primary organization

Standing: established by the conversation and the instruction to set up the already-created repository. Recorded 7 September 2026.

Subsequent refinement: [0003](0003-custom-systems.md) develops the subject-first direction into custom systems for each subject and purpose. This record preserves the initial decision; a shared source placement alone is no longer the complete working architecture.

## Name

Visible name: **Subject Systems**. Repository: **subjectsystems**. Description: **Systems organized around subjects**.

The name should identify an entity and remain general while making a modest claim. The naming discussion favored simple, literal words with depth in their actual meaning. Grand claims of mastery, completeness, universal understanding, or readiness were repeatedly reduced. System Kit was liked, then a lower claim was requested. The user proposed subjectsystems and accepted that direction before authorizing setup.

This selection does not establish domain availability, trademark clearance, or a decision to publish the private repository. Earlier name proposals are historical material.

## Organization

The user's central change was that systems become components of the subjects to which they are relevant. Subjects are the beginnings of hierarchies that can develop into deeply thought-through sciences. A particular system can have several justified placements.

Reasoning, Goals, Discovery, Preferences, Questions, and the other subjects can therefore contain understanding and methods beyond those already encoded by Reasoningtool, GOSM, SDS, Discovery Engine, or QuestionRoute. Existing systems remain important sources with recoverable identities and native structures.

## Implementation choice for this setup

The primary working pages live under subjects/. System profiles and source records are secondary indexes. Shared cases can serve several subjects. This physical arrangement implements the accepted direction and remains revisable if actual use exposes a better one.

Source: the visible repository-review, subject-reorganization, and naming conversation. No independently addressable chat export was supplied; the record distinguishes direct decisions from reconstructed interpretation.
