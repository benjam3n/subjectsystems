# 0005 — Non-overlapping subjects

Standing: Current user requirement, 7 September 2026. Supersedes the classification basis of 0004.

## Correction

The user rejected grouping subjects by shared work. They require distinguishable things whose placement follows from what they are, with no overlapping sibling categories. They also questioned the hierarchy's direction: Questions could be above creation, although that particular replacement was itself tentative.

The eight functional groups do not satisfy the requirement. Giving a subject one main home plus connections elsewhere answers a filing question, not the user's classification question. The original topic roster also cannot be assumed to be a valid collection of peers.

## Established requirements

- Define the classified unit and the relation represented by each level.
- Use membership conditions that distinguish siblings; convenience, primary purpose, and relevance are insufficient to make overlapping classes exclusive.
- Preserve containment where a child is a kind of its parent. Do not silently substitute uses, creates, concerns, or contributes to for that relation.
- Treat an object, its representation, an operation on it, its properties, and a containing workflow as distinct matters when those differences determine placement.
- Reconsider the existing labels themselves, including splits, narrower senses, and removal. Preserve substantive source work independently of its former category.
- Continue developing systems for the particular subject and purpose. The customization decision remains valid; its objects and boundaries need this correction as well.

## Promotion

Promoting had no established definition. Its adoption-oriented development in the earlier proposal was an assistant interpretation. The label is now withdrawn from the candidate subject roster and retained only as a historical record. No assumption that it means marketing, influence, or Prompting is adopted.

## What this revision establishes

[DISTINCTIONS.md](../DISTINCTIONS.md) derives the exclusion criterion, distinguishes Questions from creation of questions, and supplies a bounded example with explicit scope and identity limits. The [audit](../research/SUBJECT-AUDIT.md) covers every existing topic label.

These are substantive corrections and boundary analyses. They do not establish a perfected replacement tree. The old group pages and folders remain available as legacy navigation, clearly marked as rejected classification. Retaining an address does not endorse its former parent relation.

## Implementation consequence

The main README and subject entry point now present the corrected requirement, analysis, and audit. Agent instructions no longer direct new work into a shared-work group. Old group pages and topic placements are marked as legacy; Promoting is marked withdrawn. Original evidence and the existing local design specifications remain available. Structural checks verify retained navigation and identity, and explicitly do not certify non-overlap.

The current result can be developed without requiring the user to organize the retained notes or choose among undefined categories. A later replacement must earn its boundaries through actual definitions and exclusion cases.
