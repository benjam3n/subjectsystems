# Systems developed for subjects and purposes

The [current scope model](subjects/scopes/README.md) defines the matters developed. The [contribution map](research/SCOPE-SYSTEMS.md) specifies how existing designs relate to them, including compositions and remaining target-specific gaps.

The current direction is to develop custom versions of the projects for each subject, given what the subject is trying to do. This applies across all project families. An existing project supplies material from which a local system is developed; its relevance alone does not supply the local system.

The design unit is **subject + particular purpose + local system**. A subject can need several versions from the same source for different purposes. A local system can combine several source families or introduce operations absent from all of them.

The [current subject work](subjects/README.md) requires explicit non-overlapping boundaries. The former functional groups are rejected as classification; they survive only as addresses for existing files. A subject and an operation concerning it must be distinguished: question creation and goal creation can require distinct systems even when they share source mechanisms. A local system's objects and boundaries must be examined directly, not inferred from its former group. Existing specifications remain in their legacy topic systems/ directories.

## What makes a version custom

| Part | What is determined locally |
|---|---|
| Purpose | The particular understanding, activity, or result being developed |
| Objects | What the system actually operates on and must distinguish |
| Operations | Transformations, selection rules, branches, and order needed here |
| Result | What the system supplies and which subsequent work can use it |
| Standards | What counts as success, failure, adequate support, or an unresolved outcome |
| Continuation | When to iterate, change a frame, seek a missing observation, stop, or pursue what success enables |
| Lineage | Which source material was retained, changed, dropped, or supplemented |

These distinctions guide development. They are not a mandatory response schema or an exhaustive ontology of systems. A changed title or subject argument is insufficient where the purpose requires a different operation or criterion. Shared operations can remain shared where their suitability is established; every line of code need not be duplicated to give the local design its own authority.

## Admitting a source contribution

The [study derivation](research/studies/SUBJECT-STUDY-SYSTEM.md) separates the subject, its study program, a particular inquiry, a reusable operation and the system composition. A custom design states how its contributors connect; naming their subjects alone does not supply that structure.

An operative technique must have the locally necessary input, transformation, output, applicability and termination conditions. The [admission review](research/studies/TECHNIQUES.md) separately examines specification, branch coverage, rule consistency and evidence of effect. A defined parameter retains useful generality; an unexplained choice or missing operation remains a development gap. If a repair is needed, state what changed instead of importing the source whole. The current review adds three executed finite systems to the earlier eleven designs.

## SDS gives different local systems

| Subject | Local purpose | Consequential change |
|---|---|---|
| Goals | Develop an aim and its achievement conditions | Generate candidate aims and separate favored means from intended ends |
| Preferences | Distinguish competing explanations of selections | Generate diagnostic contrasts and retain conditional preference hypotheses |
| Design | Develop an artifact and its requirements | Generate concrete forms, track selected aspects, and examine feasibility |
| Creativity | Develop different possibilities | Retain several generative directions; convergence on one winner is optional |
| Judgment | Develop the standard for a verdict | Compare candidate criteria across cases and reopen affected verdicts |
| Improvement | Develop a better version of an object | Preserve a baseline and separate favorable selection from observed improvement |

These versions have specifications under their subjects. Their common ancestry does not make their outputs or success conditions interchangeable.

## All families, open subject coverage

Reasoningtool, GOSM, QuestionRoute, ARAW, Discovery Engine, SDS, and the other project families all enter this development process. The initial relevance map supplies a first set of purposes, not the only permissible pairings. The absence of a family from a subject's initial table does not establish that a custom system cannot be developed from it.

An unresolved source or subject meaning remains a definite design gap. Record that gap instead of manufacturing an apparently complete variant. The current [development plan](research/CUSTOMIZATION-PLAN.md) distinguishes specified local designs from the remaining work.

## Source and descendant identity

Preserve the original source and its version as evidence. Give each local design its own identity, purpose, and standing. A change to a descendant is not an edit to its ancestor, and an ancestor's later revision does not automatically overwrite its descendants.

Native source distinctions should be understood before they are changed. The local system may deliberately redefine its objects, sequence, or criteria where the subject requires it. Document that change and preserve the context of any original evidence consumed. For example, a QuestionRoute-derived preferences system can replace the native questioning-survival status with explicit contextual evidence without claiming that the source already did so.

Several local systems can use a common observation or implementation. Sharing that item does not collapse their separate purposes or verdicts. A person's approval can be preference evidence while leaving the truth or utility of a claim unresolved.

## Development and evidence

Specify the actual local operations. Exercise them on a case that would produce a materially different result or continuation under another version. Preserve the result, failure cases, and conditions for reuse. Add code where the purpose requires execution or persistent state; a written method and its implementation are different deliverables.

Use accurate standing: source relevance, a proposed adaptation, a specified design, an actual execution, and evidence of effectiveness are different. These need not form a single maturity ladder, and repeated successes do not by themselves establish universal perfection.

The initial customization revision specifies ten local systems, including all six existing SDS destinations and designs derived from GOSM, Discovery Engine, QuestionRoute, Reasoningtool2, and TruthFinder. It does not claim that every family has already been customized for every subject.
