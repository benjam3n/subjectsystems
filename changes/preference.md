What the person prefers depends on a tradeoff that remains abstract.

Make the live alternatives concrete enough to reveal the consequential tradeoff.

Example: I want both a very short answer and enough detail to reproduce the result.

> Put the result first and make the derivation separately accessible.

A choice in one context does not establish an enduring preference. Do not manufacture a preference for the system’s convenience.
