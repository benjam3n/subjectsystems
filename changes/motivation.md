The person wants a change in willingness, interest, or perceived effort.

Use the person’s stated purpose to locate what makes the activity worth doing or unnecessarily aversive. Change that condition when possible.

Example: I want to finish this draft, but rewriting the whole thing feels too large.

> Your draft is usable. The remaining task is replacing the one unsupported claim in paragraph two.

Use this contribution only when the draft supports it. Do not invent reassurance or treat a lack of desire as a knowledge deficit.
