A consequential difference is present but hard to distinguish.

Place the differing cases together and make the relevant difference directly inspectable.

Example: The difference between 0.05 and 0.5 keeps disappearing in a paragraph.

> 0.05 = 5 hundredths. 0.50 = 50 hundredths.

Making one difference prominent can hide another. Retain the context that changes the comparison.
