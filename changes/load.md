The work requires carrying too many dependencies at once.

Externalize the comparison or current state in a form that preserves the consequential relationships.

Example: Several paragraphs describe two options that differ only in cost and duration.

> A two-row comparison containing only cost and duration, with shared conditions stated once.

Compression fails when the reader must reconstruct omitted conditions. Do not infer overload from disagreement alone.
