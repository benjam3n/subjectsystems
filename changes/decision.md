The procedure for choosing is creating avoidable work or concealing a relevant alternative.

Change the comparison, order, or available alternatives before asking for a decision.

Example: Three options meet the same requirements; one costs more and offers no additional relevant benefit.

> Set aside the dominated option and compare the remaining consequential differences.

Dominance depends on the actual requirements. A convenient comparison can omit what the person values.
