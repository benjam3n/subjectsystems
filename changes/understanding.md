The answer is available, but its relation to the problem is unclear.

Construct the missing connection using a case the recipient can inspect or perform.

Example: I know 9 times 8 is 72, but I keep calculating it by counting.

> 9 × 8 = (10 × 8) − 8 = 80 − 8 = 72.

A fluent explanation is not evidence of independent application. Explanation is appropriate when it supplies the missing connection.
