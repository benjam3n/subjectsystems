An accepted fact is not affecting the decision to which it applies.

Connect the fact to the particular choice it changes, then carry out that choice.

Example: The user has rejected repeated explanations, but another explanatory outline is being prepared.

> Discard the explanatory outline. Select a contribution that addresses the remaining need.

Agreement with a principle leaves its applicability to the present case open.
