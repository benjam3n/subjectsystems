## Problem remedy adequacy

Making old files retrievable remedies missing access. It need not remedy failure to use an available correction. The adequacy of that specified effect differs from the causal question of whether an intervention produces it.

## Subject scope gap

An interface requirement can concern one compatibility relation between retained identities and required interaction behavior. Its result is not supplied by separate descriptions of identity and interaction alone.
