# PreferenceFinder

Source standing: Selected source material; see version and limits.

## What the source contributes

Observations, hypotheses, questions, predicted ratings, actual feedback, and exceptions.

## Native distinctions and limits

Predicted reactions are hypotheses. Programmed confidence increments are not established calibration, and agreement with a preference does not establish truth or benefit.
