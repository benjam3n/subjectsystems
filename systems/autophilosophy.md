# Autophilosophy

Source standing: Selected source material; see version and limits.

## What the source contributes

The actual concept investigations, proposed procedures, and worked answer–attack–revision artifacts.

## Native distinctions and limits

The repository contains investigations, procedures, and answer–attack–revision artifacts. Their existence does not establish a completed theory or successful automation of all philosophy.
