Subject: [Goals](../subjects/goal.md).
Source: [SDS](../sources/systems/sds.md).

## Purpose

Develop a goal and its conditions of achievement when what should be pursued is still partly unknown.

## Modification

Generate candidate aims and experienced futures instead of candidate artifacts. Use selections to distinguish an end from a favored means. Produce a justified goal formulation and revisable criteria; do not treat the selection itself as achievement.

## Object

Candidate aims; concrete situations in which an aim would be met; stated and inferred reasons for favoring; constraints; candidate achievement conditions; unresolved tradeoffs.

## Operation

1. Construct materially different candidate aims from the live situation, including different purposes and a candidate that revises the initial framing. Preserve already explicit commitments rather than pretending they are unknown.
2. For each candidate, construct a concrete case in which it would be met and a case that appears to meet it while missing the intended purpose.
3. Use existing explicit selections or obtain the particular missing comparison when needed. Separate the selected aim, the attractive consequence, and the means through which it was presented.
4. Change a means while preserving the proposed end, then change the end while preserving the means. Use the contrast to develop competing accounts of what mattered.
5. Derive candidate achievement conditions from the supported account. Keep desired properties of an artifact separate from the purpose the artifact serves.
6. If no candidate expresses the purpose, regenerate the aims or change the representation. If the conflict concerns what is worth pursuing, retain that conflict instead of forcing a score.
7. Return the current goal, grounds for it, conditions of achievement, alternatives still live, and the next decision those conditions affect.

## Result

A goal formulation with the reasons and criteria that bear on it, plus unresolved alternatives. A plan for reaching it is a separate possible continuation.

## Failure condition

A named property must trace to the purpose or explicit direction. A candidate that passes the proposed criteria while missing the intended purpose defeats the adequacy of those criteria.

## Continuation

Use the goal in the next relevant design or decision. Reopen it when new selections change its purpose or criteria; do not require perfect preference knowledge before a justified reversible commitment.

## Case

In the naming episode, a modest name is a requirement for the name. It does not establish that the project should have modest intellectual ambitions. The local result keeps those propositions separate.
