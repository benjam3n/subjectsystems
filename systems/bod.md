# BOD

Source standing: Selected source material; see version and limits.

## What the source contributes

Context, components, capability distinctions, competing causal accounts, and realization conditions.

## Native distinctions and limits

Capability, observation, cause, constraint, target, intervention, and value judgment must retain their native distinctions. Candidate mechanisms are not established causal explanations.
