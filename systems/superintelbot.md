# Superintelbot

Source standing: Selected source material; see version and limits.

## What the source contributes

The conversational system, mixed interaction dimensions, consultation practices, and response repertoires.

## Native distinctions and limits

The source concerns choosing a contribution in mixed conversational situations and consulting actual methods. It does not justify a mandatory production workflow for every exchange.
