## Purpose

Develop possibilities that differ in what they make available, especially when early convergence would eliminate useful directions.

## Modification

Selection retains promising differences and generative principles. It need not choose a single favorite. Iteration can deliberately increase diversity instead of converging on one design.

## Object

Candidate possibilities; assumptions producing them; consequential differences; retained directions; varied constraints; undeveloped implications.

## Operation

1. Generate candidates under different assumptions, forms, or constraints relevant to the live work. Identify what each candidate makes possible.
2. Group candidates only where their differences do not change the intended inquiry or use. Keep a surprising difference visible even when no final application is known.
3. Select several directions for the distinct possibilities they expose. Preserve the reason for retaining each rather than assigning a universal novelty score.
4. For each retained direction, vary the assumption that produced it and develop its consequences. Cross selected features when their combination could create a new possibility.
5. Compare the resulting possibilities with their parents. Reject a claim of novelty when only the words changed; preserve useful recombination without falsely claiming independent invention.
6. Return the developed alternatives, the distinctions among them, and the direction each makes worth pursuing next.

## Result

A set of developed possibilities with consequential differences and continuations. The result may deliberately remain plural.

## Failure condition

A larger list with unchanged underlying options is not a demonstrated enlargement of the possibility space. Historical originality requires separate source work.

## Continuation

Hand a direction to Design, Research, or another appropriate local system when its use becomes definite. Continue divergence when the available candidates remain versions of the same restricted approach.

## Case

Keeping an artifact-building route, an inquiry route, and a representation-changing route can be a successful selection. Collapsing them into a single compromise would defeat this local purpose.
