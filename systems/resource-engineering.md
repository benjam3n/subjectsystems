# Resource-engineering

Source standing: Selected source material; see version and limits.

## What the source contributes

Ontology, configurations, operations, maps, contrasts, and verdict history. Rejected conclusions retain their historical status.

## Native distinctions and limits

Preserve the distinction between ontology, configuration, operation, map, verdict, and representation lineage. Rejected or reconstructed verdicts must retain their status.
