Subject: [Discovery](../subjects/discovery.md).
Source: [GOSM](../sources/systems/gosm.md), [Discovery Engine](../sources/systems/discovery-engine.md).

## Purpose

Determine what an investigation has established, what remains open, and what its actual result makes worth doing next.

## Modification

Adapt GOSM outcome review to inquiry whose question and criteria can change. Use Discovery Engine scope and contribution distinctions. Replace blanket completion percentages and guessed causal-attribution percentages with the exact evidence and dependencies of a finding.

## Object

Initial and revised discovery questions; scope; claims and findings; evidence; unresolved alternatives; contributions to other inquiries; next opportunities.

## Operation

1. Recover the initial inquiry and its current formulation. Record which goal, question, scope, or criterion changed and the result that warranted the change.
2. Separate artifacts produced from findings established. For each proposed finding, attach the supporting observation, derivation, or source at the required scope.
3. Compare the finding with the actual unresolved question. Record whether it resolves that scope, changes the question, exposes another alternative, or leaves the inquiry open.
4. Identify the next inquiry or method that can use the finding and preserve the conditions it needs. A merely mentioned relation is not a required dependency.
5. If work stopped for lack of access or resources, retain the needed condition without marking the question answered. If a criterion changed, reopen dependent closure claims.
6. Continue from the newly available work, including a new purpose made possible by success. Do not require the entire subject to be finished before using a scoped finding.

## Result

A scoped progress account with actual grounds, changed dependencies, and a warranted continuation.

## Failure condition

A completed report cannot close an unanswered discovery. A local finding does not close every parent inquiry. Attribution to the method needs evidence beyond a guessed share of the outcome.

## Continuation

Continue, pause with a definite dependency, revise the inquiry, or use the finding elsewhere according to its actual state.

## Case

Publishing the initial subject hierarchy resolves the setup task. It does not establish sciences of the subjects. The new customization decision changes the next development work while leaving the preserved sources useful.
