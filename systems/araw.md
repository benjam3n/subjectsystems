# ARAW

Source standing: Selected source material; see version and limits.

## What the source contributes

Exact claims, conditional contexts, constructive consequences, wrongness cases, alternatives, recursion, and unresolved tensions.

## Native distinctions and limits

Assume-right includes constructive consequences. Assume-wrong concerns cases in which the claim is false, with alternatives and consequences. Do not reduce it to a critique checklist or replace the source procedure with this summary.

## Current study contributions

These are specified investigative targets or proposed study contributions. A source procedure, stored artifact, study specification and conducted study retain different standing. The [study/system derivation](../research/studies/SUBJECT-STUDY-SYSTEM.md) and [technique contracts](../research/studies/TECHNIQUES.md) determine how the material can enter a custom composition.

| Literal subject | Particular matter | Required result | Technique standing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Claim content specification | What exactly is the proposition under examination, separated from neighboring assertions? | A fixed proposition whose consequences and failure cases concern the same content. | No complete technique admitted for this study target |
| Claim content quantifier scope | Which cases and quantifier placements does this proposition cover? | A declared domain and quantifier interpretation that do not change between branches. | No complete technique admitted for this study target |
| Claim inference specification | Which premises are being used to derive which conclusion? | An explicit premise-conclusion relation, including added assumptions. | No complete technique admitted for this study target |
| Reasoning assumption consequence derivation | What further propositions follow when the exact assumption is accepted? | Conditional consequences with the parent assumption and inference grounds retained. | No complete technique admitted for this study target |
| Claim inference counterexample | Which admitted case has true premises and a false conclusion? | A counterexample to the exact proposed inference, or an explicitly limited unsuccessful search. | M02 |
| Reasoning assumption alternative construction | Which replacement assumption follows from the identified way the original fails? | A concrete alternative with its wrongness parent, consequences and further countercases. | No complete technique admitted for this study target |
| Claim support sufficiency | What do the available grounds justify accepting under the specified rule? | A scoped support assessment; a favorable branch alone does not establish truth. | No complete technique admitted for this study target |
| Claim support limits | Which conclusions remain unwarranted after the exploration performed? | Explicit limits on domain, evidential support and unexamined alternatives. | No complete technique admitted for this study target |
| Reasoning approach termination condition | What exactly permits a branch to stop with a justified status? | A test, derivation, observation or unresolved dependency with distinct stopping force. | No complete technique admitted for this study target |
| Inquiry test design | Which observable outcome would distinguish the live alternative accounts? | A test with discriminating possible results, subject to access and method conditions. | No complete technique admitted for this study target |
| Claim acceptance revision | Which earlier verdict must change when a consequential premise changes? | A revised status whose dependency on the new finding is inspectable. | No complete technique admitted for this study target |
| Claim inference validity | Do the recorded premise-to-finding transitions actually license their conclusions? | An inference assessment that can revise the registry and its later use. | M02 |
