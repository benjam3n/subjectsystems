# PostAGI reasoning

Source standing: Selected source material; see version and limits.

## What the source contributes

Its distinct representation families and corpora. Concept hubs, dependency maps, conversational moves, and continuous regress remain distinct.

## Native distinctions and limits

Concept hubs, depth maps, dependency forms, continuous regress, and conversational moves remain separate representation families. Corpus size and generated text do not establish insight or novelty.
