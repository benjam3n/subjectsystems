## Purpose

Choose an identifiable approach that can supply the missing contribution in a live reasoning task. This version is designed for a naming or scope problem in which superficially adequate wording can hide different completion conditions. Its result is a chosen approach with the relevant grounds and limits. Executing that approach supplies separate evidence about its contribution.

## Source

[Reasoningtool2](../sources/systems/reasoningtool2.md), specifically its inspected [current contract](../sources/snapshots/reasoningtool2-CURRENT.txt) and [conditional methods](../sources/snapshots/reasoningtool2-METHODS.txt), pinned to `ae2435b0004ac44de9bb09c2f045805722038dbb`.

Retained contributions are choosing operations for the actual task, comparing consequential alternatives, distinguishing availability from use, and propagating a correction into dependent work. This descendant adds task-specific completion comparisons, stable subject identity, alias treatment, scope-change records and canonical-reference repair. It does not impose a fixed global dispatcher or claim to execute an original named skill.

## Condition

The live objects are the matter to settle, the current definitions, the contribution still needed, the approaches actually available, and the task's consequential constraints. A candidate need not be retrieved before the task begins: creation can supply one during the surrounding work. Successful selection requires an available identifiable candidate at choice time.

For this version, selection is complete when an approach is chosen and its contribution is intelligible against the stated requirements and consequential alternatives. Exhaustive enumeration, numerical scoring and proof of optimality are not universal conditions. If no candidate can supply the required contribution, approach creation or a changed task specification is separate work; calling failure a selection would conceal the missing result.

## Operation

- Identify the difference the task must settle, using the live definitions and concrete cases.
- Derive competing completion conditions when apparent synonyms conceal different matters.
- Examine a candidate's actual output or a separating case that reveals why it cannot supply the needed contribution.
- Select the available approach supported by those comparisons, retaining unexamined alternatives as unexamined.
- Execute the selected approach when execution is part of the authorized task; inspect the contribution it actually produces.
- Propagate the result into current definitions and uses, preserving aliases, changed scope and source identity.
