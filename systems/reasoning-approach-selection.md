Selection of an available reasoning approach through the contribution needed in a naming or scope problem.

1. Identify the distinction the task must settle using the current definitions and a concrete case.
2. Compare the completion conditions of candidate approaches. Where apparent synonyms conceal different matters, construct a case that separates them.
3. Inspect an actual candidate output or a separating case. Retain the approaches that can supply the required contribution and identify any missing operation.
4. Choose an available approach supported by the comparison. If none supplies the contribution, construct an approach or revise the task formulation before selecting.
5. Execute the selected approach when required by the task and inspect its actual contribution.
6. Revise dependent definitions and uses while retaining stable identity, aliases, and any change of scope.
