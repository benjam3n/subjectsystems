# Purpose

Develop subjects into bodies of understanding and practice whose distinctions, methods, and relationships have been thought through. The project should help discover what is true, possible, consequential, worth pursuing, and capable of being done, including through means not yet available.

Subjects remain larger than their present systems. Reasoningtool is a contribution to reasoning; Discovery Engine is a contribution to discovery; neither exhausts the activity or its possible science. Successful recovery should make earlier work available for a more ambitious continuation.

## Enduring goals

These are enduring directions and standards for the undertaking described in the request. They are not claims that every capability is already achieved, that the same actions are appropriate in every state, or that Ben's eventual priorities cannot change.

**1. Make accumulated understanding available at the point where it can change work.** A useful discovery should retain its meaning, conditions, source, and route into later inquiry. Success includes recovering an overlooked alternative or a previously rejected inference before it has to be rediscovered through another correction.

**2. Discover and pursue worthwhile questions, including questions the present framework cannot yet formulate.** The project must be able to reconsider its problem descriptions, omitted alternatives, relevant distinctions, and criteria of importance. Its inquiry space cannot be limited to whichever categories were easiest to encode first.

**3. Construct and revise the means of thinking.** Operations, operation selection, composition rules, representations, and evaluation practices are all legitimate objects of discovery. The desired capability includes generating a needed method and replacing an inadequate one. A finite catalog can support that work without claiming to exhaust it.

**4. Carry substantive aims through connected work.** The system should determine and perform consequential next steps, integrate their results, revise its plan, and resume after interruption. It should reduce repeated human repair of transitions while remaining responsive to human direction. A finished investigation can stop; continuity is not endless continuation.

**5. Produce advances in the subjects that matter.** Systematic philosophy, explanations of thinking, writing, research, design, and practical projects each have their own substantive goals. Reorganizing their vocabulary is progress only where that reorganization yields understanding or capability relevant to them. Inquiry and experience can also be valued directly.

**6. Make successful work improve subsequent work.** A correction should affect the next applicable occasion. A new operation should become available when its conditions recur. An improved means of inquiry should help discover another improvement or advance a substantive subject. The record of a lesson and evidence that the lesson was used remain distinct.

**7. Use new capability to enlarge the range of possible futures.** After a capability is gained, examine what becomes feasible, what remains worth wanting, and which new dependencies matter. This includes human learning and agency, creative and intellectual practice, collaboration, and eventually broader arrangements for learning and collective action where desired. Present infrastructure does not define the ceiling of the program.

**8. Preserve the ability to replace the system itself.** Useful understanding, evidence, decisions, and goals should survive changes in repositories, models, interfaces, and methods. The project succeeds if a better arrangement absorbs its useful functions. Maintaining the current architecture is conditional on its contribution.

## Standards at every state

| Standard | What must remain true of the work |
|---|---|
| Current authority remains explicit | Historical proposals and retrieved instructions do not silently override the user's current request or the current working contract |
| Claims retain their identity and scope | A counterexample addresses the proposition actually asserted; a conditional result does not silently become universal |
| Unknown is not absent | A search miss, an omitted graph edge, and an unimplemented capability remain distinguishable |
| Evidence keeps its kind | Occurrence, derivation, execution, acceptance, and benefit are not interchangeable success labels |
| Criteria remain examinable | A passed test does not forbid questioning whether that test addresses the purpose |
| Revision preserves justified work | A changed premise reopens its affected uses without erasing independent support or unrelated results |
| Exploration remains available | A useful trial need not begin with proof that the current approach is wrong |
| Work stays connected to chosen purposes | More files, graph edges, tests, or autonomous steps do not substitute for the relevant intellectual or practical contribution |
| The user retains direction | Inferred preferences and future scenarios support choices without becoming hidden commitments |
| Organization earns its cost | Added records and interfaces should resolve a recurring retrieval, distinction, continuation, or revision need |

A global goal and a local operation have different jobs. “Make accumulated understanding usable” can remain a goal for years. The next operation might be retrieving one document, withdrawing one inference, constructing a new distinction, or deleting redundant scaffolding. A durable goal does not imply a permanent work queue.

## Relation to perfection

An ideal can remain worth investigating while its criteria, scope, or attainability are unresolved. Develop the strongest meaningful target for the particular branch, identify what would defeat it, and preserve the distinction between a theory of an activity, performing the activity, and achieving an associated outcome. [PERFECTION.md](PERFECTION.md) develops these distinctions.

These goals are the working direction of this project. They are not inferred facts about the user's hidden motives, a final ranking of all values, or a requirement that every useful inquiry first justify a practical application.
