## Work to do next

1. Exercise a specified local system on actual work and retain the result appropriate to its purpose. For the SDS family, compare the different objects and continuations produced by Preferences, Goals, and Design.
2. Develop the next versions from the actual work of a subject: for example a goal-journey system for forming goals, an ARAW-derived system for discovering alternatives, or a recovery system for preserving the conditions of a finding. Retrieve their exact source mechanisms and redesign what the purpose changes.
3. Develop connections between local systems through the actual result passed. A contextual preference hypothesis, a proposed criterion, and an observed outcome cannot be interchanged merely because they concern one example.
4. Expand subject coverage, including combinations absent from the initial relevance map. An unresolved source or purpose is a design question to resolve, not evidence that the combination is useless.
5. Revisit the design after a mismatch or new purpose. Preserve shared implementations where appropriate, but keep each local specification authoritative for its own task.

The [worked distinction](../cases/custom-system-outputs.md) demonstrates why three source-related systems can require different results from the same episode.
