# Open questions

These are live research and source questions. The list is open; the next important question may require a new distinction or a different frame.

## Source and organization

1. Which exact artifact defines the Master Framework, and which version is intended?
2. What did Promoting mean in the original relevance request, and is it distinct from Prompting?
3. Do Connections and Improvement deserve roots, and what actual cases decide that question?
4. Which evaluation questions are clarified by distributing them, and which become harder to find?
5. Which source contributions remain invisible when the collection is approached through these subject entries?
6. What relation permits a single item to occupy several places without making those places redundant?
7. When should a branch become a peer subject rather than a narrower inquiry?
8. Which generated or reconstructed source claims have mistakenly acquired the authority of direct observations or user decisions?
9. Which artifact matches the user's remembered second method for planning years ahead?

## Operations and contribution

10. What determines which contribution a capable model actually makes when several useful operations are already available?
11. What changes when a model anticipates its response before producing it?
12. How much of that change comes from forecasting, intended effect, context reconstruction, or selection among alternatives?
13. Why can an anticipated contribution be more useful than the response that follows it?
14. Which unspecified executor choices make an otherwise explicit operation fail?
15. When does adding a compulsory transformation improve work, and when does it obstruct available judgment?
16. Which combinations of operations produce capabilities absent from the components used independently?
17. How can a system discover an operation that its current representation cannot express?
18. What distinguishes a new uncertainty-producing question from a renamed known answer?
19. Which kinds of departure can be selected before their value is known?
20. Can a useful discovery remain outside every current goal without being irrelevant?

## Subjects, standards, and futures

21. What would perfection of the science of a subject mean when the subject's own concepts and standards can change?
22. Which forms of understanding are lost when a subject is developed exclusively through one successful frame?
23. When does improving representation reveal a fact, and when does it change the question being asked?
24. What evidence distinguishes a preference discovered through a selection from a preference induced by the available candidates?
25. How can a standard revealed by one example be justified for another?
26. When should a result revise the goal rather than only the plan or method?
27. Which retained corrections actually alter later work, and what prevents the others from doing so?
28. Which dependencies of a conclusion survive a change of representation or language?
29. What kinds of cross-subject transfer can preserve native distinctions without a universal intermediate schema?
30. What should follow after a subject's present methods become sufficiently capable to remove the original obstacle?
31. Which larger philosophical, scientific, design, or practical aims become feasible from those successes?
32. What would a new interface need to do that the present files and model cannot already do adequately?

See the corresponding [subject frontiers](../subjects/README.md) for narrower questions and [NEXT.md](../NEXT.md) for prepared work. A question here is not evidence that its answer is absent from every source.
