# Findings from the perspective process development

These are explicit deductions from constructed cases and the proposed definitions. They do not establish a general empirical theory of cognition.

**1. Relevance cannot require uncertainty between two substantive answers.** A study may be relevant because its result supplies a needed justification, even when the expected answer does not vary. The more general comparison is whether the result is available in a usable form. Where several substantive outcomes are possible, their different consequences supply further relevance distinctions.

For example, a known algorithm can be applicable only when a prerequisite has been established. Establishing that prerequisite may enable use without changing the predicted answer. A relevance procedure that only asks which answer would change the chosen action can miss this contribution.

The revised operation therefore compares continuation with and without the result, then distinguishes alternative result contents when the subject admits them. This separates subject relevance from mere topical association without requiring every relevant study to produce a surprising answer.

**2. The result's role belongs in the configuration.** The same proposition can be a temporary premise, a supported finding, a required input, a possible countercase or the target of investigation. Its literal content does not determine its use. Passing a trial assumption to a later step as established can alter the system even when no text changes.

This gives Perspective study configuration construction a precise task: specify the result, its standing, its consumer and the change it permits. Perspective result interpretation rule determines the update; System interface compatibility determines whether that update supplies the consumer's actual requirement. The entire episode can contain all three determinations.

**3. Reachability makes an apparently weaker starting perspective useful.** Consider a supplied graph where perspective A enables a cue comparison, whose result makes a missing-application-condition question recognizable. That question enables construction of a relevant contrast, which can reveal perspective B. A cannot itself supply B's answer, but choosing A can make B accessible.

Therefore immediate answer quality alone does not determine the contribution of a starting perspective. Its available continuations matter. This does not imply that the longest path is best, that every incomplete perspective is productive, or that graph reachability establishes actual execution. The application retains each path's unresolved observation conditions.

**4. Retaining a result and retaining a transition are different achievements.** A stored explanation can preserve the conclusion while losing the cue that made it relevant. A retained transition additionally preserves the starting conditions, the operation that changed the interpretation and the result-dependent continuation.

For the transfer case, the useful retained content includes what minimal-cue success changes, why a case contrast becomes relevant, and what failure would reopen. Evidence of retention requires a later relevant occasion; a file or an immediate repeat does not supply it.

**5. Complete enumeration can coexist with an actual perspective gap.** In the first application construction, three interpretations combine with two representations and two commitments. All twelve assignments are enumerated. Yet the set has no interpretation specifically targeting application-condition recognition. The separating case is cued success followed by failure to recognize changed wording.

Adding the fourth interpretation produces sixteen assignments and supplies a new proposed contribution: contrast applicable and inapplicable cases while varying incidental wording. The first enumeration was complete relative to its dimensions; it was incomplete relative to the newly identified matter. Those claims do not conflict.

**6. A perspective can be excluded from one role while retained in another.** An unestablished causal account can be unsuitable as a settled explanation while useful as a trial assumption directing a discriminating observation. Excluding it from all exploration because it cannot yet be relied on would remove that contribution.

The construction examples therefore retain excluded assignments and state what their exclusion concerns. A construction incompatibility, an evidential limitation, a resource limit and final selection are different grounds. Unknown conditions do not silently become exclusions.

**7. The nonconscious extension changes which fields can be universal.** A ligand interaction can be described through its conditions and response distinctions without giving it a stated goal, concern, chosen method or internal error report. Requiring those fields would decide against the user's broader hypothesis before examining the case.

The general description therefore supplies bearer, matter, conditions and response relations. Interpretive capacities are added where warranted. The application can examine a hormone's disposition while locating the larger response in an interaction. Different bearer attributions remain investigable.

**8. A problem detector is itself perspective-dependent.** If only represented discrepancies count as problems, an omitted distinction can remain invisible to the existing detector. A temporary alternative, a new case or a changed environment can expose the omission before the current interpretation establishes that it needs revision.

This supports preserving entry routes beyond diagnosis: activation, exploration, deliberate variation and encounter. The attachment's problem-detection/revision loop remains a major form of regulation, with its universal necessity still open.
