# Constructing an expanding space of perspectives

The target is an expanding account of possible perspectives, their distinctions and the operations that make new ones available. No current list is certified as every possible perspective. Finite enumeration is one contribution within this larger program.

**Four different completeness questions.**

| Target | What completeness would establish | What it leaves open |
|---|---|---|
| Recorded inventory | Every item in a named source snapshot is represented | Items absent from that source |
| Finite construction space | Every assignment satisfying declared finite rules has been examined | Missing dimensions, values or rules |
| Declared case coverage | Every case in a fixed domain receives the required distinction or contribution | Cases and adequacy requirements outside that domain |
| General perspective space | Every admissible perspective is captured under a justified identity relation | No such complete basis is established here |

The 449-subject snapshot and sixty perspective patterns from the accepted account remain valuable under the first meaning. The current atlas extends the subject inventory. Neither step establishes the fourth meaning.

**Construction operations with consequential content.**

| Operation | Input | Transformation | Result | Continuation condition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Target substitution | A matter and its current interpretation | Hold the surrounding situation fixed; replace what is under examination | A different determination with a distinct required contribution | Find what result would differ between the targets |
| Bearer substitution | A response attributed to one entity | Assign the candidate configuration to a component, interaction or larger system; specify the observable each attribution explains | Competing bearer attributions | Identify a change that separates their predictions or explanatory coverage |
| Distinction construction | Two cases currently treated as equivalent | Identify a difference that changes an intended consequence; state its possible values and conditions | A new candidate dimension with a separating case | Check whether the difference was already encoded elsewhere |
| Role substitution | A subject with one current use | Change what the result is used for while retaining its content | A different relevance relationship | Trace the changed next operation |
| Scope variation | A fixed interpretation and declared domain | Change a boundary, time span or unit; preserve which assumptions still hold | A qualified extension or limitation | Identify a case the changed scope newly admits or excludes |
| Temporary adoption | A candidate with a specified contrast | Use it provisionally to construct the next question or operation | A concrete consequence of the candidate | Separate usefulness of the trial from truth of its premise |
| Assumption substitution | A current assumption and a specified alternative | Retain the matter; derive the consequential differences under the alternative | A branching interpretation | Seek a distinguishing observation or argument |
| Perspective composition | Contributors with defined results | Specify which result supplies which later input and under what conditions | An arrangement with inspectable dependencies | Check incompatibility and order dependence |
| Condition creation | A desirable perspective inaccessible under current conditions | Construct an encounter, record, resource or operation that supplies a missing condition | A route to making the perspective usable | Attempt the condition-producing operation or establish why it is unavailable |
| Exclusion reconsideration | A candidate excluded for a recorded reason | Recheck the reason against the actual intended contribution | Retention, reinstatement or a narrower justified exclusion | Preserve both the original exclusion and its changed scope |

These operations may be used independently. There is no compulsory universal sequence. Their names alone do not establish a general executable technique; each use fixes its target, operation, conditions and result.

**A developed expansion beyond the earlier account.** Consider two readers who currently choose the same method for the same case. One does so because the principle is recognizable; the other does so only because a familiar wording retrieves the method. The current answer is identical, but a wording change may separate their later performance.

The additional dimension is the **activation condition**. It is not just another answer, method or goal. A perspective description limited to current verdicts hides the difference. The separating case supports investigating the dimension; it does not diagnose any real reader without observations.

A second expansion concerns the **bearer**. A hormonal input, receptor response and whole-cell adaptation can belong to one causal episode while requiring different perspective attributions. The question becomes which configuration supplies the consequential discrimination. The biological source cases in [the foundation](FOUNDATION.md) ground the distinctions; the perspective attribution remains an authored theoretical proposal.

A third expansion concerns the **change locus**. Changing an input, changing an internal configuration and changing access through an environment can all alter an outcome. Keeping those changes separate prevents the generator from equating every different outcome with a new response rule.

**The applied finite construction.** Perspective Optimizer contains a declared learning-difficulty space with three interpretations, two representations and two continuation commitments. Enumeration constructs all twelve assignments. Stated incompatibility rules identify combinations requiring a representation or commitment change; unexcluded combinations remain candidates for semantic examination. Enumeration does not diagnose a learner or select a universally best perspective.

The application then adds a fourth interpretation, missing application-condition recognition, with its own distinguishing operation and subject contributions. The space grows to sixteen assignments. The added interpretation is tied to a concrete case: correct execution after a cue, followed by failure to recognize an altered case. The operation and the domain expansion are separately recorded.

The implemented expansion operation requires a new value identity, an explicit before/after separating case and a specified contribution. It does not treat unfamiliarity as a reason for exclusion. It checks declared structure; whether a proposed difference is semantically new remains a matter for study.

**What should drive the next expansion?**

- A case requires a distinction absent from every current candidate.
- Two candidates appear equivalent until a consequential context is varied.
- A useful interpretation cannot be expressed without changing the declared fields.
- Every available continuation depends on an unavailable perspective or operation.
- An exclusion reason defeats the intended purpose or discards a useful conditional contribution.
- A nonconscious example exposes a field that was wrongly required of all bearers.

Record the discovered gap, the new construction, the changed case coverage and the remaining limit. New words alone do not establish expansion. A finite generator supplies inspectable combinations; model-led investigation, observation and newly constructed operations supply material that its old dimensions could not have generated.

**Questions whose answers would change the construction process.**

1. Can every consequential perspective distinction be expressed through a finite contrast case, or do some require an open temporal pattern?
2. Which changes of bearer preserve a response explanation, and which require a different mechanism?
3. When do two dimensions encode the same determination under different names?
4. Can a perspective make an otherwise inaccessible distinction available without changing its explicit content?
5. How should the space preserve conflicting criteria without silently combining them into one ordering?
6. Which missing perspective can only be discovered after the conditions for using it have been created?
7. When does exclusion help a particular operation while impairing the wider inquiry?
8. What identifies a productive temporary perspective when its final adequacy is still unlikely?

The current finite cases are constructed demonstrations. Their counts establish the scope of enumeration, not completeness of the perspective space or improved LLM intelligence.
