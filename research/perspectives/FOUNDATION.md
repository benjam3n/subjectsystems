# Perspectives without a consciousness requirement

Current theoretical extension, 7 September 2026. The user explicitly includes hormones, genes and cells among possible perspective bearers. This extension develops that claim without assigning consciousness, intention or a human-style representation to those entities. The earlier interpretive account remains a useful specialization.

**Proposed general meaning:** a perspective is a configuration through which specified differences become consequential for an entity or interaction.

The matter, bearer, conditions, distinctions and consequences must be identifiable. A consequence can be a physical response, an available discrimination, an inference, a valuation or an action. The relevant configuration can be stable or changing, intrinsic to one component or dependent on an interaction. Deliberate selection, articulated goals, conceptual interpretation and consciousness are possible additional properties. They are not entry requirements.

This is a proposed unifying description, not an established biological identity. Its scientific contribution depends on preserving mechanisms and enabling distinctions or inferences that would otherwise remain unavailable. Simply redescribing every cause as a perspective does not establish that contribution.

**Three readings need separate scopes.**

| Reading | What constitutes a perspective | What is additionally required |
|---|---|---|
| Dispositional | Specified differences have different consequences through a physical configuration | A declared interaction and an observable response distinction |
| Regulatory | A configuration relates changing conditions to responses that maintain or alter a specified condition | An actual response mechanism; any maintained range must be identified |
| Interpretive | A matter is organized through distinctions, assumptions, concerns and possible responses | The relevant representation or interpretation and evidence of its use |

These are compatible readings with additional conditions, not three exclusive kinds of whole entities. A cell may support dispositional and regulatory accounts; an LLM may be studied through physical, computational and interpretive accounts. Scope inclusion is a question about specified matters, not about assigning each entity one permanent folder.

**Having a perspective and regulating a perspective are different capabilities.** A molecule need not examine its configuration, compare alternatives or detect a problem. A cell's adaptive response need not involve an articulated judgment. An LLM's ability to discuss a perspective does not establish that the described perspective is operative in its next response. Consciousness is not being smuggled back in through words such as selection or interpretation.

The description should therefore distinguish the following:

- The **bearer** or interaction whose configuration is under examination.
- The **matter** relative to which differences are being considered.
- The **conditions** that fix the comparison, including the time scale and observable.
- The **response relation** that states what changes when a relevant condition changes.
- The **change relation**, if any, by which the configuration itself changes.
- The **evidence** supporting the response or change relation.
- The **additional capacities** actually attributed: retention, adaptation, representation, deliberate selection, self-description or consciousness. Unestablished properties remain unestablished.

An optimizer additionally needs an intended contribution and a justified comparison. The hormone or gene under study need not possess that objective. A researcher's objective, an organism-level consequence and a component's physical disposition are different determinations.

**Biological cases make the bearer question consequential.**

| Case | Perspective reading to investigate | Consequential distinction | What does not follow |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hormone | The hormone's interaction disposition, or the receiving system's response configuration | The molecule's structure differs from its concentration history and from the receptor system responding to it | A hormone has beliefs, evaluates problems or chooses its target |
| Gene or regulatory sequence | A sequence's contribution within a specified regulatory context | Sequence, regulatory interaction and resulting expression are distinct targets | An isolated gene contains a context-independent intention or a complete cellular viewpoint |
| Cell | The cell's sensitivity, response and adaptation to specified conditions | Which external differences produce which response, and how response conditions change | Every cell implements the same learning or problem-recognition mechanism |
| LLM | The context and computational operations making distinctions and continuations available | A described perspective differs from one that changes the next consequential operation | A written self-description is complete access to the generating mechanism |
| Person | Operative distinctions, concerns, learned tendencies and available responses | A perspective can be active without being consciously selected or verbally expressible | Every act begins with explicit diagnosis or deliberation |

In experiments on bacterial chemotaxis, changing network-component concentrations altered some response properties while adaptation precision was comparatively robust. This supplies a concrete distinction between a response property and the mechanisms maintaining it. Calling the configuration a perspective is our proposed interpretation. [Alon and colleagues, 1999](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9923680/).

Pulsed glucocorticoid stimulation produced cyclic receptor-mediated transcriptional activity, with different transcriptional programs under pulsed and constant stimulation. The relevant account therefore includes the temporal stimulus and receiving machinery. This does not locate the entire account inside an isolated hormone molecule. [Stavreva and colleagues, 2009](https://research.birmingham.ac.uk/en/publications/ultradian-hormone-stimulation-induces-glucocorticoid-receptor-med/).

Glucocorticoid-receptor binding sequences differing by a single base pair affected receptor conformation and regulatory activity. A specified sequence–receptor interaction therefore supplies a more precise starting matter than attributing one general perspective to all genes. [Meijsing and colleagues, 2009](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19372434/).

**A response is not automatically a change of perspective.** Fix a bearer, input domain and consequence measure. Its response relation might be written as R(x, c), where x is the presented condition and c fixes relevant context. Different inputs can produce different responses under the same relation. A change in that relation, its accessible inputs, its context or its current state must be identified separately.

For example, a thermostat can switch a heater on under one temperature and off under another while retaining the same threshold rule. If the current state is included in a broad perspective description, the perspective state changes. The rule need not change. This is a constructed separating case, not a claim that thermostats exhaust the relevant mechanism.

This distinction preserves two possible meanings in the user's action claim: action can change the agent's current perspective state or conditions of access; it need not revise the rule or interpretation governing that action. Expanding the meaning to encompass both is possible, provided the distinction remains available.

**The proposed perspective-problem loop.** The attachment suggests that an entity already has a perspective and repeatedly detects a problem in it and modifies it. For an adaptive interpretive system, the account can be developed through these distinct matters:

1. Which current configuration determines what becomes consequential?
2. What departure, incompatibility, uncertainty or opportunity is registered?
3. Relative to which condition is that departure a problem?
4. Which part can change: representation, concern, response rule, accessible information, resource arrangement or external situation?
5. Which available or newly constructed operation produces that change?
6. What consequence would justify retaining, reversing, extending or abandoning it?

This loop is one important form of perspective regulation. Its necessity for every change is still an open claim. Exposure can activate an interpretation before a defect is identified. Exploration can reveal a previously unformulated concern. A maintained perspective may already be adequate. External changes can alter access without an endogenous problem detector.

There is also a deeper problem: a configuration helps determine which failures are visible. A system that only repairs internally registered errors may preserve its own omissions. The construction program therefore includes trial perspectives and external encounters that need not first prove the current one wrong.

**Writing can operate on perspective conditions.** An explanation can introduce a distinction, a question can reopen a presupposition, a list can keep alternatives available, an example can make an application condition recognizable, a narrative can organize anticipation, and a diagram can expose dependencies. These are candidate functional analyses of concrete writing operations. A written artifact may also preserve, coordinate, express or explore a perspective without identifying an error in another person's perspective.

The proposed writing study therefore asks what changes between the reader's available distinctions and continuations before and after a specified passage. It also permits a maintenance result: the intended change may be preserving a usable perspective across time or an interruption. Literary or aesthetic worth is not thereby reduced to correcting an error.

**Perception can change what is available.** Perception may supply a new difference while the current interpretation stays stable, or may reorganize what a situation is taken to be. Those are separate cases of the larger account. Treating every perceptual change as revision of an explanatory model would erase the first case.

**The consciousness claim remains a research hypothesis.** Removing a consciousness prerequisite lets the perspective account be tested across more systems. It does not by itself explain why some processes are accompanied by experience. Likewise, identifying problem recognition and perspective development as functions does not assign them to opposite hemispheres. A large resting-state study found local lateralization patterns without evidence of a general whole-brain left-dominant versus right-dominant phenotype. It did not test the exact problem-recognition/perspective-development split proposed here. [Nielsen and colleagues, 2013](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0071275).

**Several useful deductions follow within this account.**

1. A perspective need not be chosen, and the process that formed it need not exist within the current bearer.
2. The same component can contribute differently to several perspectives through different interactions.
3. An unchanged bearer can acquire different consequential access when its environment changes.
4. A stable perspective can generate many different actions; constant action does not prove a stable perspective either.
5. Adaptation, improvement and perspective change are different determinations. A change can be adaptive to one condition while impairing another contribution.
6. A perspective can be useful because it makes another perspective reachable, even before it supplies the final answer.
7. Falsehood applies directly to a truth-bearing claim. Other failures require their own descriptions: unsuitable response, missing distinction, misleading expression, failed regulation or criterion conflict.
8. Stated, enacted and attributed perspectives can disagree without establishing a hidden true self or a diagnosis. The particular discrepancy and its evidence are the subject.
9. A perspective inventory must preserve unfamiliar or conflicting candidates long enough to examine what they contribute. Rejection requires an identified reason and scope.
10. The means of forming perspectives are themselves revisable subjects. A fixed generator cannot establish that its current dimensions exhaust its own possible improvement.

The proposed general definition remains open to countercases. A satisfactory extension must show the particular differences, response relation and contribution it makes available.
