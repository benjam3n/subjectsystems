# Type relations, reversals, and exclusions

The user's subsequent contribution adds [subject completion and order](SUBJECT-COMPLETION.md): distinguish intelligence improvement from intelligence maintenance, art writing from writing art, and word-order changes from changes in the relation being expressed. The workbook now includes 211 specified expressions and one explicitly unresolved label, 14,840 expression-to-kind comparisons, 23 order/scope trials, and all 58 revisions of the specified Reasoningtool About page. The earlier single-word matrix remains an experiment under one primary sense per word; it does not settle every completed expression.

The key correction is that a refuted broad inclusion cannot automatically be inherited by a narrower class. Development need not be improvement, but development that improves intelligence belongs to both. The extension propagates universal inclusions and explicit disjointness, while leaving other qualification-specific claims open.

The user asked to reopen the direction of the type relationships, test their negatives, find possible common parents, and make the same comparisons for the projects. This is an exploratory comparison model. The previous scope hierarchy is material to examine, not a restriction on which interpretations can be tried.

## What was constructed

The workbook covers 70 concept names and 21 project families. It includes all 4,900 ordered concept comparisons, 1,470 project-to-concept kind comparisons, and 441 ordered project-to-project subtype questions. Self-comparisons are present and explicitly uninformative. These are enumerated assessments under visible assumptions, not thousands of independently observed facts.

The model records primary working senses, direct inclusion assumptions, nine explicit exclusions between fixed senses, 93 constructed cases, and 45 deeper reversal investigations. Every pair receives an admitted inclusion, a refutation with its grounds, an overlap plus separating case, identity, or an explicit open result.

The per-item lists and concept matrix are formulas referencing the detailed result tables. Definitions, implication rules, cases, source limits, and deeper reinterpretations are visible in the workbook. Changing a meaning requires re-examining its dependent claims; a spreadsheet formula does not determine the meaning of revised prose.

## Findings from the reversals

**Improvement and development do not require one fixed orientation.** Under the stated senses, development can elaborate a structure while making its performance worse, and improvement can remove a defect without elaborating a structure. They overlap, neither universally includes the other, and both fall under Change in this particular model. Restricting development to advancement under a standard makes it a kind of improvement, but changes what development includes.

**Intelligence as improvement is a substantive reconstruction.** Intelligence in the primary reading is a capability. Improvement is a change with comparative benefit. To place intelligence below improvement, one can investigate intelligent activity as improving the adequacy of available means. This changes the classified item from retained ability to an activity or transition. It raises a real question: how does competent preservation of an already good state fit the account? The reverse orientation must account for accidental betterment and fixed repairs without adaptive intelligence.

**Intelligence as creation changes the account of competent reuse.** The proposal makes construction of distinctions, representations, or possible actions central. Its universal form must identify what is newly created when an agent simply chooses an existing adequate solution. Conversely, bringing something new into existence need not establish adaptive capability or comparative benefit. The capacity, its exercise, and its result must retain their identities during the comparison.

**Two true directions collapse the proposed levels at the chosen extension.** If all A are B and all B are A under the same conditions, the two classes have the same members in that model. That does not prove identical ordinary meanings. Planning and Strategy, under the previously adopted shared course/result sense, illustrate this. A substantive hierarchy would need a further discriminating property.

**A negative subtype result is weaker than disjointness.** A single A that is not B refutes “every A is B.” It does not establish “no A is B.” The workbook separately records overlap witnesses and the specific fixed-sense rules that assert disjointness. Improvement/Development is an overlap case, not an exclusion of shared instances.

**Shared parenthood does not select a unique tree.** Creation is admitted under Process and Transformation; Tool under Instrument and Artifact; Framework under System and Artifact. A graph can retain these simultaneous implications. Choosing one branch for a tree requires deciding which distinction that tree is supposed to expose. It cannot be justified just by deleting the other valid relations.

**Several comparisons concern different predicates.** A method can produce an improvement, an instrument can enable reasoning, and a capability can govern creation without being a subtype of the result or activity. Retaining these relationships enlarges the analysis; converting them all into “is a type of” destroys their distinct consequences.

## What the project comparisons establish

Project Kinds classifies an explicit source-described view: a method specification, organization, artifact collection, or software scaffold. This is kind membership for a particular described item, not universal containment between classes. A repository about intelligence is not thereby an intelligent capability, and an improvement method is not itself an observed improvement.

Project Pairs separately records the proposed universal subtype claims between project families. The inspected profiles do not establish those containments. Distinct repository names are also insufficient to refute them. Their shared described kinds, shared uses, native contributions, and missing discriminations are recorded instead of manufacturing a complete yes/no taxonomy.

The project reinterpretations are substantive proposals: SDS through discovery of standards from contrasts; QuestionRoute through conditional future work; Discovery Engine through changes in what an inquiry can use; GOSM through the relationship between intended conditions and continuing work; Reasoningtool2 through the selection of a fitting contribution. These interpretations do not replace their sources or claim demonstrated implementation.

Master Framework remains source-unresolved. Promoting remains meaning-unresolved. Education, Collaboration, and the previously reconsidered names are included as experimental comparison terms without being reinstated as canonical top-level subjects.

## Reproducible inputs

- [model.json](model.json): primary senses, inclusion assumptions, constructed cases, fixed-sense exclusions, and other relationship predicates.
- [explorations.json](explorations.json): the deeper reversals and source-grounded project descriptions.
- [build.mjs](build.mjs): analysis, consistency checks, formula-driven workbook construction, inspection, and visual rendering.

The builder uses `@oai/artifact-tool`. Run a copy from a writable temporary directory with the Codex primary runtime's `node_modules` linked there. Set `SUBJECTSYSTEMS_REPO` to the actual repository and `SUBJECT_RELATIONS_OUTPUT` to the intended output directory. `--analyze-only` checks the model without creating a workbook. Source profiles are pinned to the inspected Subject Systems commit recorded in the model; this work does not claim to refresh or execute the original project sources.

## What this enables next

A disputed pair now has an exact claim, a reverse claim, known parents, and a concrete reason for its current standing. The next contribution can be a separating case, an inclusion argument, a changed definition with its consequences, or a useful new parent. Reframing can be tested without first condemning the previous frame. Admitting a new interpretation requires preserving what changed, including its unit, target range, and standard.
