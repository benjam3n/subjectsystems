# Extending Subject Systems across existing disciplines

Integrating existing subjects is useful when it increases the actual matters we can identify, investigate, or handle. It distracts from the purpose when importing a discipline's vocabulary becomes an end in itself. The catalog can be broad while development remains directed by a consequential next result.

This expansion defines **85 additional subjects**, bringing the working inventory to **384**, and examines **64 conventional terms across 14 domain views**. It supplies 39 scoped requirement records, 16 separating cases, 15 technique evidence records, 12 claim-review cases, nine candidate gaps, and ten proposed programs. These are representative translations. They do not enumerate all established subjects or certify exclusive placement of the 46 general headings.

The attachment's proposed scope includes the brain, LLMs, psychology, philosophy, business, mathematics, engineering, entertainment, law, government, economics, retention, and the development of subjects themselves. Each has actual defined entries or qualified mappings here, with residual coverage explicitly recorded. Physics, chemistry and many other sciences remain further source material; their names have not been inserted as empty canonical subjects.

## What is being integrated

| Record | What it identifies | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Discipline entry point | An established way to locate a body of work | Psychology; a JEL or MSC category |
| Subject | A specified matter of consideration | Shape representation rotation duration |
| Case | The parameters fixing one investigation | These stimuli, this agent, these timing endpoints |
| Claim | A possible answer with scope and standing | A stated performance difference under those conditions |
| Technique | A way to investigate or produce a result | A particular matching task or solver portfolio |
| Requirement | A condition for a specified operation, result, quality, or justification | A candidate exists at selection time |
| Program | Coordinated work whose contributions span subjects | Resource retention |

A discipline can split into many subjects. Several disciplines can study the same subject. One project can supply different custom contributions to several subjects. Neither relationship forces the discipline or project to become a canonical top-level subject.

The [translation table](DOMAIN-TRANSLATIONS.md) records the exact reading, actual subjects, relationship, residual content, and source. An alias needs scope equivalence. A partial decomposition is not an alias. A general operation shared across different targets does not establish either subject identity or shared implementation.

## Actual subjects include conditions

An operation is not required. Reasoning approach availability asks which approaches a selector can access in usable form at a stated time. It remains a real subject when the answer is none. Equation solution existence and Rule authority basis likewise remain meaningful when the proposed condition fails.

The sentence “an approach is available” is a proposition. Reasoning approach availability names the matter the proposition concerns. Investigating it, arranging access to approaches, choosing an approach, and executing one are different results. They should not be encoded as one mandatory chain.

In this definition, availability means access to a usable representation. It does not automatically include existence in every repository, recognition as a candidate, applicability to the current case, executable inputs, or authorization for an action. Those distinctions identify real causes of failure and different possible remedies.

## Requirements must state their logical force

The [requirements review](SUBJECT-REQUIREMENTS.md) distinguishes six kinds of condition. The most important correction is that a common route to a result is not necessarily required by the result.

Successful approach selection needs an identifiable candidate at choice time. It does not universally require retrieval, several candidates, an explicit ranking, or an optimal choice. A user can supply one candidate. A larger workflow can create one before the act of choosing. A choice can occur under an inadequate criterion; that is a quality failure, not proof that no selection happened.

Alternative routes should be represented as alternatives: direct provision, access to stored material, or prior construction can each satisfy a need for candidate material in appropriate cases. Conditions required jointly should be marked jointly. The present records give scoped individual conditions and countercases; they do not claim to enumerate all sufficient combinations for all 384 subjects.

Case specification, truth, execution, quality, and justified belief also have different requirements. A claim can be true without an assessor knowing it. A method can execute correctly without contributing the required result. A quantity can be measured repeatably without being a valid proxy for the intended property.

## How much common structure can be recovered

There is useful shared structure, but the stronger reduction hypothesis remains open. SAT solver selection is a concrete application of approach selection with published implementation and performance evidence. That demonstrates existing development within one scoped subject; it does not establish a universal selector for open-ended reasoning. [SATzilla](https://arxiv.org/abs/1111.2249)

Our translation proposes connections among selection, representation, comparison, prediction, and other operations across fields. The domain-specific target and constraints still carry indispensable content. Integer factor decomposition, Rule authority basis, and Narrative audience enjoyment cannot be recovered merely by renaming them all selection or resource allocation.

Mental rotation illustrates the required separation. The geometric transformation, an agent's internal representational process, and a timed response are distinct matters. The original experiment reports a relationship between angular difference and task response time. That is evidence about the studied task; treating it as a complete identification of the underlying process would go beyond it. [Shepard and Metzler](https://facultypsy.hope.edu/psychlabs/exp/rotate/readings/ShepardMetzler_1971.pdf)

Brain-function and LLM mappings require the same discipline. The RDoC framework supplies useful functional entry points and explicitly recognizes overlapping constructs. Transformer attention names a specified numerical computation. The shared word does not establish that the model computation and human attention are the same mechanism. The catalog's cross-field connections are authored analysis, not such a source claim. [NIMH RDoC](https://www.nimh.nih.gov/research/research-funded-by-nimh/rdoc/definitions-of-the-rdoc-domains-and-constructs), [original Transformer paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762)

## Development, importance, attention, and success

Each subject now has a development record. These are assessment records, not 384 completed literature reviews. Twenty have an explicit goal-relative importance analysis; selected others have technique evidence. Unreviewed properties remain unknown.

| Dimension | What would establish it |
|---|---|
| Importance | Consequences for specified beneficiaries, goals, costs, risks, and alternatives |
| Recognition | Defined work under the exact name or verified aliases |
| Attention received | A declared corpus, time period, effort measure and denominator |
| Technique development | Specified methods, requirements, available implementations and limitations |
| Technique success | Results for defined tasks, criteria and comparisons |
| Transfer | Evidence that the result or method remains applicable under specified changes |
| Underrepresentation | A discrepancy between measured effort and a justified priority allocation |

A handbook can establish that a practice is articulated without establishing its average causal benefit. An implemented model can demonstrate execution without proving every component's independent contribution. A subject newly named here can already have extensive research elsewhere. No worldwide attention or neglect score has been invented.

The [development review](SUBJECT-DEVELOPMENT.md) therefore distinguishes gaps in this catalog, missing connections between existing work, absent usable techniques for a particular case, and possible global underdevelopment. Only the first two can be directly evidenced by this catalog review alone.

## Handling junk without losing the actual subject

The useful version of a nonsense category identifies the failed object: an undefined target, conflated bundle, unsupported claim, invalid proxy, circular justification, or ineffective method in a specified test. An elaborate theory can be highly developed as a set of assertions and poorly supported as an account of its target.

A precisely specified false claim is still investigable. Its failure may be an important result within a well-defined subject. Likewise, hypothetical objects and normative disagreements are not excluded merely because they cannot be handled as straightforward measurements of existing physical objects.

The review includes constructed cases with exact failure conditions and one source-described empirical example. The phrenology abstract describes a scalp-curvature study; this review does not manufacture a result verdict from the study title. Inspecting the full results is the stated next step for that evidence record. [Jones et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29864593/)

## Resource retention as a worked reconstruction

The proposed hoarding sciences become a resource-retention program with distinct subjects: future-use likelihood, future-use value, retention cost, retrieval cost, disposal consequences, disposal reversibility, retention selection, and retention duration.

These do not imply a goal of keeping more. A better outcome can require keeping, discarding, indexing, converting, or restoring access, depending on the particular resource and purpose. The benefit of an item, its accessibility, and the cost imposed by retaining it can change independently.

Existing archival appraisal already addresses related retention questions, including the value of records that help locate other records. This is a connection to established work, not evidence that a new universal science has been invented. Inventory theory, caching, physical storage and the separate clinical subject of hoarding require their own source reviews and case definitions. [NARA appraisal policy](https://www.archives.gov/records-mgmt/scheduling/appraisal)

## What to develop next

First, complete one declared source slice rather than adding unsystematic coverage claims. Select its version, retain every included source label, record exact readings, map definitions, and account for every residual. This yields a checkable local completeness result while allowing the overall catalog to keep expanding.

Second, develop one complete custom system for a consequential subject. Reasoning approach selection is a useful candidate: record actual available candidates, the chosen means, selection cost, performed contribution, and a comparison that bears on adequacy. Missing availability, poor choice, failed execution, and inadequate result must remain distinguishable.

Third, measure representation in one bounded research or practice corpus, using verified aliases. Compare that effort with a stated importance model. This is the work needed before claiming that an apparently fundamental subject is neglected.

The enduring target is that a finding changes what a later inquiry, system, or decision can accomplish, while preserving the meaning and evidence that justify its use. More names, fewer folders, and stronger claims are not substitutes for that result.
