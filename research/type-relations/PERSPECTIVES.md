## What makes a perspective consequential

A perspective organizes a matter so that particular distinctions and operations become available. Its value can therefore appear before a final answer: a hidden option becomes constructible, an assumption becomes identifiable, or an inference becomes feasible with available resources.

This supports distinct subjects:

| Subject | Exact matter |
|---|---|
| Perspective representation construction | Construct the organization that exposes the selected distinctions. |
| Perspective representation omission | Identify consequential distinctions it leaves unavailable. |
| Perspective consequence accessibility | Determine what an agent can derive with that perspective and stated resources. |
| Perspective change contribution | Determine what the change contributes to a fixed inquiry under a fixed standard. |
| Perspective change criterion drift | Determine whether the verdict changed because the grading rule changed. |
| Perspective scope extension | Construct a justified application outside the previously recorded use domain. |
| Perspective scope coverage | Determine which matters in a declared domain it adequately addresses. |
| Perspective scope limitation | Identify a question it cannot settle under its stated assumptions. |

“Behavior is a perspective” becomes useful when a behavior pattern preserves a consequential distinction or opens particular responses. “Intelligence is a perspective” becomes useful when a capability is reconstructed as access to distinctions, operations and future continuations. Neither sentence, without a mapping and a result, establishes an identity or explains everything about behavior or intelligence.

The [nine worked frame applications](FRAME-APPLICATIONS.md) apply this idea to intelligence, LLM response construction, moral deliberation, planning, decision making, biology, psychology, conflict and subject development. They retain a native matter, a performed reconstruction, its consequence and its limit. Biology viewed through allocation, for example, exposes resources, competing uses and bottlenecks; it does not remove a task's need for kinetic or geometric detail. The useful test is what becomes answerable, not how many fields can be renamed.

## Anticipation and prediction

The proposed distinction has substance, but ordinary-language meanings cannot be imposed on a technical source that defines its terms differently. In the examined active-inference account, model expectations concern hidden states and policies; the term does not require spoken assertions. The later paper explicitly distinguishes reactive prediction from anticipation of future sensory consequences and acknowledges narrow mathematical meanings. [Active Inference: A Process Theory](https://activeinference.github.io/papers/process_theory.pdf), [Active Inference and Intentional Behaviour, glossary §1.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2312.07547v2).

The following are authored subject distinctions prompted by that review; they are not a claim that the cited theory already performs all these operations:

| Subject | Distinction preserved |
|---|---|
| Model state estimation | Estimate an unobserved state from observations; the state need not be future. |
| Anticipation outcome expectation | Determine what an agent expects at a later point. |
| Anticipation action dependence | Determine how expected consequences depend on alternative actions. |
| Anticipation preparation selection | Select what to prepare for possible later conditions. |
| Prediction claim expression | Express a scoped forecast or probability claim. |
| Anticipation response construction | Construct a prospective contribution for the anticipated exchange. |
| Anticipation continuation construction | Use that prospective contribution to construct a further prompt or response. |
| Anticipation continuation exclusion | Preserve the live matter while excluding a specified anticipated way of proceeding. |
| Anticipation outcome correspondence | Compare the earlier expectation with the later outcome. |
| Anticipation influence attribution | Determine how using or expressing the expectation changes the outcome. |

This last distinction matters for LLM work. Anticipating a response and then deliberately producing it may be successful prospective control. It is not, by that agreement alone, an independent test of prediction. The effect on construction can be the intended benefit; it should receive its own subject and evidence.

Intelligence through anticipation asks which operation preserves the continuations needed for a later goal. Planning through anticipation derives what must be prepared before a future condition arrives. Moral deliberation through anticipation supplies possible consequence premises while leaving the justification of the normative rule explicit. Decision making through anticipation can expose an information-producing action or an otherwise lost later option. These are distinct contributions, not one claim that every subject reduces to forecasting.

## Deviation, inefficiency and perfection

Deviation requires a reference. Its extent requires a difference relation. Its benefit requires a baseline, consequences and an evaluative standard. Its creativity or rarity requires further determinations. None follows from the others simply because the behavior looks unusual.

“Anti behavior” therefore remains an entry phrase to interpret, rather than a canonical subject that presupposes its verdict. If the concern is avoidable waste, **Resource use avoidable cost** asks whether an available feasible alternative met the same actual result standard with fewer resources. The comparison must preserve exploration, information gain or option preservation when those belong to the task. Calling a departure creative also does not establish that it was useful; its contribution remains examinable.

**Deviation effect comparison** separates positive from negative consequences under fixed conditions. **Deviation effect robustness** asks whether those consequences persist across a declared set of conditions or perspectives. **Deviation optimality condition derivation**, **Deviation optimality attainability**, and **Deviation optimality case determination** separate a formal characterization, the existence of a best feasible result, and whether a particular case attains it.

The [executed finite case](DEVIATION-CASE.md) makes those differences concrete. Scheduled use yields stipulated payoffs of (4,4), improving both participants relative to chaotic sharing at (2,2). Exclusive use yields (6,0) or (0,6), so scheduling is not best under both individual criteria. There are three nondominated arrangements and no common individual optimum. The full outcome domain has varying payoff totals; the two exclusive-use outcomes alone have a constant total. A local tradeoff therefore does not establish a zero-sum structure for the broader game.

“Good from every perspective” needs a declared perspective domain. With completely unrestricted goals, one can introduce a perspective whose sole objective is opposing the act under consideration. That defeats the unrestricted claim by construction. Agreement across a justified admitted set remains a real and useful subject. “Bad from all,” “good for none,” “better for all,” and “no worse for all with a gain for someone” also have different strictness and baseline requirements.

## Conflict, studies and practices

The general target can be **Conflict resolution method selection**. A particular interpersonal problem supplies the people, incompatible conditions, authority, incentives and relevant completion rule. The people qualifier belongs in the subject name only when that narrowing is the actual matter being distinguished.

Related actual subjects are **Conflict incompatibility specification**, **Conflict resolution condition**, **Conflict resolution method creation**, **Conflict resolution method applicability**, and **Conflict resolution effect attribution**. A list of methods is an artifact. The questions of which method to choose, whether it applies and what it actually changes remain independently answerable.

Studies and practices expose further determinations because studying an operation, performing it, and establishing its contribution can end in different results. A person can resolve a dispute without studying resolution methods; a researcher can compare methods without resolving the live dispute. This is a reason to define those particular matters, not to duplicate every existing subject under Study and Practice headings.

## Precision about names themselves

**Subject name scope underdetermination** asks which needed distinction remains unspecified. **Subject name origin reconstruction** investigates the label's documented history. **Subject name persistence cause** asks why use continues despite an identified defect. **Subject name substitution effect** asks what actually changes when the label changes. None licenses an invented motive for the original author.

**Explanation account operational content** separately examines the variables, relations or transformations doing explanatory work. A literal title can accompany an empty account, while a compressed title can accompany precise definitions. **Expression precision requirement** states how much distinction a particular use needs, so precision does not mean maximal verbal length.

The actual [shape naming execution](APPROACH-SELECTION-CASE.md) uses **Shape representation rotation** for the transformation and **Shape representation rotation mechanism** for how an identified implementation produces it. The earlier human-internal case remains available as a qualification. Stable IDs are preserved. Source titles retain their historical wording. The longer phrase was not defended merely because it already existed.
