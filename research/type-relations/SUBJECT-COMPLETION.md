# Completing subjects before comparing their types

The user's next contribution identifies an operation missing from the first matrix: expand a broad subject into more specified expressions, then examine their types, orders, attachments, and negatives. Intelligence improvement and intelligence maintenance are more determinate subjects than an unqualified Intelligence row. Art writing and writing art may expose different matters. Technical writing and writing technical may preserve one matter while changing the order in which it is indexed.

The extension contains 149 concept-expression rows, including one unresolved Promoting label, and 63 project-specific subjects. It compares those 212 rows against the 70 base kinds. The 211 specified expressions have explicit readings and parent assumptions. It also includes 23 order and scope comparisons, 16 qualification tests, all 58 revisions in the specified About page's main history, and 15 comparisons of historical wording. These counts describe a bounded experiment, not an exhausted language of subjects.

## What the earlier matrix missed

The first matrix selected a primary sense for each word. That made its claims testable, but left many useful expressions outside the classified unit. Intelligence meant a capability; improvement meant a beneficial change. The resulting exclusion concerned those particular senses. It did not decide intelligence improvement, intelligence maintenance, the intelligence required for improvement, or improvement in a method that measures intelligence.

Completing a subject can perform several different transformations:

| Transformation | Example | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Narrow the same class | Development that improves intelligence under criterion C | The resulting class satisfies both Development and Improvement |
| Change which item is considered | Intelligence to intelligence improvement | The capability becomes the target of the classified change |
| Make a relation explicit | Art writing as writing about art | Writing supplies the kind; Art supplies the topic |
| Select a different relation | Writing art as an artwork about writing | The produced item and the represented topic change |
| Reorder one fixed reading | Technical writing to writing technical | Both can denote Writing with a technical-domain qualifier |
| Change nested scope | Improving intelligence maintenance to maintaining intelligence improvement | Improving preservation differs from preserving a gain |
| Change the question | How is art made? to How is its worth assessed? | A creation process and an assessment become different specified matters |

Consequently, adding a word is not always ordinary subclassing. Intelligence improvement is not automatically a narrower class of intelligent capabilities. It can instead denote changes whose target is an intelligent capability. The relation must be retained alongside the nouns.

## A consequential inheritance result

The first comparison refutes the universal claim that all development is improvement. A structure can become more elaborate while performing worse. That counterexample does **not** refute the narrower claim that development which improves intelligence is improvement. The counterexample must satisfy the additional condition before it can defeat the narrower claim.

Three different inheritance rules therefore apply:

- If all A are B, a subclass of A remains within B while the meaning of A is fixed.
- If A and B are disjoint, a subclass of A remains disjoint from B under the same meanings.
- If some A is not B, nothing follows about whether a particular subclass of A is contained in B until the separating case is shown to belong to that subclass.

The new Expression Pairs computation implements this distinction. It derives inclusion from the completed definition and admitted universal implications. For genuinely narrower readings, it derives exclusions only from applicable explicit disjointness rules. The 58 expressions explicitly declared equivalent to a primary sense retain that sense's actual separating and overlap cases; this prevents a mere rewording from manufacturing uncertainty. It never copies a broad separating example into a narrower class without establishing that the case satisfies the qualifier. Unsettled cases remain open. These are semantic-model consequences, not observed effectiveness results.

## What order changes

A fixed structural reading permits more than one useful index. Writing with a technical-domain qualifier can be displayed among kinds of writing or among work concerning technical subjects. Those views need not create two entities. The Subject Completions sheet has topic-first and kind-first indexes linked to the same expression ID within the workbook revision.

Word reversal can also change the actual subject. A method for search is a prescription; a search for a method is an activity. The goal of a question is not the question about a goal. An assessment of truth is not whether that assessment is true.

The six permutations of intelligence, maintenance, and improvement produce a useful explicit comparison when their roles are supplied:

| Order | Selected reading |
|---|---|
| Intelligence maintenance improvement | Improve the process that maintains intelligence |
| Intelligence improvement maintenance | Maintain an achieved improvement in intelligence |
| Maintenance intelligence improvement | Improve intelligence used for maintenance work |
| Maintenance improvement intelligence | Specify intelligence required to improve maintenance |
| Improvement intelligence maintenance | Maintain intelligence used for improvement work |
| Improvement maintenance intelligence | Specify intelligence required to preserve improvements |

These are constructed readings, not claims that ordinary word order uniquely determines them. Even the first order can mean improvement of maintenance **of** intelligence or improvement of maintenance **using** intelligence. Specifying the relation is what separates those two targets.

Negation also needs a scope. Within a universe of changes, not an improvement of intelligence includes changes concerning unrelated targets. That is different from intelligence whose measured level is not improving. It is also different from a goal to improve intelligence that has not yet been achieved.

The compressed form has identifiable uses. Repeating guess method keeps a proposed common parent visible while its candidate members change. Contrasting claim hard and claim not hard exposes a common target with an altered qualifier. Keeping question goal and answer goal adjacent invites comparison of the same relation at different targets. These structural affordances are visible in the text. Their universal superiority or performance benefit is not established by the revision history alone.

The [7 March rewrite](https://github.com/benjam3n/reasoningtool/commit/1249cd873868d491773d882b8eaf2a2bb4c6d359) does several different things. The [9 March revision](https://github.com/benjam3n/reasoningtool/commit/bb2882933321c7eda45d5518067b9aaff81a3879) also narrows the described aim from universally working structures and certainty toward making assumptions, alternatives, tests, uncertainty, and continuation visible. That is a substantive project-description change. It does not establish that either version's empirical or philosophical claims are true.

The earlier essay uses different orders for different purposes; it does not specify a single formal head-first grammar. A useful reconstruction can preserve the explicit roles and repeated positions without treating every earlier fragment as unambiguous or every later sentence as a loss.

## Distinguishable subjects and custom project work

A complete expression does not automatically create exclusive siblings. Writing about technical matters and writing about art can overlap in a text about conservation engineering. A subject index may group several kinds of matter under Art while each fully specified inquiry still needs its own target and distinction to settle.

The project extension accordingly identifies particular work within each family. SDS preference inference, SDS candidate construction, and SDS feasibility judgment have different targets and required contributions. QuestionRoute question identity, conditional course specification, and continuation selection concern different units. Reasoningtool procedure specification, procedure selection, and subject-expression construction are also different tasks. The same treatment is applied to every inspected family, without claiming that the proposed local work is already implemented.

Master Framework's source identity remains unresolved. Its three rows specify source identification and explicitly proposed local investigations, not invented native mechanisms. Promoting remains undefined. No predecessor repository or historical source is changed by this investigation.
