# Literal subject names

The current naming rule is **general subject first, then the more specific target, then the operation, property, or relation being considered**. Use only the words that add a consequential distinction. The user's examples, Reasoning approach selection, Reasoning approach creation, and Reasoning approach modification, identify different results that the older General intellectual operations label concealed.

The [named inventory](../../subjects/scopes/NAMES.md) defines 442 subjects under 52 general headings, with intermediate target groups where they help. Every entry states its matter to settle and a nearby determination it does not settle. These are developed working definitions for the current vocabulary. They do not certify that the general headings are mutually exclusive roots or that every possible subject has been enumerated.

## What makes a name precise

| Name | What completes the specified work |
|---|---|
| Reasoning approach selection | An existing approach is chosen for the matter under stated selection conditions. |
| Reasoning approach creation | An approach absent from the initial inventory is constructed with actual intellectual operations. |
| Reasoning approach modification | An identified approach receives a changed version with its changed operations explicit. |
| Reasoning approach applicability | The approach's operating assumptions are checked against the case. |
| Reasoning approach execution | The prescribed intellectual transformations are performed on supplied material. |
| Reasoning approach execution fidelity | A performance is compared with the specified approach. |
| Reasoning approach contribution adequacy | The resulting contribution is compared with the intellectual result the task required. |
| Reasoning approach selection criterion | What should govern approach choice is specified. |

Selection is a reasonably clear task type here. A particular selection needs identifiable candidates and its target matter. The rule may be implicit; a claim that the selection is suitable additionally needs its governing quality criterion. Those can be supplied by context or by the definition; the name does not need to contain every case parameter.

An approach means an organized way of making an intellectual contribution. It can include candidate construction, representation change, assumption testing, or a newly designed transformation; it is not restricted to a fixed published procedure. Creating an approach requires a usable specification of its operations, not merely a new title.

Creation and modification need an identity boundary. A new version can be both newly created as a version and a modification of an existing approach. Distinguishing the requested results is useful; declaring all creation events disjoint from all modification events would be false under those readings.

## What the disputed names were concealing

Grounds and consequence is replaced by several subjects: Claim support identification, Claim support sufficiency, Claim support limits, Claim inference validity, and Reasoning assumption consequence derivation. Its old matter to settle already contained these distinctions; the heading failed to express them.

Truth boundaries does not select one of those matters. Three possible readings need separate names:

| Intended question | Literal subject name |
|---|---|
| Which situations would make this proposition true? | Claim truth conditions |
| What do these grounds leave unwarranted? | Claim support limits |
| Under which restricting conditions is this assertion being made? | Claim content qualification |

Changing the evidence can change a support limit while leaving the proposition's truth conditions unchanged. An unsupported assertion need not be false. Thus Claim support limits is the closer replacement for the limiting part of A2; Claim truth conditions concerns part of the old A1 scope.

Expression representation is a useful target group for part of Encoding and textual arrangement. Its refinements include Expression representation selection, creation, modification, fidelity, conversion, ambiguity, and compression. Expression structure sequence and Expression structure dependency visibility name other determinations previously buried in the same compound heading. A clearer order of paragraphs does not establish the truth of their claimed dependency.

No canonical subject name joins targets with “and.” Historical labels retain their exact wording in the change map. The rule does not forbid investigating a single relation with two arguments: System interface compatibility is one subject because the compatibility relation itself is the matter to settle.

## The hierarchy relation must stay explicit

Three different structures must not be confused:

1. **The displayed name** orders the general subject before its qualification.
2. **The scope relation** says which specified matters one subject contains.
3. **The entity type relation** says what kind of thing an approach, representation, event, or capability is.

Reasoning approach selection can sit in a subject scope about reasoning approaches without the selection event being a kind of approach. Nor does every extra word simply narrow the same classified object: Reasoning approach selection criterion concerns a criterion governing selection, not another selection event.

Intermediate target groups in the inventory denote the union of their listed determinations. They are partial named scopes, not assertions that every lexical prefix proves semantic containment. The inventory's broad headings are useful starting names; the [case review](../../cases/subject-names.md) explicitly identifies where their global relations prevent an exclusive tree.

The review found a genuine duplicate: Claim inference consequence and Reasoning assumption consequence derivation had the same defined result. Only the latter remains a canonical entry. The former is an alias in the change map.

It also found real specializations and overlaps. Question answer completeness refines the coverage aspect of Question answer fit. Question-specific routes can instantiate plan contingencies. Understanding use transfer is a qualified capability-transfer matter. Intelligence and Understanding can describe overlapping capability aspects. These cannot be asserted as mutually exclusive siblings just because their names differ. The inventory keeps their meanings inspectable while the strict placement problem remains open.

## How this finds more subjects

The expansion follows consequences of the target's actual structure, not a Cartesian product of nouns with verbs. A reasoning approach has operating assumptions, accepted inputs, intended contributions, executions, versions, possible substitutes, and use conditions. Each supplies a different question: applicability, input specification, contribution adequacy, execution fidelity, modification, substitution, and transfer conditions. Those are additional subjects with distinct completion conditions.

A memory record has a source identity, earlier context, version, and status; retrieval does not necessarily preserve them. That yields Memory record identity, provenance, context recovery, status recovery, version distinction, and Memory reuse fidelity. A system interface exchanges specified meanings under conditions; that yields System interface contract, compatibility, and failure propagation.

These extensions can continue recursively. A criterion's justification, a representation's fidelity, or an approach-selection criterion can themselves become targets. An operation is admitted only when the target supports its meaning. A never-created item cannot be retrieved; truth is not modified merely by changing a sentence; support limits do not create truth boundaries. Names for impossible or incoherent combinations do not become completed subjects just because the grammar permits them.

The 27 determination terms in the workbook's Naming Terms sheet record their completion conditions and context requirements. Expansion Families shows eight concrete target families and explains what made their extensions available. Neither is imposed as a universal workflow.

## Projects contribute within the named subjects

The 63 previously developed project contributions now have a binding review: 62 have proposed specializations or components within the literal subject names; one Autophilosophy investigation has no specified conceptual target, so its exact binding remains open. Each retains its particular purpose and proposed result. A contribution-to-subject binding is a proposed specialization or component, not blanket equivalence of the earlier project expression with the new name. A project-specific binding narrows a general subject to a concrete case; it does not mean that the project defines that subject's limits or has implemented every mapped contribution.

For example, Reasoningtool can contribute an exact approach specification or approach-selection mechanism; QuestionRoute can contribute question identity or a conditional question route; SDS can contribute preference hypotheses or discriminating design alternatives. Those are different custom designs within already named subjects. The source profiles retain their original evidence standing. Master Framework's exact source is unresolved; its bindings remain proposals. Project Dashboard's described functions remain unverified here.

## What is established now

The [perspective extension](PERSPECTIVES.md) adds 58 subjects and executes the shape-rotation naming repair. The naming work covers every old scope label and every recorded source family. The [integration extension](INTEGRATION.md) adds representative conventional-field translations, scoped requirements and development evidence; it is not full coverage of those fields. It supplies 442 named determinations, 46 name-change records, 48 substantive boundary comparisons, eight expansion families, and 63 contribution records (62 with proposed subject bindings). Coverage of this source vocabulary is different from coverage of all possible subjects. Mechanical checks verify counts, references, duplicate names, and the no-conjunction naming rule; the case arguments support only the relations they actually examine.

The immediate intellectual task after naming is to settle the actual remaining collisions and derive justified containment for the named matters. A larger exclusive ontology requires a declared coverage domain and exclusion arguments. It is not established by making the list longer or assigning every row a folder.

The earlier subtype matrices remain fixed-sense experiments about their own defined items. The named subject catalog is a later refinement of what to consider; a name change does not automatically rewrite those earlier type assertions. Their qualification rules, explicit unknowns, and historical evidence remain intact.
