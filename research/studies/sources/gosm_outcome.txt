name: outcome_verification
version: "1.0"
description: |
  Final gate for all projects to verify whether the goal was actually achieved.
  Mandatory before marking any project as COMPLETE.

when_to_use:
  - Project reaches final phase
  - All execution gates passed
  - Ready to close out project

purpose: |
  Plans are excellent, but did they work? This gate closes the loop between
  planning and outcomes, enabling learning about what actually drives success.

inputs:
  - Original goal (from context.json)
  - Refined goal (from assessment phase)
  - Success criteria (from planning phase)
  - Actual results (measured)

process:
  step_1_gather_evidence:
    description: Collect all measurable outcomes
    actions:
      - Pull target metrics from plan
      - Measure actual values for each metric
      - Gather qualitative evidence (user feedback, personal assessment)
      - Document timeline vs plan

  step_2_assess_achievement:
    description: Determine if goal was met
    criteria:
      achieved:
        definition: "All primary success criteria met"
        threshold: "100% of must-have criteria, 70%+ of nice-to-have"
      partial:
        definition: "Some but not all criteria met"
        threshold: "50-99% of must-have criteria"
      not_achieved:
        definition: "Primary criteria not met"
        threshold: "<50% of must-have criteria"
      abandoned:
        definition: "Project stopped before completion"
        reason_required: true

  step_3_attribution:
    description: Understand what drove the outcome
    questions:
      - "What percentage of outcome was due to GOSM process?"
      - "What percentage was due to external factors?"
      - "What percentage was luck?"
      - "What was the single biggest factor?"

  step_4_document:
    description: Record for future learning
    outputs:
      - Update OUTCOMES.md in library
      - Complete LEARNINGS.md in project
      - Update procedure effectiveness log

output_template: |
  # Outcome Verification: [Project Name]

  **Verification Date**: YYYY-MM-DD
  **Verified By**: [name]

  ## Goal Achievement

  ### Primary Goal
  **Goal**: [Refined goal statement]
  **Target Date**: [Original target]
  **Actual Date**: [When achieved, or current date if ongoing]

  ### Success Criteria Assessment

  | Criterion | Target | Actual | Met? | Notes |
  |-----------|--------|--------|------|-------|
  | [Criterion 1] | [target] | [actual] | ✅/❌ | [notes] |
  | [Criterion 2] | [target] | [actual] | ✅/❌ | [notes] |
  | [Criterion 3] | [target] | [actual] | ✅/❌ | [notes] |

  ### Overall Outcome
  **Status**: [ACHIEVED | PARTIAL | NOT_ACHIEVED | ABANDONED]
  **Confidence**: [HIGH | MEDIUM | LOW]

  ### Outcome Summary
  [2-3 paragraphs explaining what happened]

  ---

  ## Attribution Analysis

  ### What Drove This Outcome?

  | Factor | Contribution | Evidence |
  |--------|--------------|----------|
  | GOSM Process | __% | [specific examples] |
  | External Factors | __% | [market, timing, luck] |
  | Personal Effort | __% | [skills, dedication] |
  | Other | __% | [specify] |

  ### Single Biggest Factor
  [One sentence: The most important reason for this outcome was...]

  ---

  ## Gap Analysis (If Not Fully Achieved)

  ### What Was the Gap?
  [Specific description of what fell short]

  ### Root Cause
  - [ ] Goal was unrealistic
  - [ ] Strategy was wrong
  - [ ] Execution was flawed
  - [ ] External factors changed
  - [ ] Gave up too early
  - [ ] Other: [specify]

  ### What Would Have Changed the Outcome?
  [Specific things that could have led to success]

  ---

  ## Verification Checklist

  - [ ] All metrics measured and documented
  - [ ] Outcome status determined
  - [ ] Attribution analysis complete
  - [ ] Gap analysis complete (if applicable)
  - [ ] LEARNINGS.md started
  - [ ] OUTCOMES.md entry prepared

  ---

  ## Sign-off

  **Outcome Verified**: [ACHIEVED | PARTIAL | NOT_ACHIEVED | ABANDONED]
  **Date**: YYYY-MM-DD
  **Lessons Captured**: [Yes/No]

integration_with_gosm:
  mandatory: true
  when: "Before changing project status to COMPLETE"
  outputs_to:
    - library/OUTCOMES.md
    - project/LEARNINGS.md
  enables:
    - Project archival
    - Success rate tracking
    - Procedure effectiveness analysis
