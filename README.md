| Subject | Definition |
|---|---|
| [Action](subjects/action.md) | Specified behavioral responses and conditions governing their selection or inhibition. |
| [Anticipation](subjects/anticipation.md) | Represented or inferred expectations of possible later events and their role in preparing, selecting or constructing subsequent activity. |
| [Attention](subjects/attention.md) | Which available material receives operative focus under specified conditions. |
| [Business](subjects/business.md) | Customer demand and cash requirements within specified commercial activity. |
| [Capability](subjects/capability.md) | Usable ability under specified conditions; includes the qualified Intelligence and Understanding refinements below. |
| [Claim](subjects/claim.md) | The content, support, or recorded acceptance of an assertion. Target facts retain their substantive subject. |
| [Commitment](subjects/commitment.md) | An agent undertaking an option or treating it as an operative instruction. |
| [Comparison](subjects/comparison.md) | Comparison structures studied across target kinds, with the standard held fixed. Target-specific comparisons retain their qualified subjects. |
| [Conflict](subjects/conflict.md) | Specified conditions that cannot jointly be met, and the means or effects of resolving that incompatibility. |
| [Criterion](subjects/criterion.md) | An explicit standard governing a specified comparison or decision. |
| [Design](subjects/design.md) | Relations among requirements, candidate forms, feasibility, or use studied across artifact kinds. Particular artifacts retain their own construction subjects. |
| [Deviation](subjects/deviation.md) | A specified departure from a reference, with its extent, causes, consequences and attainable quality kept distinct. |
| [Discovery](subjects/discovery.md) | An increase in what is available to a specified inquirer relative to an explicit earlier position. |
| [Equation](subjects/equation.md) | Existence or uniqueness of admissible solutions to specified equation systems. |
| [Exchange](subjects/exchange.md) | Feasibility or incentive properties of specified exchange allocations. |
| [Execution](subjects/execution.md) | Actual control states of an adopted activity, with its substantive result kept distinct. |
| [Experience](subjects/experience.md) | Discriminable qualitative aspects of specified experiences. |
| [Explanation](subjects/explanation.md) | The relation between an explanatory account and the occurrence or contrast it purports to explain. |
| [Expression](subjects/expression.md) | The form used to express identified content, or its interpretation by a specified recipient. |
| [Game](subjects/game.md) | Specified participant payoffs and their invariance over an admitted outcome domain. |
| [Goal](subjects/goal.md) | Intended outcomes or their achievement conditions. |
| [Graph](subjects/graph.md) | Defined connectivity, path costs, and equivalence of graph structures. |
| [Improvement](subjects/improvement.md) | Comparative betterment of a changed item against a baseline under a specified standard. The change process is a separate subject. |
| [Inquiry](subjects/inquiry.md) | Investigative organization studied as a method for establishing or revising findings. |
| [Integer](subjects/integer.md) | Specified properties and decompositions of integers. |
| [Intelligence](subjects/intelligence.md) | Adaptive use of available abilities when the appropriate means are not fixed in advance; a capability refinement, not an exclusive peer of all Capability. |
| [Learning](subjects/learning.md) | Experience-dependent change in subsequent capability or conduct. |
| [Measurement](subjects/measurement.md) | Defined quantities, instruments, observable proxies, and measurement result properties. |
| [Memory](subjects/memory.md) | Continuity with earlier material under the identity and context needed for later use. |
| [Model](subjects/model.md) | Specified token, attention, position, or parameter computations in computational models. |
| [Narrative](subjects/narrative.md) | Presentation of represented events and defined responses of specified audiences. |
| [Norm](subjects/norm.md) | Justificatory grounds for specified normative conclusions. |
| [Object](subjects/object.md) | Criteria for an object's identity over specified changes. |
| [Perception](subjects/perception.md) | Detection, discrimination, or identification from specified sensory presentations. |
| [Perspective](subjects/perspective.md) | Configurations through which specified differences become consequential for an entity or interaction, including interpretive organizations and their formation, use, comparison or revision. Consciousness and deliberate selection are not required; bearer and mechanism are case-specific matters. |
| [Plan](subjects/plan.md) | Prospective ordering of activities, prerequisites, or conditional continuations. Question-specific routes have their qualified Question entries. |
| [Prediction](subjects/prediction.md) | A forecast specified before its outcome is available to the predictor. |
| [Preference](subjects/preference.md) | What a specified agent favors in specified circumstances, or an account of that favoring. |
| [Price](subjects/price.md) | Demand response to specified price variation. |
| [Problem](subjects/problem.md) | A specified obstruction relative to a requirement, or a remedy's adequacy to that obstruction. |
| [Production](subjects/production.md) | Input requirements and costs of specified output production. |
| [Question](subjects/question.md) | The distinction requested by a question, its possible resolutions, or its relation to a response. |
| [Reasoning](subjects/reasoning.md) | Methods for transforming intellectual material, with their input, intended contribution, operating conditions, or construction at issue. |
| [Relation](subjects/relation.md) | Predicates or their composition studied across target kinds; local instances remain qualified by their own subjects. |
| [Resource](subjects/resource.md) | Means available for a specified use, including their capacities or feasible configurations. |
| [Reward](subjects/reward.md) | Expected rewarding outcomes, assigned value, and discrepancies from their occurrence. |
| [Rule](subjects/rule.md) | Authority, scope, interpretation, compliance, revision, or effects of specified rules. |
| [Shape](subjects/shape.md) | Form and orientation of represented shapes, including defined human representational tasks. |
| [Subject](subjects/subject.md) | Specified matters of consideration, their names, boundaries, or relationships. |
| [System](subjects/system.md) | Specified systems as designed artifacts, including custom descendants, interfaces, or observed executions. |
| [Uncertainty](subjects/uncertainty.md) | Unresolved alternatives relative to a specified agent, information state and target, including how their resolution affects work. |
| [Understanding](subjects/understanding.md) | Usable grasp of specified relations; a capability refinement that can overlap Intelligence rather than an exclusive alternative to it. |
