Experience-dependent change in subsequent capability or conduct.

## Learning change detection

Which relevant ability or disposition changed after the specified experience. Excludes: Counting a changed stored note as sufficient behavioral evidence.

## Learning change attribution

Whether the identified change is attributable to the specified experience. Excludes: Inferring causation solely from before-after order.

## Learning change retention

Whether the experiential change persists across a specified interval. Excludes: Rechecking immediately under unchanged conditions alone.

## Learning change transfer

Whether the experiential change affects a specified new applicable case. Excludes: Assuming that a corrected example establishes transfer.

## Learning change interference

How later experience alters an earlier learned ability under comparable conditions. Excludes: Calling every later failure forgetting without isolating the earlier ability.

## Learning correction uptake

Whether a supplied correction changes the next applicable conduct. Excludes: Merely acknowledging or storing the correction.

## Learning correction generalization

Which further cases the correction legitimately governs. Excludes: Applying a local correction to every superficially related case.

[Correction propagation](../systems/correction-propagation.md)

## Learning correction overgeneralization

A later use that extends the correction beyond its justified conditions. Excludes: Treating every broader application as an error.
