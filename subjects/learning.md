Experience-dependent change in subsequent capability or conduct.

## Learning change

| Subject | Definition | Exclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Learning change detection | Determine which relevant ability or disposition changed after the specified experience. | Counting a changed stored note as sufficient behavioral evidence. |
| Learning change attribution | Determine whether the identified change is attributable to the specified experience. | Inferring causation solely from before-after order. |
| Learning change retention | Determine whether the experiential change persists across a specified interval. | Rechecking immediately under unchanged conditions alone. |
| Learning change transfer | Determine whether the experiential change affects a specified new applicable case. | Assuming that a corrected example establishes transfer. |
| Learning change interference | Identify how later experience alters an earlier learned ability under comparable conditions. | Calling every later failure forgetting without isolating the earlier ability. |

## Learning correction

| Subject | Definition | Exclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Learning correction uptake | Determine whether a supplied correction changes the next applicable conduct. | Merely acknowledging or storing the correction. |
| Learning correction generalization | Specify which further cases the correction legitimately governs. | Applying a local correction to every superficially related case. |
| Learning correction overgeneralization | Identify a later use that extends the correction beyond its justified conditions. | Treating every broader application as an error. |
