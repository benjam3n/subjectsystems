Defined quantities, instruments, observable proxies, and measurement result properties.

## Measurement target specification

The quantity intended to be measured, including its object and conditions. Excludes: Choosing an instrument or establishing that a proxy measures that quantity.

## Measurement instrument calibration

The relation between instrument indications and reference values under specified conditions. Excludes: Removing every source of error or validating the intended construct.

## Measurement result uncertainty

Characterization of the range or distribution of values attributable to a stated measurement result under explicit assumptions. Excludes: The actual unknown error or the truth of every claim based on that result.

## Measurement result repeatability

Variation across repeated measurements under stated stable conditions. Excludes: Agreement across changed instruments, operators, or laboratories.

## Measurement proxy validity

Whether a specified observable supports the intended inference about a target property in a bounded setting. Excludes: Reliable measurement of the observable itself or general validity for every purpose.
