Comparison structures studied across target kinds, with the standard held fixed. Target-specific comparisons retain their qualified subjects.

## Comparison basis alignment

Which features or conditions must be made comparable before applying a fixed standard. Excludes: Altering the standard's justification.

## Comparison basis baseline

The reference item or state used by the comparison. Excludes: Choosing a convenient baseline after seeing the result.

## Comparison result ordering

The ordering induced by the stated values under a fixed comparison rule. Excludes: Adopting the highest ranked item.

## Comparison result incomparability

Why the supplied comparison rule leaves a pair unordered. Excludes: Treating absent information as demonstrated equality.

## Comparison result sensitivity

Which changes in stated comparison inputs would reverse the ordering. Excludes: Changing the criterion without saying so.

## Comparison result aggregation

Whether a stated aggregation rule preserves the distinctions the comparison requires. Excludes: Asserting that one score captures every relevant distinction.

## Comparison alternative dominance

Whether one alternative is no worse on every specified criterion and better on at least one. Excludes: A weighted average concealing a loss or superiority under every possible criterion.

Within [Comparison result ordering](comparison.md#comparison-result-ordering).

## Comparison alternative robust dominance

Whether a dominance relation holds under every admitted scenario with its strictness rule stated. Excludes: Dominance established only after averaging scenarios or changing criteria.

Within [Comparison alternative dominance](comparison.md#comparison-alternative-dominance).
