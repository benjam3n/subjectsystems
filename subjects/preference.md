What a specified agent favors in specified circumstances, or an account of that favoring.

## Preference observation identification

What a recorded choice or rejection actually reveals about the offered contrast. Excludes: Inferring a universal preference from one choice.

## Preference observation context

The circumstances that constrain interpretation of a preference observation. Excludes: Explaining the agent's entire personal history.

## Preference hypothesis creation

Construction of an account of favoring that explains identified contrasts without absorbing every candidate feature. Excludes: Restating the selected example as the whole preference.

[Preference discovery](../systems/preference-discovery.md)

## Preference hypothesis discrimination

A contrast whose possible outcomes separate competing preference accounts. Excludes: Offering alternatives all accounts rank identically.

[Preference question route](../systems/preference-question-route.md)

## Preference hypothesis exception

Location of a case that falls outside the stated preference account despite meeting its conditions. Excludes: A context change already excluded by the account.

## Preference hypothesis revision

Modification of a preference account in response to a consequential observation. Excludes: Recording the observation without changing dependent predictions.

[Preference discovery](../systems/preference-discovery.md)

## Preference hypothesis prediction

Specification of the choice a preference account anticipates before the choice is observed. Excludes: Explaining a choice only after learning its result.

## Preference change detection

Whether favoring changed with comparable choice conditions held fixed. Excludes: A different choice forced by different available options.

## Preference change cause

What explains the identified change in favoring. Excludes: Treating temporal succession alone as a causal account.

## Preference interest identification

Which unresolved matters attract the agent's interest in finding out. Excludes: Assuming interest entails commitment to investigate.
