What a specified agent favors in specified circumstances, or an account of that favoring.

## Preference observation

| Subject | Definition | Exclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Preference observation identification | Identify what a recorded choice or rejection actually reveals about the offered contrast. | Inferring a universal preference from one choice. |
| Preference observation context | Specify the circumstances that constrain interpretation of a preference observation. | Explaining the agent's entire personal history. |

## Preference hypothesis

| Subject | Definition | Exclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Preference hypothesis creation | Construct an account of favoring that explains identified contrasts without absorbing every candidate feature. | Restating the selected example as the whole preference. |
| Preference hypothesis discrimination | Identify a contrast whose possible outcomes separate competing preference accounts. | Offering alternatives all accounts rank identically. |
| Preference hypothesis exception | Locate a case that falls outside the stated preference account despite meeting its conditions. | A context change already excluded by the account. |
| Preference hypothesis revision | Modify a preference account in response to a consequential observation. | Recording the observation without changing dependent predictions. |
| Preference hypothesis prediction | State the choice a preference account anticipates before the choice is observed. | Explaining a choice only after learning its result. |

## Preference change

| Subject | Definition | Exclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Preference change detection | Determine whether favoring changed with comparable choice conditions held fixed. | A different choice forced by different available options. |
| Preference change cause | Determine what explains the identified change in favoring. | Treating temporal succession alone as a causal account. |

## Preference interest

| Subject | Definition | Exclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Preference interest identification | Determine which unresolved matters attract the agent's interest in finding out. | Assuming interest entails commitment to investigate. |
