# Preferences

Standing: Retained root; initial development. Scope and branches below are starting proposals, not an exhaustive theory.

Patterns of favoring alternatives, the contexts in which those patterns hold, and what can be learned from choices.

## Starting hierarchy

- Elicitation.
- Concrete contrasts.
- Context.
- Conflicts.
- Stability.
- Change.
- Predictive use.

## Frontier

What evidence distinguishes a durable preference from a reaction to this example in this context?

Which new comparison would distinguish the remaining explanations of a selection? When should a contrary choice revise a preference hypothesis rather than add a context exception?

## Next substantive work

Use the recorded naming choices to separate explicit preferences from inferred patterns, then identify an unobserved comparison that could distinguish the latter.

## Development standard

Develop the subject’s distinctions, accounts, methods, and evidence at the branch where they matter. A local system can retain, revise, combine, or replace source methods according to this subject’s purpose. A theory of the activity, competent performance of it, and attainment of an associated outcome have separate standards.
