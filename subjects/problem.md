A specified obstruction relative to a requirement, or a remedy's adequacy to that obstruction.

## Problem formulation creation

An obstruction relative to an explicit requirement and situation. Excludes: Assuming a requested artifact is the underlying problem.

## Problem formulation modification

Revision of the alleged obstruction, with the reason for the revision retained. Excludes: Relabeling the same obstruction.

## Problem formulation cause

What produces the specified obstruction under an explicit causal account. Excludes: Merely describing its symptoms.

[Problem formulation comparison](../systems/problem-formulation-comparison.md)

## Problem remedy creation

Construction of a proposed change whose stated effect addresses the specified obstruction. Excludes: Assuming that the proposed intervention will produce its stated effect.

## Problem remedy adequacy

Whether a specified effect, if obtained, would remove or reduce the stated obstruction. Excludes: Establishing that the intervention actually causes that effect.

[Problem formulation comparison](../systems/problem-formulation-comparison.md)

## Problem remedy incompleteness

Which necessary problem condition a proposed effect leaves unsatisfied. Excludes: Rejecting a useful partial remedy merely because it is partial.

[Problem formulation comparison](../systems/problem-formulation-comparison.md)

## Problem remedy displacement

A new obstruction introduced by the proposed remedy under fixed requirements. Excludes: Calling every cost a new problem without a requirement.

[Problem formulation comparison](../systems/problem-formulation-comparison.md)

## Problem remedy selection

Selection of an already specified remedy under fixed adequacy facts and decision criteria. Excludes: Developing a new remedy.
