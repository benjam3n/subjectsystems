Methods for transforming intellectual material, with their input, intended contribution, operating conditions, or construction at issue.

## Reasoning approach

| Subject | Definition | Exclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Reasoning approach selection | Choose an identifiable reasoning approach from a nonempty candidate set available at the time of choice for a specified matter; the selection rule may be implicit or explicit. | Creating an approach as part of a larger workflow, proving the choice optimal, or executing it. |
| Reasoning approach creation | Construct a reasoning approach absent from the stated initial inventory, with executable intellectual operations. | Retrieving an existing approach under a new name. |
| Reasoning approach modification | Produce a changed version of an identified reasoning approach with the changed operations explicit. | Claiming the revision is better without a comparison. |
| Reasoning approach identification | Identify which approach a description or performance instantiates under stated identity conditions. | Choosing whether to use it. |
| Reasoning approach applicability | Determine whether an approach's operating assumptions hold for the specified case. | Ranking several applicable approaches. |
| Reasoning approach comparison | Determine the differences between candidate approaches under fixed comparison criteria. | Adopting the preferred candidate. |
| Reasoning approach selection criterion | Specify what should govern choosing among reasoning approaches for the stated class of matters. | Making a particular choice under an already fixed criterion. |
| Reasoning approach requirement | Specify what a proposed reasoning approach must accomplish under stated conditions. | Prescribing its internal steps. |
| Reasoning approach input specification | Specify the intellectual material an approach accepts, including required distinctions. | Establishing that the supplied premises are true. |
| Reasoning approach output specification | Specify the contribution whose production counts as executing the approach. | Proving the contribution is correct. |
| Reasoning approach assumption identification | Identify the assumptions on which the approach's operations rely. | Establishing whether those assumptions hold in a particular case. |
| Reasoning approach composition | Construct an arrangement of approaches with explicit intermediate contributions. | Merely listing compatible approach names. |
| Reasoning approach substitution | Determine whether one approach can replace another while preserving the specified required contribution. | General similarity of their descriptions. |
| Reasoning approach termination condition | Specify when further execution of the approach should stop for the stated purpose. | Reporting that a particular execution stopped. |
| Reasoning approach failure diagnosis | Locate the approach feature responsible for a specified failure under an explicit failure account. | Treating every unsuccessful result as evidence of an approach defect. |
| Reasoning approach transfer condition | Determine which changes of target preserve the applicability of the approach's transformation. | Inferring transfer from reuse of the same vocabulary. |
| Reasoning approach specification | Specify the operations and their composition that constitute an identified approach. | Claiming a newly written specification describes a newly invented approach. |
| Reasoning approach execution | Perform the specified intellectual transformations on the supplied material. | Merely naming or describing the selected approach. |
| Reasoning approach retrieval | Locate an existing approach matching an identified intellectual transformation need. | Selecting it as best among available candidates. |
| Reasoning approach representation | Express an identified approach so its prescribed transformations can be recovered. | Constructing different transformations while claiming only a clearer description. |
| Reasoning approach execution fidelity | Determine whether a particular performance followed the specified approach. | Establishing that faithful execution helped the task. |
| Reasoning approach contribution adequacy | Determine whether the approach's actual output supplies the fixed required intellectual contribution. | Inferring usefulness solely from procedure compliance. |
| Reasoning approach cost estimation | Estimate the stated time or resource demand of using an approach in the specified case. | Ranking approaches without fixing their required contribution. |
| Reasoning approach retirement | Remove an identified approach from the operative candidate inventory under stated reasons. | Deleting its historical source identity. |
| Reasoning approach availability | Determine which reasoning approaches a specified agent can access in a usable representation at the time of choice. | Existence somewhere, applicability to this case, recognition by the agent, or authorization to execute. |
| Reasoning approach repertoire coverage | Determine which specified reasoning needs have at least one represented approach in an identified repertoire. | Choosing an approach for one case or proving universal completeness of reasoning. |
| Reasoning approach specification completeness | Determine whether an approach specification resolves every execution-relevant choice required by its declared contract, including explicit permitted discretion. | Requiring deterministic choice for every generative method or proving effectiveness from textual completeness. |
| Reasoning approach branch coverage | Determine whether every admitted input or reachable state has a defined continuation or terminal outcome under the approach's rules. | Correctness of the continuation, coverage outside the declared domain, or counting branch labels alone. |
| Reasoning approach rule consistency | Determine whether simultaneously applicable rules can be jointly satisfied under the approach's declared precedence and scope. | Whether the compatible rules are desirable or empirically effective. |

## Reasoning candidate

| Subject | Definition | Exclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Reasoning candidate space specification | Specify which possibilities the reasoning process can currently consider. | Enumerating only the candidates already generated. |
| Reasoning candidate generation | Produce possibilities within a specified reasoning candidate space. | Claiming that the space includes every relevant possibility. |
| Reasoning candidate exclusion | Establish why an identified possibility can be removed under a stated exclusion condition. | Removing a possibility merely because it is inconvenient. |
| Reasoning candidate space expansion | Add a distinct candidate dimension or admissible region absent from the earlier space. | Producing more candidates within the unchanged space. |

## Reasoning assumption

| Subject | Definition | Exclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Reasoning assumption consequence derivation | Derive what follows if an identified assumption is accepted under stated inference rules. | Establishing that the assumption actually holds. |
| Reasoning assumption alternative construction | Construct a specified alternative to an assumption that changes a consequential implication. | A caution compatible with the original assumption. |

## Reasoning error

| Subject | Definition | Exclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Reasoning error location | Identify the particular intellectual step that fails a stated correctness condition. | Locating the implementation defect that caused the step to be made. |
| Reasoning error correction | Replace the identified faulty intellectual step with a defensible contribution. | Merely recording that an error was noticed. |

## Reasoning contribution

| Subject | Definition | Exclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Reasoning contribution requirement | Specify which kind of intellectual contribution would change the current unresolved matter. | Prescribing one existing procedure before identifying the contribution needed. |
| Reasoning contribution selection | Choose the next kind of contribution from the specified available alternatives. | Choosing among approaches that produce an already fixed contribution kind. |
