Methods for transforming intellectual material, with their input, intended contribution, operating conditions, or construction at issue.

## Reasoning approach selection

Selection of an identifiable reasoning approach from a nonempty candidate set available at the time of choice for a specified matter; the selection rule may be implicit or explicit. Excludes: Creating an approach as part of a larger workflow, proving the choice optimal, or executing it.

[Reasoning approach selection](../systems/reasoning-approach-selection.md)

## Reasoning approach creation

Construction of a reasoning approach absent from the stated initial inventory, with executable intellectual operations. Excludes: Retrieving an existing approach under a new name.

## Reasoning approach modification

Production of a changed version of an identified reasoning approach with the changed operations explicit. Excludes: Claiming the revision is better without a comparison.

## Reasoning approach identification

Which approach a description or performance instantiates under stated identity conditions. Excludes: Choosing whether to use it.

## Reasoning approach applicability

Whether an approach's operating assumptions hold for the specified case. Excludes: Ranking several applicable approaches.

## Reasoning approach comparison

The differences between candidate approaches under fixed comparison criteria. Excludes: Adopting the preferred candidate.

## Reasoning approach selection criterion

What should govern choosing among reasoning approaches for the stated class of matters. Excludes: Making a particular choice under an already fixed criterion.

## Reasoning approach requirement

What a proposed reasoning approach must accomplish under stated conditions. Excludes: Prescribing its internal steps.

## Reasoning approach input specification

The intellectual material an approach accepts, including required distinctions. Excludes: Establishing that the supplied premises are true.

## Reasoning approach output specification

The contribution whose production counts as executing the approach. Excludes: Proving the contribution is correct.

## Reasoning approach assumption identification

The assumptions on which the approach's operations rely. Excludes: Establishing whether those assumptions hold in a particular case.

## Reasoning approach composition

Construction of an arrangement of approaches with explicit intermediate contributions. Excludes: Merely listing compatible approach names.

## Reasoning approach substitution

Whether one approach can replace another while preserving the specified required contribution. Excludes: General similarity of their descriptions.

## Reasoning approach termination condition

When further execution of the approach should stop for the stated purpose. Excludes: Reporting that a particular execution stopped.

## Reasoning approach failure diagnosis

Location of the approach feature responsible for a specified failure under an explicit failure account. Excludes: Treating every unsuccessful result as evidence of an approach defect.

## Reasoning approach transfer condition

Which changes of target preserve the applicability of the approach's transformation. Excludes: Inferring transfer from reuse of the same vocabulary.

## Reasoning candidate space specification

Which possibilities the reasoning process can currently consider. Excludes: Enumerating only the candidates already generated.

## Reasoning candidate generation

Production of possibilities within a specified reasoning candidate space. Excludes: Claiming that the space includes every relevant possibility.

[Design alternative construction](../systems/design-alternative-construction.md) · [Reasoning candidate finite generation](../systems/reasoning-candidate-finite-generation.md)

## Reasoning candidate exclusion

The grounds establishing why an identified possibility can be removed under a stated exclusion condition. Excludes: Removing a possibility merely because it is inconvenient.

## Reasoning candidate space expansion

Addition of a distinct candidate dimension or admissible region absent from the earlier space. Excludes: Producing more candidates within the unchanged space.

## Reasoning assumption consequence derivation

Derivation of what follows if an identified assumption is accepted under stated inference rules. Excludes: Establishing that the assumption actually holds.

## Reasoning assumption alternative construction

Construction of a specified alternative to an assumption that changes a consequential implication. Excludes: A caution compatible with the original assumption.

## Reasoning error location

The particular intellectual step that fails a stated correctness condition. Excludes: Locating the implementation defect that caused the step to be made.

## Reasoning error correction

Replacement of the identified faulty intellectual step with a defensible contribution. Excludes: Merely recording that an error was noticed.

## Reasoning approach specification

The operations and their composition that constitute an identified approach. Excludes: Claiming a newly written specification describes a newly invented approach.

## Reasoning approach execution

Execution of the specified intellectual transformations on the supplied material. Excludes: Merely naming or describing the selected approach.

## Reasoning approach retrieval

Location of an existing approach matching an identified intellectual transformation need. Excludes: Selecting it as best among available candidates.

## Reasoning approach representation

Expression of an identified approach so its prescribed transformations can be recovered. Excludes: Constructing different transformations while claiming only a clearer description.

## Reasoning approach execution fidelity

Whether a particular performance followed the specified approach. Excludes: Establishing that faithful execution helped the task.

## Reasoning approach contribution adequacy

Whether the approach's actual output supplies the fixed required intellectual contribution. Excludes: Inferring usefulness solely from procedure compliance.

## Reasoning approach cost estimation

Estimation of the stated time or resource demand of using an approach in the specified case. Excludes: Ranking approaches without fixing their required contribution.

## Reasoning approach retirement

Removal of an identified approach from the operative candidate inventory under stated reasons. Excludes: Deleting its historical source identity.

## Reasoning contribution requirement

Which kind of intellectual contribution would change the current unresolved matter. Excludes: Prescribing one existing procedure before identifying the contribution needed.

## Reasoning contribution selection

Selection of the next kind of contribution from the specified available alternatives. Excludes: Choosing among approaches that produce an already fixed contribution kind.

## Reasoning approach availability

Which reasoning approaches a specified agent can access in a usable representation at the time of choice. Excludes: Existence somewhere, applicability to this case, recognition by the agent, or authorization to execute.

## Reasoning approach repertoire coverage

Which specified reasoning needs have at least one represented approach in an identified repertoire. Excludes: Choosing an approach for one case or proving universal completeness of reasoning.

## Reasoning approach specification completeness

Whether an approach specification resolves every execution-relevant choice required by its declared contract, including explicit permitted discretion. Excludes: Requiring deterministic choice for every generative method or proving effectiveness from textual completeness.

## Reasoning approach branch coverage

Whether every admitted input or reachable state has a defined continuation or terminal outcome under the approach's rules. Excludes: Correctness of the continuation, coverage outside the declared domain, or counting branch labels alone.

## Reasoning approach rule consistency

Whether simultaneously applicable rules can be jointly satisfied under the approach's declared precedence and scope. Excludes: Whether the compatible rules are desirable or empirically effective.
