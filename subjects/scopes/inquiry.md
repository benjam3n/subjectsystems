# Inquiry methods

Standing: Working scope definitions, derived for the present repository; revisable through counterexamples.

The organization and justification of investigative practices as ways of establishing or revising findings. Substantive findings are studied in the scopes they concern.

This parent means the union of the scopes defined below. It does not mean everything associated with its short name. Membership concerns what a specified inquiry must settle, not every object or operation it mentions.

## N1 Investigative practice

Settle how a method or programme organizes questions, observations, assumptions, criticism, and revision to establish findings, including the limitations of that organization.

**Includes:** Research, Science, and Philosophy can be studied as practices here. Their entire contents are not three exclusive sibling subjects. Particular scientific or philosophical inquiries retain definite substantive scopes; broad programmes can deliberately coordinate many of them.

**Excludes:** A particular answer-dependent course belongs to R3; the support of a particular assertion to A2; general construction of an intellectual operation to G2. N1 concerns the method's investigative organization and the grounds for using that organization.

**Boundary case:** “When should counterexamples revise a theory rather than only its application conditions?” investigates a practice. “Does this counterexample defeat this inference?” concerns the specified support relation in A2.

**Next substantive result:** Develop one source-backed investigative method for a particular subject and compare how a consequential result changes its continuation. Do not claim all science or philosophy has thereby been organized.
