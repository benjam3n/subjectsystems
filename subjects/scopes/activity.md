# Regulation of activity

Standing: Working scope definitions, derived for the present repository; revisable through counterexamples.

Which work receives focus and how an adopted activity proceeds in execution. This scope does not include everything merely because it is an action.

This parent means the union of the scopes defined below. It does not mean everything associated with its short name. Membership concerns what a specified inquiry must settle, not every object or operation it mentions.

| Scope | Matter to settle |
|---|---|
| [T1 — Focus allocation](#t1-focus-allocation) | Settle which available content or activity receives operative attention, for how long, and under what switching or interruption conditions. |
| [T2 — Execution control](#t2-execution-control) | Settle whether an adopted activity is initiated, continuing, interrupted, completed, or resumed, and how the control state changes. |

## T1 Focus allocation

Settle which available content or activity receives operative attention, for how long, and under what switching or interruption conditions. The matter is actual or specified regulation of focus among competing possibilities.

**Includes:** persistence, distraction, returning to a live question, and available material failing to enter consideration. Attention does not by itself establish that the selected work is relevant or worthwhile.

**Excludes:** The general contribution relation belongs to G1; merit under an evaluative standard to V3; a proposed multi-step course to R3; configuration feasibility to R4. A capacity to attend is a B1 matter when the issue is capability rather than this allocation.

**Boundary case:** A correction may be available in context while the next response attends to a superseded framing. Availability is unchanged while focus changes.

**Next substantive result:** Identify an observable transition at which available material did or did not influence the live work, rather than inferring attention from mere retrieval.

## T2 Execution control

Settle whether an adopted activity is initiated, continuing, interrupted, completed, or resumed, and how the control state changes. Completion refers to the specified activity and conditions.

**Includes:** continuity across interruptions and distinctions between intended, attempted, and performed operations. The substantive content of a performed operation is not absorbed into a universal Action subject.

**Excludes:** Adopting the activity belongs to P2, arranging its prospective steps to R3, and adequacy of the specified result to the problem to R2. Learning from its results belongs to B2.

**Boundary case:** A repository update can be completed while the larger aim of using recovered understanding remains unachieved. The completed operation and goal achievement have different conditions.

**Next substantive result:** Carry an actual task through an interruption with the relevant scope, permissions, changed premises, and next work intact.
