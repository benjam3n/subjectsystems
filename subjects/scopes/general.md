# General relations and construction

Predecessor scope definition: current literal names and any splits are in the [name-change map](NAME-CHANGES.md). The [named inventory](NAMES.md) supersedes these short labels; the earlier definitions and cases are retained below.

Standing: Working scope definitions, derived for the present repository; revisable through counterexamples.

Specified relations and construction principles studied across target kinds. Generality is part of the scope of the answer, not a miscellaneous destination for unclear work.

This parent means the union of the scopes defined below. It does not mean everything associated with its short name. Membership concerns what a specified inquiry must settle, not every object or operation it mentions.

## G1 General relation structures

Settle the meaning and formal consequences of stated relation predicates and their composition across target kinds. Includes the distinction between necessary, sufficient, enabling, and helpful, and which relation follows from a composition under explicit conditions.

**Includes:** Connections is admitted in this qualified sense. Relevance is the narrower investigation of what a contribution relation asserts and when that predicate applies; the particular contribution of a question, remedy, or expression is studied in its local scope. A bearer's being relevant does not turn it into a separate intrinsic type.

**Excludes:** A particular question's identity belongs to Q1; a particular inference's support to A2; a particular remedy's adequacy to R2; encoding a particular content to E1. G1 does not own their determinations merely because all involve relations.

**Boundary case:** A necessary prerequisite and one helpful route have different modal consequences. Establishing that distinction across uses is G1; showing that a particular file is necessary for this recovery task is a local dependence inquiry in its actual scope.

**Next substantive result:** Develop explicit tests for necessary, sufficient, enabling, and merely helpful relations, preserving the domain in which each test applies.

## G2 General intellectual operations

Settle how operations transform or explore intellectual material across target kinds, how they compose, and what their construction requires. Includes general reasoning methods, candidate search, generation, reframing, and changing the candidate space.

**Includes:** Search and Creativity are not exclusive peers here. A search can construct candidates; a generation method can explore a space. Locating an existing item and constructing a new one are distinguishable changes within such a process. Reasoning is not restricted to inference among assertions.

**Excludes:** Question-specific construction belongs to Q1, goal-specific construction to P1, and recovering earlier material to M1. Validity of a particular premise-to-conclusion relation belongs to A2. A general rule can be developed from local cases, but the scope claimed for its result must be explicit.

**Boundary case:** “What changes when candidate generation varies the dimensions of the candidate space?” can concern an operation across targets. “Which question should this inquiry formulate?” remains a local question or valuation matter, even if it uses that operation.

**Next substantive result:** Derive an operation from two local designs with materially different objects; identify which transformation transfers and which conditions prevent transfer.

## G3 General design relations

Settle how candidate form, requirements, feasibility, and use constrain one another across kinds of constructed artifact. Includes the emergence of requirements through concrete alternatives and the difference between an example feature and a justified requirement.

**Includes:** Design is admitted here as a general investigation of that relation. A particular repository layout, interface, question, or goal still needs its own target-specific construction work. This scope does not absorb every design task.

**Excludes:** Question formulation belongs to Q1, goal formulation to P1, course structure to R3, and expression to E1. Criteria of worth belong to V2. A new artifact kind with no defined local scope requires a scope definition; it is not assigned to G3 by convenience.

**Boundary case:** Liking one candidate's richness does not imply retaining every feature of that candidate. The inference from selection to requirement can be examined across names, interfaces, and other designs.

**Next substantive result:** Compare Goals, Preferences, and Design descendants of SDS to establish which requirement-development relations hold across them and which depend on the target.
