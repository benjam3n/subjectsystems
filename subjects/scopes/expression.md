# Expression and uptake

Predecessor scope definition: current literal names and any splits are in the [name-change map](NAME-CHANGES.md). The [named inventory](NAMES.md) supersedes these short labels; the earlier definitions and cases are retained below.

Standing: Working scope definitions, derived for the present repository; revisable through counterexamples.

How identified content is expressed and how contributions are taken up in an interaction. The substantive matters expressed retain their own scopes.

This parent means the union of the scopes defined below. It does not mean everything associated with its short name. Membership concerns what a specified inquiry must settle, not every object or operation it mentions.

| Scope | Matter to settle |
|---|---|

## E1 Encoding and textual arrangement

Settle how an identified or developing content is represented in a form, and how the arrangement of that form preserves, exposes, or obscures its distinctions. Includes text construction insofar as its particular matter is expression and arrangement.

**Includes:** Writing is a local practice here for ordering and expressing contributions. Writing can also create a question or derive a claim; those substantive contributions belong to Q1 or A2 rather than being reclassified merely because they occur in prose.

**Excludes:** Determining the referents or truth conditions of a particular assertion belongs to A1; identifying a question to Q1. E1 uses those content determinations to examine encoding and arrangement. Actual interpretation by a recipient belongs to E2.

**Boundary case:** Whether two paragraphs make a dependency visible concerns arrangement. Whether the alleged dependency actually holds is a separate substantive question, even when discovering it occurs through rewriting.

**Next substantive result:** Use contribution-construction to produce actual text and distinguish a changed substantive account from a clearer expression of the same account.

## E2 Communicative uptake

Settle what an addressee takes a contribution to mean or require, and how that interpretation is established or repaired within an interaction. The matter is attributed interpretation and communicative alignment. Response fit, substantive correctness, and actual execution are separate determinations.

**Includes:** Instruction interpretation is the narrower prompting inquiry here. Prompt development can also require Q1, E1, R3, T1, and T2; causal accounts and general capability claims require A3 and B1. The broad activity Prompting therefore composes several matters.

**Excludes:** The intended question specification belongs to Q1, fit of an answer to Q2, text arrangement to E1, and an encoded course to R3. Actual execution belongs to T2. Interpreting an instruction correctly does not establish that it was carried out; a compliant-looking answer does not establish the recipient's interpretation.

**Boundary case:** The user can intend “general name” to mean broad enough for the project while an addressee interprets it as a claim of universal capability. The intended matter and the attributed interpretation differ; correcting that interpretation still does not by itself finish the naming task.

**Next substantive result:** Investigate one actual instruction whose anticipated effect differs from the response, with a concrete revision and observed continuation.
