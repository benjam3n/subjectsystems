# Availability of material

Standing: Working scope definitions, derived for the present repository; revisable through counterexamples.

Continuity with earlier material and the increment in what is available to an inquirer. Content, warrant, and capability are not inferred from availability.

This parent means the union of the scopes defined below. It does not mean everything associated with its short name. Membership concerns what a specified inquiry must settle, not every object or operation it mentions.

| Scope | Matter to settle |
|---|---|
| [M1 — Retention and recovery](#m1-retention-and-recovery) | Settle whether and how earlier material can be retained or recovered with the identity, context, conditions, and status needed to reuse it. |
| [M2 — Discovery increment](#m2-discovery-increment) | Settle what became newly available to a specified inquirer or investigation relative to a stated earlier position, and which new distinctions or possibilities that increment opens.. |

## M1 Retention and recovery

Settle whether and how earlier material can be retained or recovered with the identity, context, conditions, and status needed to reuse it. The relation is to an earlier item or state.

**Includes:** reconstruction, retrieval from retained records, loss of context, and recovery of a withdrawn interpretation as withdrawn. A stored false assertion is still retained material.

**Excludes:** Whether the content is warranted belongs to A2 or its substantive inquiry. Whether access gives a particular inquirer something new belongs to M2. General exploration methods, beyond recovery of earlier material, belong to G2.

**Boundary case:** Recovering the old eight-group proposal without its rejected status restores text but loses consequential context. Recovery quality must include that status when it governs use.

**Next substantive result:** Recover a previously corrected distinction and demonstrate a later use that preserves the correction.

## M2 Discovery increment

Settle what became newly available to a specified inquirer or investigation relative to a stated earlier position, and which new distinctions or possibilities that increment opens.

**Includes:** Discovery here means a scoped acquisition or advance in availability. New to the inquirer, newly constructed, and true are different claims. Creation can produce the acquired material but is not entailed by acquisition.

**Excludes:** Continuity with an older record belongs to M1. Support for the acquired claim belongs to A2. A change in usable ability belongs to B1 or B2. Planning the next inquiry belongs to R3.

**Boundary case:** An old question found in a notebook can be new to the present investigation without being newly created. Its earlier existence and its present novelty answer different questions.

**Next substantive result:** Use discovery-progress to distinguish a new distinction, a recovered result, stronger support, and a merely longer description of the same position.
