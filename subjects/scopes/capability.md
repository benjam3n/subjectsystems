# Capability and change

Standing: Working scope definitions, derived for the present repository; revisable through counterexamples.

What an agent can usefully do under specified conditions and which changes in that ability are attributable to experience.

This parent means the union of the scopes defined below. It does not mean everything associated with its short name. Membership concerns what a specified inquiry must settle, not every object or operation it mentions.

| Scope | Matter to settle |
|---|---|
| [B1 — Usable capability](#b1-usable-capability) | Settle what an agent or system can do across a stated range of cases and conditions, including whether it can select and apply available means. |
| [B2 — Experience-dependent change](#b2-experience-dependent-change) | Settle whether and how an agent's dispositions, knowledge use, or capabilities changed because of experience or previous results, and the conditions under which that change persists or transfers.. |

## B1 Usable capability

Settle what an agent or system can do across a stated range of cases and conditions, including whether it can select and apply available means. Distinguish evidence from one performance from a claim about a capacity.

**Includes:** Understanding concerns usable grasp of specified relations within this scope. Intelligence concerns broader adaptation and use of ability here. Their ordinary meanings are not asserted to partition capacities: a performance can provide evidence about both aspects of one capability account.

**Excludes:** Possessing an explanatory account is not sufficient demonstration of using it; the account's explanatory relation belongs to A3. Mere retention belongs to M1, comparative quality to V3, and the cause of an experiential change to B2.

**Boundary case:** A system may reproduce a method description but fail to select it in the case where it would matter. Availability of the description does not establish the claimed usable capability.

**Next substantive result:** Develop a transfer case that requires an appropriate contribution rather than verbatim reproduction of a known procedure.

## B2 Experience-dependent change

Settle whether and how an agent's dispositions, knowledge use, or capabilities changed because of experience or previous results, and the conditions under which that change persists or transfers.

**Includes:** Learning is the working name for this relation. A changed stored record is evidence of a record change; learning requires grounds for the claimed change in the agent or system being studied.

**Excludes:** Whether the change is better belongs to V3. What capability exists now belongs to B1. Retaining an account of a lesson belongs to M1. An artifact repair without the relevant experiential update is not automatically learning.

**Boundary case:** Recording “do not group by relevance” and then repeating the grouping establishes retained advice without establishing the relevant change in subsequent conduct.

**Next substantive result:** Follow a correction into a later applicable case and distinguish changed behavior from a newly written claim that learning occurred.
