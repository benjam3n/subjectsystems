A specified departure from a reference, with its extent, causes, consequences and attainable quality kept distinct.

## Deviation reference specification

The reference state, rule or pattern from which a departure is being considered. Excludes: Calling the reference desirable or treating customary behavior as obligatory.

## Deviation extent determination

The amount or structure of a departure under a specified difference relation. Excludes: Its beneficial effect, creativity or unusualness in a population.

## Deviation cause attribution

What produced a specified departure from its reference under stated causal assumptions. Excludes: Whether the departure was beneficial or intentionally chosen.

## Deviation effect comparison

How a specified departure changes the relevant outcomes under a fixed comparison standard. Excludes: The distance from the reference or its rarity.

## Deviation effect robustness

Whether a departure's comparative effect persists across a declared set of conditions or evaluative perspectives. Excludes: Benefit for an unrestricted set of all conceivable goals.

## Deviation optimality condition derivation

Derivation of conditions under which a departure attains the best permitted result in a specified formal model. Excludes: Evidence that an actual agent attained that result or that the model covers reality completely.

## Deviation optimality case determination

Whether a particular departure attains the specified optimum under the actual case's admitted alternatives and evidence. Excludes: Optimality in a model whose relevant assumptions fail in the case.

## Deviation optimality attainability

Whether any feasible departure attains the stipulated optimum or all required criteria jointly. Excludes: Assuming a supremum is attained or incomparable objectives share one best item.
