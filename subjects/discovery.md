An increase in what is available to a specified inquirer relative to an explicit earlier position.

## Discovery baseline

| Subject | Definition | Exclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Discovery baseline specification | Specify the earlier position against which a contribution is claimed to be new. | Claiming novelty without an inquirer or comparison boundary. |
| Discovery baseline inventory | Identify what distinctions or possibilities were already available within that baseline. | Assuming unrecorded material was absent. |

## Discovery contribution

| Subject | Definition | Exclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Discovery contribution novelty | Determine what the proposed contribution adds relative to the specified baseline. | Inferring truth from novelty. |
| Discovery contribution identity | Determine whether two contributions add the same distinction despite different wording. | Counting paraphrases as independent discoveries. |
| Discovery contribution implication | Identify which previously unavailable possibilities become accessible through the contribution. | Counting a longer description as a new possibility. |
| Discovery contribution dependence | Identify which earlier contributions are necessary for this specified discovery increment. | Treating chronological proximity as dependence. |
| Discovery contribution recovery distinction | Determine whether the increment comes from recovering earlier material or constructing something absent from that material. | Equating new-to-this-inquirer with newly created. |

## Discovery continuation

| Subject | Definition | Exclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Discovery continuation opportunity | Identify a concrete unresolved matter made investigable by the new contribution. | Planning a whole route without identifying what the discovery opened. |
