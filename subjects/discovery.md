An increase in what is available to a specified inquirer relative to an explicit earlier position.

## Discovery baseline specification

The earlier position against which a contribution is claimed to be new. Excludes: Claiming novelty without an inquirer or comparison boundary.

## Discovery baseline inventory

What distinctions or possibilities were already available within that baseline. Excludes: Assuming unrecorded material was absent.

## Discovery contribution novelty

What the proposed contribution adds relative to the specified baseline. Excludes: Inferring truth from novelty.

## Discovery contribution identity

Whether two contributions add the same distinction despite different wording. Excludes: Counting paraphrases as independent discoveries.

[Discovery contribution assessment](../systems/discovery-contribution-assessment.md)

## Discovery contribution implication

Which previously unavailable possibilities become accessible through the contribution. Excludes: Counting a longer description as a new possibility.

## Discovery contribution dependence

Which earlier contributions are necessary for this specified discovery increment. Excludes: Treating chronological proximity as dependence.

## Discovery contribution recovery distinction

Whether the increment comes from recovering earlier material or constructing something absent from that material. Excludes: Equating new-to-this-inquirer with newly created.

## Discovery continuation opportunity

A concrete unresolved matter made investigable by the new contribution. Excludes: Planning a whole route without identifying what the discovery opened.
