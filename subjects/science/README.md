# Science

Standing: Retained root; initial development. Scope and branches below are starting proposals, not an exhaustive theory.

The development of concepts, accounts, evidence, and methods for investigating a subject systematically.

## Starting hierarchy

- Concepts.
- Models.
- Hypotheses.
- Evidence.
- Experiments.
- Explanation.
- Discovery.
- Correction.
- Methods of science.

## Frontier

Which claims about the subject have been established, and which methods establish them under which conditions?

What kind of establishment is appropriate to a claim in this subject? How does a new method change what the subject can investigate?
