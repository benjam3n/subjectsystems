Specified conditions that cannot jointly be met, and the means or effects of resolving that incompatibility.

## Conflict incompatibility specification

Which identified demands, states or commitments cannot jointly be satisfied under stated constraints. Excludes: Treating disagreement in language as proof of incompatible outcomes.

## Conflict resolution condition

What change would count as resolving an identified incompatibility. Excludes: Merely suppressing its expression or assuming one party must lose.

## Conflict resolution method selection

Selection of a method for resolving a specified incompatibility under identified constraints. Excludes: Creating the method or proving that its execution resolved the conflict.

## Conflict resolution method creation

Construction of a method for changing the conditions producing a specified incompatibility. Excludes: Inventing a new method name without executable changes.

## Conflict resolution method applicability

Whether a proposed resolution method's operating conditions hold in a specified conflict. Excludes: Its desirability to every participant or its observed effectiveness.

## Conflict resolution effect attribution

Which changes in the incompatibility are attributable to a specified resolution attempt. Excludes: Success inferred only from agreement with the method's name or from silence.
