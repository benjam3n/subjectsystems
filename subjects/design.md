Relations among requirements, candidate forms, feasibility, or use studied across artifact kinds. Particular artifacts retain their own construction subjects.

## Design requirement elicitation

Derivation of a candidate requirement from identified contrasts among designs. Excludes: Retaining every feature of a favored example.

## Design requirement justification

The grounds establishing why the proposed requirement follows from the stated purpose or constraints. Excludes: Treating an observed preference as automatically mandatory.

## Design requirement revision

Revision of an identified requirement when a concrete alternative exposes a missing distinction. Excludes: Changing only how the requirement is worded.

## Design requirement compatibility

Whether the specified requirements admit at least one joint realization under fixed conditions. Excludes: Assuming separately satisfiable requirements are jointly satisfiable.

## Design candidate space specification

The design dimensions and admissible values within the current design investigation. Excludes: Assuming the initial dimensions exhaust possible designs.

## Design candidate contrast construction

Construction of alternatives that isolate a consequential design difference. Excludes: Comparing alternatives that differ on every feature without interpretation.

## Design candidate feature attribution

Which feature supports a specified contribution attributed to the design. Excludes: Assuming every feature caused the favorable judgment.

## Design realization feasibility

Whether the specified candidate can satisfy the stated cross-artifact realization constraints. Excludes: Resolving a target-specific implementation question by generic assertion.

## Design realization requirement trace

Where each specified requirement is realized or left unfulfilled in the candidate. Excludes: Counting a requirement's mention as its realization.

## Design candidate construction

Construction of a design candidate that realizes specified selected aspects while making its other design choices explicit. Excludes: Assuming all features of a selected example are requirements.
