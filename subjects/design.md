Relations among requirements, candidate forms, feasibility, or use studied across artifact kinds. Particular artifacts retain their own construction subjects.

## Design requirement

| Subject | Definition | Exclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Design requirement elicitation | Derive a candidate requirement from identified contrasts among designs. | Retaining every feature of a favored example. |
| Design requirement justification | Establish why the proposed requirement follows from the stated purpose or constraints. | Treating an observed preference as automatically mandatory. |
| Design requirement revision | Change an identified requirement when a concrete alternative exposes a missing distinction. | Changing only how the requirement is worded. |
| Design requirement compatibility | Determine whether the specified requirements admit at least one joint realization under fixed conditions. | Assuming separately satisfiable requirements are jointly satisfiable. |

## Design candidate

| Subject | Definition | Exclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Design candidate space specification | Specify the design dimensions and admissible values within the current design investigation. | Assuming the initial dimensions exhaust possible designs. |
| Design candidate contrast construction | Construct alternatives that isolate a consequential design difference. | Comparing alternatives that differ on every feature without interpretation. |
| Design candidate feature attribution | Determine which feature supports a specified contribution attributed to the design. | Assuming every feature caused the favorable judgment. |
| Design candidate construction | Construct a design candidate that realizes specified selected aspects while making its other design choices explicit. | Assuming all features of a selected example are requirements. |

## Design realization

| Subject | Definition | Exclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Design realization feasibility | Determine whether the specified candidate can satisfy the stated cross-artifact realization constraints. | Resolving a target-specific implementation question by generic assertion. |
| Design realization requirement trace | Identify where each specified requirement is realized or left unfulfilled in the candidate. | Counting a requirement's mention as its realization. |
