Comparative betterment of a changed item against a baseline under a specified standard. The change process is a separate subject.

## Improvement comparison baseline

The earlier item or state against which improvement is claimed. Excludes: Selecting the baseline to hide an earlier better state.

[Improvement candidate revision](../systems/improvement-candidate-revision.md)

## Improvement comparison criterion

The fixed standard under which the changed item is judged better. Excludes: Inferring improvement from change alone.

## Improvement comparison result

Which stated respects are better, unchanged, or worse against the baseline. Excludes: Reporting only the favorable differences.

## Improvement comparison criterion drift

Whether an apparent improvement depends on changing the evaluative standard. Excludes: Treating a changed criterion as an improved artifact.

## Improvement effect persistence

How long the demonstrated betterment survives under specified conditions. Excludes: Assuming an immediate gain persists indefinitely.

## Improvement effect transfer

Whether the betterment also holds in a specified changed context under a comparable standard. Excludes: Generalizing from one favorable example.

## Improvement effect attribution

What explains the identified comparative betterment. Excludes: Assuming the latest intervention caused it.
