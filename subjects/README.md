| [Measurement](measurement.md) | Defined quantities, instruments, observable proxies, and measurement result properties. |
| [Memory](memory.md) | Continuity with earlier material under the identity and context needed for later use. |
| [Model](model.md) | Specified token, attention, position, or parameter computations in computational models. |
| [Narrative](narrative.md) | Presentation of represented events and defined responses of specified audiences. |
| [Norm](norm.md) | Justificatory grounds for specified normative conclusions. |
| [Object](object.md) | Criteria for an object's identity over specified changes. |
| [Perception](perception.md) | Detection, discrimination, or identification from specified sensory presentations. |

| [Preference](preference.md) | What a specified agent favors in specified circumstances, or an account of that favoring. |
| [Price](price.md) | Demand response to specified price variation. |
| [Problem](problem.md) | A specified obstruction relative to a requirement, or a remedy's adequacy to that obstruction. |
| [Production](production.md) | Input requirements and costs of specified output production. |
| [Question](question.md) | The distinction requested by a question, its possible resolutions, or its relation to a response. |
| [Reasoning](reasoning.md) | Methods for transforming intellectual material, with their input, intended contribution, operating conditions, or construction at issue. |
| [Relation](relation.md) | Predicates or their composition studied across target kinds; local instances remain qualified by their own subjects. |
| [Resource](resource.md) | Means available for a specified use, including their capacities or feasible configurations. |
| [Reward](reward.md) | Expected rewarding outcomes, assigned value, and discrepancies from their occurrence. |
| [Rule](rule.md) | Authority, scope, interpretation, compliance, revision, or effects of specified rules. |
| [Shape](shape.md) | Form and orientation of represented shapes, including defined human representational tasks. |
| [Subject](subject.md) | Specified matters of consideration, their names, boundaries, or relationships. |
| [System](system.md) | Specified systems as designed artifacts, including custom descendants, interfaces, or observed executions. |
| [Uncertainty](uncertainty.md) | Unresolved alternatives relative to a specified agent, information state and target, including how their resolution affects work. |
| [Understanding](understanding.md) | Usable grasp of specified relations; a capability refinement that can overlap Intelligence rather than an exclusive alternative to it. |
