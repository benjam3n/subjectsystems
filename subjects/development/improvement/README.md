# Improvement

[Subject groups](../../README.md) / [Learning and improvement](../README.md)

Standing: Proposed subject. Scope and branches below are starting proposals, not an exhaustive theory.

What becomes better, how that is established, and which interventions or transfers produce it.

## Starting hierarchy

- What could be better.
- Standards.
- Alternatives.
- Interventions.
- Tradeoffs.
- Demonstrated change.
- Transfer.
- Improving improvement.

## Frontier

What became better, why, at what cost, and what supports expecting the improvement to recur?

Can the comparison standard improve while the artifact stays unchanged? What evidence distinguishes a repeatable improvement from a favorable isolated case?

## Next substantive work

Trace one correction into a later applicable use and identify the resulting change and its remaining scope limits.

## Development standard

Develop the subject’s distinctions, accounts, methods, and evidence at the branch where they matter. A local system can retain, revise, combine, or replace source methods according to this subject’s purpose. A theory of the activity, competent performance of it, and attainment of an associated outcome have separate standards.
