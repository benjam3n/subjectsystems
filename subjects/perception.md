Detection, discrimination, or identification from specified sensory presentations.

## Perception signal discrimination

Whether specified sensory inputs can be distinguished by an agent under stated conditions. Excludes: Correctly identifying the external cause of each input.

## Perception signal detection

Whether an agent detects a specified signal against a stated background. Excludes: Distinguishing two detected signals or identifying their cause.

## Perception object identification

Which candidate object a specified sensory presentation represents. Excludes: Establishing the object's persistence across time or its real existence from presentation alone.
