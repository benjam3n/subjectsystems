Expected rewarding outcomes, assigned value, and discrepancies from their occurrence.

## Reward outcome anticipation

What rewarding outcome an agent expects before the relevant event. Excludes: The outcome actually received or the pleasure it produces.

## Reward outcome valuation

The value an agent assigns to a specified rewarding outcome under stated conditions. Excludes: Its objective benefit under an external goal or its delivery probability alone.

## Reward prediction error

The discrepancy between an expected rewarding outcome and its observed occurrence under a fixed coding rule. Excludes: The rule by which that discrepancy changes later behavior.
