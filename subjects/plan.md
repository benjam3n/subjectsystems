Prospective ordering of activities, prerequisites, or conditional continuations. Question-specific routes have their qualified Question entries.

## Plan creation

Construction of a prospective arrangement of activities for a stated aim. Excludes: Adopting or executing the arrangement.

## Plan modification

Revision of the prospective arrangement in response to a changed condition. Excludes: Merely updating which steps have already occurred.

## Plan prerequisite

What must hold before a specified planned activity can proceed. Excludes: Treating one helpful precursor as necessary.

## Plan sequence

The precedence relations among specified activities. Excludes: Inferring actual execution order from the plan.

## Plan contingency

Which activity follows when a stated condition obtains. Excludes: Branching on a condition that changes no continuation.

## Plan gap

A reachable condition for which the plan supplies no adequate continuation. Excludes: Demanding branches for states excluded by the plan's scope.

## Plan fallback

The continuation used when an identified primary route becomes unavailable. Excludes: Repeating the unavailable route with different wording.

## Plan commitment reversibility

Which planned choices preserve or remove later options under stated constraints. Excludes: Assuming that postponing every choice is beneficial.

## Plan horizon specification

The extent of the future for which the arrangement makes commitments. Excludes: Claiming that unspecified later steps are resolved.
