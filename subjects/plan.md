Prospective ordering of activities, prerequisites, or conditional continuations. Question-specific routes have their qualified Question entries.

| Subject | Definition | Exclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Plan creation | Construct a prospective arrangement of activities for a stated aim. | Adopting or executing the arrangement. |
| Plan modification | Revise the prospective arrangement in response to a changed condition. | Merely updating which steps have already occurred. |
| Plan prerequisite | Identify what must hold before a specified planned activity can proceed. | Treating one helpful precursor as necessary. |
| Plan sequence | Determine the precedence relations among specified activities. | Inferring actual execution order from the plan. |
| Plan contingency | Specify which activity follows when a stated condition obtains. | Branching on a condition that changes no continuation. |
| Plan gap | Identify a reachable condition for which the plan supplies no adequate continuation. | Demanding branches for states excluded by the plan's scope. |
| Plan fallback | Specify the continuation used when an identified primary route becomes unavailable. | Repeating the unavailable route with different wording. |

## Plan commitment

| Subject | Definition | Exclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Plan commitment reversibility | Determine which planned choices preserve or remove later options under stated constraints. | Assuming that postponing every choice is beneficial. |

## Plan horizon

| Subject | Definition | Exclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Plan horizon specification | Specify the extent of the future for which the arrangement makes commitments. | Claiming that unspecified later steps are resolved. |
