Unresolved alternatives relative to a specified agent, information state and target, including how their resolution affects work.

## Uncertainty alternative specification

The unresolved alternatives for a defined target relative to an information state. Excludes: Assigning probabilities without a basis or confusing uncertainty with unspecified wording.

## Uncertainty source attribution

Which missing observations, ambiguous representations or model assumptions sustain a specified uncertainty. Excludes: Assuming every uncertainty can be removed by collecting more data.

## Uncertainty decision sensitivity

Which unresolved differences would change a specified choice under fixed decision conditions. Excludes: Requiring every uncertainty to be resolved before acting.

## Uncertainty reduction intervention selection

Selection of an observation or transformation expected to distinguish specified live alternatives under stated costs. Excludes: Seeking information that cannot affect the unresolved matter.

## Uncertainty reduction attainability

Whether specified uncertainty can be reduced using the admissible observations or operations. Excludes: Worldwide unknowability inferred from failure of the current method.
