An agent undertaking an option or treating it as an operative instruction.

| Subject | Definition | Exclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Commitment adoption | Establish which specified undertaking an agent has adopted within its authority. | Preferring the option without undertaking it. |
| Commitment deferral | Establish that an undertaking remains undecided until a specified condition. | Rejecting it permanently. |
| Commitment withdrawal | Establish that an earlier undertaking is no longer in force. | Erasing that it was previously authorized. |
| Commitment reopening | Identify which changed condition makes a prior undertaking subject to renewed decision. | Reopening unaffected decisions automatically. |

## Commitment scope

| Subject | Definition | Exclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Commitment scope specification | Specify the actions, conditions, or duration covered by an undertaking. | Inferring unlimited authority from a related earlier action. |
| Commitment scope authority | Determine who can establish or alter the specified undertaking. | Determining who happens to prefer it. |
| Commitment scope persistence | Determine which commitments remain in force across an interruption or changed premise. | Preserving every commitment regardless of its conditions. |
