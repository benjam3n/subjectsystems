An agent undertaking an option or treating it as an operative instruction.

## Commitment adoption

The grounds establishing which specified undertaking an agent has adopted within its authority. Excludes: Preferring the option without undertaking it.

## Commitment deferral

The grounds establishing that an undertaking remains undecided until a specified condition. Excludes: Rejecting it permanently.

## Commitment withdrawal

The grounds establishing that an earlier undertaking is no longer in force. Excludes: Erasing that it was previously authorized.

## Commitment reopening

Which changed condition makes a prior undertaking subject to renewed decision. Excludes: Reopening unaffected decisions automatically.

## Commitment scope specification

The actions, conditions, or duration covered by an undertaking. Excludes: Inferring unlimited authority from a related earlier action.

## Commitment scope authority

Who can establish or alter the specified undertaking. Excludes: Determining who happens to prefer it.

## Commitment scope persistence

Which commitments remain in force across an interruption or changed premise. Excludes: Preserving every commitment regardless of its conditions.
