Predicates or their composition studied across target kinds; local instances remain qualified by their own subjects.

## Relation predicate definition

What must hold between identified arguments for the stated relation predicate to apply. Excludes: Naming an association without its conditions.

## Relation predicate direction

What changes when the argument order of the relation is reversed. Excludes: Assuming every relation is symmetric.

## Relation predicate inverse

Definition of the relation obtained by reversing a specified predicate's argument roles. Excludes: Treating inverse as logical negation.

## Relation predicate negation

What denial of the stated relation rules out under its domain and quantifiers. Excludes: Treating not-a-subtype as disjointness.

## Relation predicate necessity

Whether the relation expresses a necessary condition for its stated consequent. Excludes: Inferring necessity from one successful route.

## Relation predicate sufficiency

Whether the relation establishes a sufficient condition under its stated scope. Excludes: Treating a helpful contribution as sufficient.

## Relation composition validity

Which relation follows from a specified chain under explicit relation rules. Excludes: Treating every connected path as a valid implication.

## Relation composition domain

Which argument domains make the proposed relation composition well formed. Excludes: Composing predicates whose intermediate arguments have different meanings.

## Relation pattern extraction

A stated relational structure shared by specified cases while omitting designated incidental features. Excludes: Mapping that structure to a further case or claiming that every abstraction is relational.

## Relation pattern case mapping

Which elements of a target case can occupy the roles of a specified relational pattern. Excludes: Creating the pattern or establishing that mapped objects share every property.
