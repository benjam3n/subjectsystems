The distinction requested by a question, its possible resolutions, or its relation to a response.

## Question specification

What a question asks about, including its requested distinction. Excludes: Choosing whether asking it is worthwhile.

## Question identity

Whether two formulations request the same distinction under fixed interpretation. Excludes: Matching their wording.

## Question presupposition

What must be taken for granted for the question's requested distinction to apply. Excludes: Accepting that presupposition as true.

## Question scope

The referents, cases, or time range covered by what is asked. Excludes: Selecting the audience for the wording.

## Question candidate creation

Construction of a question specification introducing a stated unresolved distinction. Excludes: Producing another wording of an already identical question.

## Question candidate modification

Revision of an identified question's requested distinction or conditions. Excludes: Changing only its typography.

## Question candidate selection

Selection of an already specified question to pursue under fixed criteria. Excludes: Constructing an answer-dependent route through several questions.

## Question answer space specification

What differences among possible answers the question permits. Excludes: Treating the currently listed answers as exhaustive without grounds.

## Question answer fit

Whether a response addresses the question's requested distinction with its interpretation fixed. Excludes: Determining whether the response is true.

## Question answer completeness

Which parts of the requested resolution a response supplies or leaves unsettled. Excludes: Asking whether the answer is exhaustive outside the question's scope.

Within [Question answer fit](question.md#question-answer-fit).

## Question answer presupposition correction

How rejecting a false presupposition resolves the original request. Excludes: Silently answering an unrelated question.

## Question answer equivalence

Whether two responses settle the same requested distinctions. Excludes: Requiring identical sentences.

## Question route specification

Which next question follows from each relevant answer condition. Excludes: Changing the identity of an individual question.

Within [Plan contingency](plan.md#plan-contingency).

## Question route branch distinction

Which answer differences require different continuations. Excludes: Branching on wording that changes no next investigation.

## Question route gap detection

Identification of an admissible answer for which the stated route lacks an appropriate continuation. Excludes: A missing answer to an unrelated question.

## Question route termination condition

Which resolved distinctions make further traversal unnecessary for the stated purpose. Excludes: Exhausting an arbitrary number of turns.

## Question answer construction

Construction of a response that supplies the requested distinction with any unresolved truth claims explicit. Excludes: Assuming structural responsiveness establishes correctness.

## Question answer revision

Revision of a specified response to repair an identified failure to address the fixed question. Excludes: Revising a claim solely because it is false when answer fit was already satisfied.

## Question route continuation selection

Selection of the applicable continuation after a specified answer is available. Excludes: Designing the route's branch rules.
