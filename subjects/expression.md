The form used to express identified content, or its interpretation by a specified recipient.

## Expression representation

| Subject | Definition | Exclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Expression representation selection | Choose a form for expressing fixed content under stated requirements. | Changing the content to make a chosen form convenient. |
| Expression representation creation | Construct an expression that represents the specified content. | Establishing the truth of the content. |
| Expression representation modification | Revise an existing expression with the intended content change or preservation explicit. | Assuming every edit preserves meaning. |
| Expression representation fidelity | Determine which consequential content distinctions the representation preserves or loses. | Assuming readability proves fidelity. |
| Expression representation conversion | Translate identified content into another representational form under an explicit correspondence. | Merely relabeling the existing form. |
| Expression representation ambiguity | Identify distinct interpretations permitted by the representation under stated conventions. | Treating every unfamiliar expression as ambiguous. |
| Expression representation compression | Reduce representational extent while specifying what information is retained or lost. | Claiming that shorter text necessarily contains the same distinctions. |

## Expression structure

| Subject | Definition | Exclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Expression structure sequence | Specify the order in which the expression presents identified contributions. | Changing the logical dependence among the contributions themselves. |
| Expression structure dependency visibility | Determine whether the expression makes a specified dependency inspectable. | Establishing that the alleged dependency holds. |
| Expression structure grouping | Determine which expressed contributions are grouped together under a stated representational distinction. | Inferring a subject taxonomy from page layout alone. |

## Expression wording

| Subject | Definition | Exclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Expression wording selection | Choose words for an identified content distinction under stated expression criteria. | Choosing what proposition to assert. |
| Expression wording modification | Revise the verbal form of identified content with semantic differences recorded. | Calling an identity-to-correlation change a grammatical edit. |

## Expression interpretation

| Subject | Definition | Exclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Expression interpretation attribution | Determine what a specified recipient takes an expression to mean. | Inferring interpretation solely from a compliant-looking response. |
| Expression interpretation alignment | Determine whether the attributed interpretation matches the specified intended content. | Establishing that the recipient acted on it. |
| Expression interpretation repair | Produce a communicative change directed at a specified interpretation mismatch. | Repeating the original wording without addressing the mismatch. |

## Expression instruction

| Subject | Definition | Exclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Expression instruction requirement | Specify what the expression directs its recipient to do under its intended interpretation. | Treating an implied unlimited permission as explicit instruction. |
| Expression instruction interpretation | Determine what action requirement the recipient attributes to the instruction. | Establishing execution of that requirement. |

## Expression precision

| Subject | Definition | Exclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Expression precision requirement | Specify which distinctions an expression must preserve for a particular use to be adequate. | Demanding maximal detail in every context or confusing brevity with imprecision. |
