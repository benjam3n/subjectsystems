Selection of a form for expressing fixed content under stated requirements. Excludes: Changing the content to make a chosen form convenient.

## Expression representation creation

Construction of an expression that represents the specified content. Excludes: Establishing the truth of the content.

## Expression representation modification

Revision of an existing expression with the intended content change or preservation explicit. Excludes: Assuming every edit preserves meaning.

## Expression representation fidelity

The consequential content distinctions a specified representation preserves or loses. Excludes: Assuming readability proves fidelity.

## Expression representation conversion

Translation of identified content into another representational form under an explicit correspondence. Excludes: Merely relabeling the existing form.

## Expression representation ambiguity

Distinct interpretations permitted by the representation under stated conventions. Excludes: Treating every unfamiliar expression as ambiguous.

## Expression representation compression

Reduction of representational extent while specifying what information is retained or lost. Excludes: Claiming that shorter text necessarily contains the same distinctions.

## Expression structure dependency visibility

Whether the expression makes a specified dependency inspectable. Excludes: Establishing that the alleged dependency holds.

## Expression wording selection

Selection of words for an identified content distinction under stated expression criteria. Excludes: Choosing what proposition to assert.

## Expression wording modification

Revision of the verbal form of identified content with semantic differences recorded. Excludes: Calling an identity-to-correlation change a grammatical edit.

## Expression interpretation attribution

What a specified recipient takes an expression to mean. Excludes: Inferring interpretation solely from a compliant-looking response.

## Expression interpretation alignment

Whether the attributed interpretation matches the specified intended content. Excludes: Establishing that the recipient acted on it.

## Expression interpretation repair

Production of a communicative change directed at a specified interpretation mismatch. Excludes: Repeating the original wording without addressing the mismatch.

## Expression instruction requirement

What the expression directs its recipient to do under its intended interpretation. Excludes: Treating an implied unlimited permission as explicit instruction.

## Expression instruction interpretation

What action requirement the recipient attributes to the instruction. Excludes: Establishing execution of that requirement.

## Expression precision requirement

Which distinctions an expression must preserve for a particular use to be adequate. Excludes: Demanding maximal detail in every context or confusing brevity with imprecision.
