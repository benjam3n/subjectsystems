Configurations through which specified differences become consequential for an entity or interaction, including interpretive organizations and their formation, use, comparison or revision. Consciousness and deliberate selection are not required; bearer and mechanism are case-specific matters.

## Perspective representation construction

Construction of a specified organization of a matter that exposes selected distinctions and operations. Excludes: Changing only terminology without a consequential representational change.

## Perspective representation omission

Which consequential distinctions of the target matter a specified perspective leaves unavailable. Excludes: Proving the perspective useless for every task.

## Perspective representation equivalence

Whether two specified perspectives preserve the same discriminations and licensed transitions on a declared case domain. Excludes: Literal wording identity or a claim of equivalent implementation cost.

## Perspective consequence accessibility

Which consequences an agent can derive using a specified perspective under stated resources. Excludes: Consequences merely true in the world or present in an inaccessible representation.

## Perspective change contribution

What changing a perspective adds to a specified inquiry or performance with the target and success standard fixed. Excludes: Improvement produced only by replacing the goal or grading rule.

[Mind change selection](../systems/mind-change-selection.md)

## Perspective change criterion drift

Whether a changed verdict follows from a changed evaluative rule rather than a changed representation of the same matter. Excludes: Judging the new criterion illegitimate simply because it changed.

[Mind change selection](../systems/mind-change-selection.md)

## Perspective scope applicability

Whether a perspective's required distinctions and assumptions can be instantiated in a specified new matter. Excludes: Proving explanatory adequacy or equivalent empirical mechanisms.

## Perspective scope extension

Construction of a justified application of a perspective to a matter outside its previously recorded use domain. Excludes: Declaring every subject an instance by redefining the terms after the fact.

## Perspective scope coverage

Which matters in a declared domain a perspective can address under a specified adequacy requirement. Excludes: Worldwide pervasiveness inferred from a few examples.

## Perspective scope limitation

A specified question or distinction the perspective cannot resolve under its stated assumptions. Excludes: A failure caused only by missing resources outside those assumptions.

## Perspective selection

Selection of a perspective for a specified inquiry from stated candidates under a declared selection rule. Excludes: Constructing a missing perspective or establishing that the selected one is universally best.

## Perspective comparison admissibility

Which perspectives are admitted to a comparison and why each belongs to that set. Excludes: A conclusion quantified over every imaginable preference or goal.

## Perspective bearer attribution

Which specified entity, component or interaction bears the configuration responsible for a declared response distinction. Excludes: Assigning consciousness, goals or all downstream effects to the named component.

## Perspective response sensitivity

Which specified input or context changes alter an identified bearer response under declared comparison conditions. Excludes: The bearer consciously noticing the difference or its response being beneficial.

## Perspective response equivalence

Whether two response configurations yield the same specified observable response on every case of a declared domain. Excludes: Identity of mechanisms, experience, cost or behavior outside the domain.

## Perspective change locus

Which specified part of a bearer-context arrangement changed between two response episodes. Excludes: Attributing improvement, causal sufficiency or intentional revision from the changed result alone.

[Mind change selection](../systems/mind-change-selection.md)

## Perspective problem recognition contribution

How changing a perspective changes which problems are recognized in a fixed presented situation under a fixed relevance standard. Excludes: The choice to act on a recognized problem or a difference caused solely by new presented evidence.

[Perspective consequence comparison](../systems/perspective-consequence-comparison.md)

Within [Perspective change contribution](perspective.md#perspective-change-contribution).

## Perspective subject relevance determination

What result from a specified subject would contribute to the current matter under an identified perspective and conditions. Excludes: Technique selection, truth of the result or relevance inferred only from topic similarity.

[Perspective consequence comparison](../systems/perspective-consequence-comparison.md)

## Perspective study configuration construction

Construction of a system arrangement of specified studies whose results supply identified subsequent contributions under an adopted perspective. Excludes: An unordered subject list, automatic truth of outputs or proof of overall effectiveness.

Within [System design composition](system.md#system-design-composition).

## Perspective result interpretation rule

How materially different results of an identified study change a perspective, its commitment or its next contribution. Excludes: Producing the study result or establishing the interpretation rule's truth.

## Perspective transition construction

Construction of an operation intended to make a specified change of perspective available from declared starting conditions. Excludes: Merely naming the destination or assuming the operation necessarily succeeds.

## Perspective transition applicability

Whether the declared requirements of a specified perspective-transition operation hold in identified starting conditions. Excludes: Actual transition success, retention or suitability of the destination.

## Perspective transition reachability

Which specified perspectives can be reached through identified transition operations under declared starting conditions and path constraints. Excludes: Reachability through unknown operations, successful real execution or a universally optimal route.

## Perspective improvement retention

Whether a previously established beneficial perspective change remains available for relevant use after a specified interval or transition. Excludes: Establishing the original benefit, immediate repetition alone or assuming a saved note is used.

[Mind change selection](../systems/mind-change-selection.md) · [Correction propagation](../systems/correction-propagation.md)

## Perspective formation condition

Which specified conditions make a perspective newly available to an identified bearer or arrangement. Excludes: Later activation of an available perspective or universal necessity of one origin.

## Perspective activation trigger

Which event or condition brings an available perspective into operative use in a declared context. Excludes: Its construction, conscious choice or suitability for the context.

## Perspective adoption commitment

The scope and degree of reliance assigned to an interpretation in a particular inquiry or action. Excludes: Evidence-warranted confidence or commitment attributed to every physical bearer.

## Perspective adoption correspondence

Whether an agent's observed distinctions and operations correspond to its declared perspective in a specified context. Excludes: A diagnosis, a hidden true self, complete introspective access or truth of the declared account.

[System description examination](../systems/system-description-examination.md)

## Perspective candidate dimension construction

Construction of a consequential distinction for organizing perspectives absent from the stated initial construction scheme. Excludes: Renaming an existing dimension or adding a label without a separating case.

[Perspective consequence comparison](../systems/perspective-consequence-comparison.md)

## Perspective candidate space specification

The perspectives constructible from declared dimensions, values and compatibility rules under a fixed identity convention. Excludes: All possible perspectives being captured or every combination being semantically adequate.

## Perspective candidate space coverage

Whether a specified perspective inventory supplies required distinctions or contributions across every case of a declared domain. Excludes: Universal perspective completeness or coverage by one individual perspective.

## Perspective exploration exclusion justification

Whether an identified reason justifies excluding a candidate perspective from a specified exploration purpose and stage. Excludes: Permanent rejection, falsity of every candidate claim or final perspective selection.

[Perspective consequence comparison](../systems/perspective-consequence-comparison.md)

## Perspective composition interference

How combining perspectives impairs an identified contribution relative to their separately specified uses. Excludes: Mere component count, independent component failure or automatic incompatibility of different viewpoints.

[Perspective consequence comparison](../systems/perspective-consequence-comparison.md)

## Perspective improvement transfer

Which conditions permit a previously established beneficial perspective change to contribute in a new setting. Excludes: Shared wording, retention only in the original setting or benefit in every related case.
