The relation between an explanatory account and the occurrence or contrast it purports to explain.

## Explanation target

| Subject | Definition | Exclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Explanation target specification | Specify the occurrence, pattern, or dependence the account must explain. | Merely naming the broad topic. |
| Explanation target contrast | Specify why this outcome rather than which alternative is being explained. | Explaining a different contrast using related vocabulary. |

## Explanation account

| Subject | Definition | Exclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Explanation account creation | Construct an account of the specified target's dependence or mechanism. | Merely redescribing the target. |
| Explanation account comparison | Identify how competing accounts differ in their explanation of a fixed target. | Choosing an account solely because its prose is clearer. |
| Explanation account discrimination | Identify an observation that would separate the specified explanatory accounts. | An observation both accounts already accommodate identically. |
| Explanation account scope | Specify the conditions within which the proposed explanation applies. | Claiming unrestricted explanatory coverage. |
| Explanation account circularity | Determine whether the proposed explanation assumes the very dependence it claims to explain. | Treating any shared vocabulary as circularity. |
| Explanation account revision | Change the explanation's dependence structure in response to an identified defect. | Improving only the expression of an unchanged account. |
| Explanation account adequacy | Determine whether a proposed account resolves its specified explanatory contrast under stated adequacy conditions. | Selecting among accounts or claiming that an adequate explanation is uniquely possible. |
| Explanation account operational content | Identify the specified variables, relations or transformations by which an account explains its target and the choices it leaves unspecified. | Treating the title of a theory as its mechanism or requiring every explanation to be a software procedure. |
