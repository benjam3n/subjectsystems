The relation between an explanatory account and the occurrence or contrast it purports to explain.

## Explanation target specification

The occurrence, pattern, or dependence the account must explain. Excludes: Merely naming the broad topic.

## Explanation target contrast

Why this outcome rather than which alternative is being explained. Excludes: Explaining a different contrast using related vocabulary.

## Explanation account creation

Construction of an account of the specified target's dependence or mechanism. Excludes: Merely redescribing the target.

## Explanation account comparison

How competing accounts differ in their explanation of a fixed target. Excludes: Choosing an account solely because its prose is clearer.

## Explanation account discrimination

An observation that would separate the specified explanatory accounts. Excludes: An observation both accounts already accommodate identically.

## Explanation account scope

The conditions within which the proposed explanation applies. Excludes: Claiming unrestricted explanatory coverage.

## Explanation account circularity

Whether the proposed explanation assumes the very dependence it claims to explain. Excludes: Treating any shared vocabulary as circularity.

## Explanation account revision

Revision of the explanation's dependence structure in response to an identified defect. Excludes: Improving only the expression of an unchanged account.

## Explanation account adequacy

Whether a proposed account resolves its specified explanatory contrast under stated adequacy conditions. Excludes: Selecting among accounts or claiming that an adequate explanation is uniquely possible.

## Explanation account operational content

The specified variables, relations or transformations by which an account explains its target and the choices it leaves unspecified. Excludes: Treating the title of a theory as its mechanism or requiring every explanation to be a software procedure.
