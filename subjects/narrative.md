Presentation of represented events and defined responses of specified audiences.

## Narrative event ordering

The order in which specified events are presented in a narrative. Excludes: The chronological order of the represented events or their causal dependence.

Within [Expression structure sequence](expression.md#expression-structure-sequence).

## Narrative event causal coherence

Whether proposed causal links between represented events fit the narrative's stated world conditions. Excludes: Historical truth of fictional events or merely chronological ordering.

## Narrative audience expectation

What a specified audience anticipates at a given point in a narrative. Excludes: What actually happens next or what every audience must expect.

## Narrative audience information access

Which story facts are available to a specified audience at each presentation point. Excludes: What characters know or which available facts the audience noticed.

## Narrative audience enjoyment

The enjoyment a specified audience experiences from a defined narrative exposure under stated conditions. Excludes: Universal artistic merit or enjoyment from every entertainment form.
