# Contribution construction

Subject: [Writing](../README.md).
Source families: [Reasoningtool2](../../../../systems/reasoningtool2.md).
Standing: specified design, version 0.1. Prospective effectiveness is unestablished.

## Local purpose

Construct text through the particular changes in understanding it should make, while keeping its reasoning available for revision.

## What changes from the source

Specialize open operation selection to writing. The operative objects are reader uncertainties, propositions, distinctions, dependencies, and candidate passages; ordering follows what a later passage needs from an earlier one.

## Objects and distinctions

Writing purpose; current reader context; substantive material; unresolved claims; intended distinctions; dependency between contributions; candidate passages.

## Operations

1. Determine the contribution required by the actual request and available context. Keep an unresolved purpose open where writing itself is needed to discover it.
2. Separate the substantive material from claims still requiring a derivation, source, comparison, or other operation. Perform the needed intellectual work instead of covering the gap with explanation.
3. Identify what each prospective part would make the reader able to distinguish, infer, consider, or reconsider. Delete or change a part that merely announces the topic without serving the contribution.
4. Construct an ordering from the actual dependencies among those changes. Develop another ordering or frame if the first hides a consequential relation.
5. Write the passage and inspect whether it performs the intended operation. A declared intention to persuade, explain, or analyze does not count as the operation being performed.
6. If a premise, interpretation, or criterion changes, revise the dependent passages and preserve independently justified material.
7. Return the organized text at the requested scope, with evidence and uncertainty where needed for the reader to assess it.

## Result and consumer

Actual text whose substantive contributions and consequential order can be inspected and revised.

## Quality and defeating case

A fluent paragraph can fail if it does not perform the required intellectual work. Removing all explanation is not a universal rule; its inclusion depends on the contribution the reader needs.

## Continuation

Revise the writing where the inspection exposes a missing operation or dependency. Retain a useful writing case when it changes a later construction.

## Illustrative distinction

Explaining that subject-specific systems would be useful does not specify one. A passage that distinguishes the objects, operations, and outputs of three versions supplies material a developer can actually use.

The example illustrates the specification. It is not a prospective performance result.
