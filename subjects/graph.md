Defined connectivity, path costs, and equivalence of graph structures.

## Graph path existence

Whether a path connects specified vertices under a graph's permitted edge directions. Excludes: The shortest such path or whether a Hamiltonian cycle exists.

## Graph path minimum cost

A connecting path with minimum total cost under specified edge costs and admissibility conditions. Excludes: Existence of a finite minimum when negative cycles or other conditions prevent one.

## Graph structure equivalence

Whether a bijection between vertex sets preserves the specified adjacency relation. Excludes: Visual similarity of graph drawings or equality of node labels.
