Investigative organization studied as a method for establishing or revising findings.

## Inquiry method

| Subject | Definition | Exclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Inquiry method selection | Choose an investigative method for a specified subject and evidential purpose. | Selecting a single intellectual operation with no investigative organization at issue. |
| Inquiry method creation | Construct an investigative organization with explicit ways its results can alter the inquiry. | Relabeling a generic workflow as a subject-specific science. |
| Inquiry method modification | Revise a specified investigative organization in response to a concrete limitation. | Changing only the method's title. |
| Inquiry method assumption | Identify the assumptions required by the method's route from observations to findings. | Establishing a particular factual claim's support. |
| Inquiry method limitation | Identify a class of finding the specified investigative organization cannot establish under its conditions. | Claiming universal failure from one poorly executed use. |

## Inquiry test

| Subject | Definition | Exclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Inquiry test design | Construct a test whose possible results distinguish the specified live alternatives. | Collecting observations that cannot separate those alternatives. |
| Inquiry test interpretation rule | Specify how each possible test outcome changes the status of the alternatives. | Writing the interpretation only after observing the result. |
| Inquiry test confound | Identify an alternative factor that prevents the planned observation from discriminating its target. | Listing unrelated possible problems. |

## Inquiry finding

| Subject | Definition | Exclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Inquiry finding replication condition | Specify what must be preserved for a repetition to test the same finding. | Requiring every irrelevant detail to be identical. |
| Inquiry finding revision condition | Specify which result should revise the finding rather than only its stated application conditions. | Protecting every finding by adding exceptions indefinitely. |
| Inquiry finding transfer condition | Determine which assumptions and case qualifications must hold for an existing finding to justify a stated conclusion in a new inquiry. | Reusing a method without transferring its prior conclusion or copying a result without its premises. |
| Inquiry finding use contribution | Determine how using a specified earlier finding changes the work or result of a later inquiry with its target and standard fixed. | Truth of the reused finding alone, method transfer alone or a merely added citation. |
