Investigative organization studied as a method for establishing or revising findings.

## Inquiry method selection

Selection of an investigative method for a specified subject and evidential purpose. Excludes: Selecting a single intellectual operation with no investigative organization at issue.

## Inquiry method creation

Construction of an investigative organization with explicit ways its results can alter the inquiry. Excludes: Relabeling a generic workflow as a subject-specific science.

## Inquiry method modification

Revision of a specified investigative organization in response to a concrete limitation. Excludes: Changing only the method's title.

## Inquiry method assumption

The assumptions required by the method's route from observations to findings. Excludes: Establishing a particular factual claim's support.

## Inquiry method limitation

A class of finding the specified investigative organization cannot establish under its conditions. Excludes: Claiming universal failure from one poorly executed use.

## Inquiry test design

Construction of a test whose possible results distinguish the specified live alternatives. Excludes: Collecting observations that cannot separate those alternatives.

## Inquiry test interpretation rule

How each possible test outcome changes the status of the alternatives. Excludes: Writing the interpretation only after observing the result.

## Inquiry test confound

An alternative factor that prevents the planned observation from discriminating its target. Excludes: Listing unrelated possible problems.

## Inquiry finding replication condition

What must be preserved for a repetition to test the same finding. Excludes: Requiring every irrelevant detail to be identical.

## Inquiry finding revision condition

Which result should revise the finding rather than only its stated application conditions. Excludes: Protecting every finding by adding exceptions indefinitely.

## Inquiry finding transfer condition

Which assumptions and case qualifications must hold for an existing finding to justify a stated conclusion in a new inquiry. Excludes: Reusing a method without transferring its prior conclusion or copying a result without its premises.

## Inquiry finding use contribution

How using a specified earlier finding changes the work or result of a later inquiry with its target and standard fixed. Excludes: Truth of the reused finding alone, method transfer alone or a merely added citation.
