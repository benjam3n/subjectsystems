# Truth

Standing: Retained root; initial development. Scope and branches below are starting proposals, not an exhaustive theory.

What an exact claim asserts and what establishes or defeats it under its stated interpretation.

## Starting hierarchy

- Claim content.
- Grounds.
- Inference.
- Alternatives.
- Counterexamples.
- Scope.
- Warranted conclusions.

## Frontier

What establishes this exact claim, and which possible case would show the establishment was inadequate?

Which case falsifies the claim rather than challenges its desirability? What assumptions are needed to extend a conditional result beyond the original case?

## Next substantive work

Select a consequential claim and develop an actual countercase or supporting derivation with its scope intact.
