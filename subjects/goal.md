Intended outcomes or their achievement conditions.

| Subject | Definition | Exclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Goal creation | Construct a candidate intended outcome absent from the stated initial goal inventory. | Constructing a means to a fixed outcome. |
| Goal modification | Revise the intended outcome or its scope while identifying what changed. | Changing only the route used to pursue it. |
| Goal identity | Determine whether two formulations specify the same intended outcome. | Equating goals because they share a method. |
| Goal selection | Choose an intended outcome from specified candidates under fixed criteria. | Committing to pursue the chosen outcome. |

## Goal achievement

| Subject | Definition | Exclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Goal achievement condition specification | Specify what must be true for the goal to count as achieved. | Choosing a convenient indicator without establishing its relation to success. |
| Goal achievement indicator adequacy | Determine how faithfully an indicator distinguishes goal achievement from non-achievement. | Observing the indicator in one instance. |
| Goal achievement status | Determine whether the stated achievement conditions hold in the specified situation. | Reporting that the planned work was completed. |

## Goal relation

| Subject | Definition | Exclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Goal relation compatibility | Determine whether the specified goals can be jointly achieved under fixed conditions. | Comparing which goal is more desirable. |
| Goal relation priority | Determine precedence among specified goals under an explicit conflict rule. | Inferring precedence from the order of their names. |
| Goal relation dependency | Determine which goal achievement is required for another specified goal. | Treating one useful route as a necessary goal. |

## Goal means

| Subject | Definition | Exclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Goal means dependence | Specify how a proposed activity contributes to an intended outcome under explicit dependency claims. | Treating every preceding action as a necessary means. |

## Goal representation

| Subject | Definition | Exclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Goal representation maintenance | Determine how a represented intended outcome remains available to govern activity over a stated interval. | Choosing the outcome or establishing its achievement. |
