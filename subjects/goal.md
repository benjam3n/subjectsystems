Intended outcomes or their achievement conditions.

## Goal creation

Construction of a candidate intended outcome absent from the stated initial goal inventory. Excludes: Constructing a means to a fixed outcome.

[Goal discovery](../systems/goal-discovery.md)

## Goal modification

Revision of the intended outcome or its scope while identifying what changed. Excludes: Changing only the route used to pursue it.

## Goal identity

Whether two formulations specify the same intended outcome. Excludes: Equating goals because they share a method.

## Goal selection

Selection of an intended outcome from specified candidates under fixed criteria. Excludes: Committing to pursue the chosen outcome.

## Goal achievement condition specification

What must be true for the goal to count as achieved. Excludes: Choosing a convenient indicator without establishing its relation to success.

[Goal discovery](../systems/goal-discovery.md)

## Goal achievement indicator adequacy

How faithfully an indicator distinguishes goal achievement from non-achievement. Excludes: Observing the indicator in one instance.

## Goal achievement status

Whether the stated achievement conditions hold in the specified situation. Excludes: Reporting that the planned work was completed.

[Goal achievement assessment](../systems/goal-achievement-assessment.md)

## Goal relation compatibility

Whether the specified goals can be jointly achieved under fixed conditions. Excludes: Comparing which goal is more desirable.

## Goal relation priority

Precedence among specified goals under an explicit conflict rule. Excludes: Inferring precedence from the order of their names.

## Goal relation dependency

Which goal achievement is required for another specified goal. Excludes: Treating one useful route as a necessary goal.

## Goal means dependence

How a proposed activity contributes to an intended outcome under explicit dependency claims. Excludes: Treating every preceding action as a necessary means.

## Goal representation maintenance

How a represented intended outcome remains available to govern activity over a stated interval. Excludes: Choosing the outcome or establishing its achievement.
