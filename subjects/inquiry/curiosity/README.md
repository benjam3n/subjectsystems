# Curiosity

[Subject groups](../../README.md) / [Inquiry and discovery](../README.md)

Standing: Retained subject; initial development. Scope and branches below are starting proposals, not an exhaustive theory.

Interest in finding out, including exploration before a definite use or outcome is known.

## Starting hierarchy

- What attracts inquiry.
- Unexplained interest.
- Exploration.
- Surprise.
- Continuation.
- Change of direction.

## Frontier

When does following interest produce valuable discoveries that a specified objective would fail to select?

What distinguishes a promising incidental departure from a diversion before either has produced a result? Can interest reveal a consequential omission in the current goal structure?

## Next substantive work

Follow one relevant incidental observation far enough to determine which new uncertainty it introduces; preserve its relation to the originating work.
