# Criterion discovery

Subject: [Judgment](../README.md).
Source families: [SDS](../../../../systems/sds.md).
Standing: specified design, version 0.1. Prospective effectiveness is unestablished.

## Local purpose

Develop and revise the standard used to judge a particular kind of work when that standard is incomplete or contested.

## What changes from the source

The candidates include standards and their consequences across cases. Judgments can be evidence for a person’s standard or claims needing justification; those tasks remain distinct. The output is a criterion account rather than a winning artifact.

## Objects and distinctions

Target of judgment; actual verdicts; proposed standards; contrasting cases; reasons for verdicts; disagreements among standards; contextual exceptions.

## Operations

1. State what kind of judgment is being developed: for example preference, validity of inference, fitness for use, or contribution to a purpose. Preserve unresolved disagreement over that target.
2. Collect the cases that distinguish the live proposed standards, including a case where an initially plausible standard gives a problematic verdict.
3. Generate alternative standards that account for the contrast. Derive what each would require in a different case before accepting it.
4. Separate an observed verdict from its justification. A user judgment can establish what the user chose; a claim of validity requires its own grounds.
5. Apply the candidate standards to the contrasting cases. Keep a contextual rule or an unresolved conflict where a single rule would erase a consequential difference.
6. Revise the standard or its scope and reopen the verdicts that depended on it. Leave unaffected factual claims and independent grounds intact.
7. Return the criterion, the comparison that motivates it, its grounds and scope, and the verdicts requiring reconsideration.

## Result and consumer

A justified or explicitly provisional standard with its consequences for actual judgments.

## Quality and defeating case

Matching known ratings alone does not establish that a standard is appropriate. A revision that merely exempts every contrary case has not resolved the conflict.

## Continuation

Use the criterion where the next real judgment needs it. Reopen its scope when a new case exposes a consequential difference.

## Illustrative distinction

Requesting a lower claim than kit changes the naming standard. It does not make the earlier positive reaction false or prove that Kit is always a poor name.

The example illustrates the specification. It is not a prospective performance result.
