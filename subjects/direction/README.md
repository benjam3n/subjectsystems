# Direction and choice

[Subject groups](../README.md)

Standing: Working functional group. The grouping layer is user-directed; this particular name, boundary, and membership are revisable implementation choices.

Form purposes, establish what matters, judge alternatives, and make commitments.

## Subjects and their roles

| Subject | Why it belongs here | Starting investigations | Standing |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Goals](goals/README.md) | Develops what to pursue and what its achievement would mean. | Origin; formation; higher purposes; alternatives; conflicts; commitment; revision; achievement. | Retained subject; initial development |
| [Preferences](preferences/README.md) | Distinguishes which alternatives are favored and under which conditions. | Elicitation; concrete contrasts; context; conflicts; stability; change; predictive use. | Retained subject; initial development |
| [Values](values/README.md) | Examines worth, importance, and reasons for favoring one possibility. | Worth; importance; reasons for favoring; conflicting commitments; consequences; revision. | Retained subject; initial development |
| [Judgment](judgment/README.md) | Develops applicable standards and reaches or revises verdicts. | What is being judged; applicable standards; comparison; evidence; conflicts; verdict; revision. | Retained subject; initial development |
| [Decisions](decisions/README.md) | Resolves commitments among alternatives, including deferral and reversal. | Alternatives; consequences; criteria; uncertainty; timing; commitment; reversal; revision. | Retained subject; initial development |

## Boundary and connections

This group concerns purposes, standards, and commitments. A preference, a judgment, a decision, and an achieved goal remain different objects. Its placement of Judgment includes factual and aesthetic judgments as well as choices about action.

Each subject has one main home for navigation and can contribute across groups. The shared function is not a claim that every member performs every part of it, a reduction of each subject to that function, or a compulsory work sequence.

| Subject elsewhere | Connection that matters |
|---|---|
| [Truth](../understanding/truth/README.md) | A favorable verdict does not settle whether its supporting account is true. |
| [Planning](../execution/planning/README.md) | A commitment supplies something to plan around; new feasibility information can reopen that commitment. |
| [Design](../creation/design/README.md) | Encountering a concrete design can reveal a purpose or criterion that was unavailable beforehand. |

## Shared investigations

- When does a concrete preference supply a reason to adopt a goal, and what additional operation is needed when it does not?
- Which revisions to a criterion require reopening a verdict, a commitment, or the goal itself?

The subject and particular purpose still determine each custom system. Group membership does not impose one procedure on its members. See [customization](../../CUSTOMIZATION.md) and the [grouping decision](../../decisions/0004-functional-groups.md).
