The content, support, or recorded acceptance of an assertion. Target facts retain their substantive subject.

## Claim content

| Subject | Definition | Exclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Claim content specification | Specify what an assertion says under an explicit interpretation. | Establishing whether its assertion is true. |
| Claim content reference | Identify what the assertion's expressions refer to in the stated context. | Checking whether evidence supports the assertion. |
| Claim content quantifier scope | Determine which cases an assertion ranges over and where its quantifiers apply. | Replacing an all-case claim with a typical-case claim. |
| Claim content qualification | Specify the conditions restricting the assertion's applicability. | Quietly adding a condition after a counterexample. |
| Claim content equivalence | Determine whether two assertions have the same stated content under a fixed interpretation. | Treating association or implication in one direction as identity. |

## Claim truth

| Subject | Definition | Exclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Claim truth conditions | Specify which situations make the identified proposition true under its interpretation. | Determining what the available evidence warrants. |
| Claim truth condition satisfaction | Determine whether the specified situation satisfies a formalized proposition's truth conditions, with its domain facts supplied. | Absorbing collection of every substantive fact into a universal Truth subject. |

## Claim support

| Subject | Definition | Exclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Claim support identification | Identify which reasons or observations bear on the specified claim and through what stated support relation. | Treating every cited source as support. |
| Claim support sufficiency | Determine whether the supplied grounds meet a stated acceptance threshold for this claim. | Describing the agent's actual confidence. |
| Claim support limits | Specify which conclusions remain unwarranted by the supplied grounds under the stated support rule. | Claiming that an unsupported conclusion is false. |
| Claim support dependence | Determine which support paths rely on the same premise or source rather than providing independent grounds. | Counting repeated restatements as independent evidence. |
| Claim support defeater | Identify a condition that undermines the specified support relation. | A generic warning compatible with that support remaining adequate. |
| Claim support preservation | Determine which grounds still support the claim after a specified premise or source correction. | Discarding all support because one path failed. |

## Claim inference

| Subject | Definition | Exclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Claim inference validity | Determine whether the identified conclusion follows from the supplied premises under stated rules. | Establishing the truth of the premises. |
| Claim inference counterexample | Construct an admissible case with true premises and a false conclusion for the specified inference. | A case with a false premise. |
| Claim inference specification | Fix the premises, conclusion, and alleged consequence relation of the argument to be assessed. | Assessing an unspecified impression that the argument is convincing. |
| Claim inference revision | Change the identified premises or inference step to address a demonstrated defect. | Claiming the unchanged conclusion must be false because its old argument failed. |

## Claim acceptance

| Subject | Definition | Exclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Claim acceptance status | Determine whether an identified record treats the claim as accepted, rejected, conditional, or unresolved. | Establishing that this treatment is justified. |
| Claim acceptance confidence | Identify the degree or form of confidence actually represented for the claim. | Inventing a probability where none is given. |
| Claim acceptance alternatives | Specify which competing possibilities the record continues to leave open. | Treating an unmentioned possibility as ruled out. |
| Claim acceptance revision | Determine which recorded claim status changes in response to a stated correction. | Claiming that the corrected record was subsequently used. |
