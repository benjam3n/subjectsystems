A forecast specified before its outcome is available to the predictor.

## Prediction target specification

The outcome variable whose future or unknown value is forecast. Excludes: Explaining an already known outcome.

## Prediction target horizon

The time or event boundary at which the prediction is to be resolved. Excludes: Choosing a convenient horizon after the observation.

## Prediction construction

Production of a forecast for the specified target from the information available at prediction time. Excludes: Retrofitting a forecast after seeing the outcome.

## Prediction information boundary

The information legitimately available to the predictor when the forecast was made. Excludes: Including later observations in the original evidence base.

## Prediction uncertainty representation

Which possible outcomes or probability assignments the forecast expresses. Excludes: Treating omitted alternatives as impossible.

## Prediction resolution

The observed outcome under the previously specified resolution rule. Excludes: Changing the target to match the forecast.

## Prediction error

The difference between forecast and outcome under a fixed error definition. Excludes: Revising the error measure after seeing comparative results.

## Prediction calibration

Comparison of stated probabilities with observed frequencies over a specified admissible set of forecasts. Excludes: Inferring calibration from one successful forecast.

## Prediction revision

Updating of the forecast using identified new information while preserving its prior version. Excludes: Overwriting the old forecast as if it had always predicted the new result.

## Prediction claim expression

Expression of a claim about a specified future or unknown outcome with its scope and epistemic force explicit. Excludes: Having an unexpressed expectation, estimating a hidden current state, or preparing for an outcome.
