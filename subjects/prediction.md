A forecast specified before its outcome is available to the predictor.

## Prediction target

| Subject | Definition | Exclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Prediction target specification | Specify the outcome variable whose future or unknown value is forecast. | Explaining an already known outcome. |
| Prediction target horizon | Specify the time or event boundary at which the prediction is to be resolved. | Choosing a convenient horizon after the observation. |

| Subject | Definition | Exclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Prediction construction | Produce a forecast for the specified target from the information available at prediction time. | Retrofitting a forecast after seeing the outcome. |
| Prediction information boundary | Identify the information legitimately available to the predictor when the forecast was made. | Including later observations in the original evidence base. |
| Prediction uncertainty representation | Specify which possible outcomes or probability assignments the forecast expresses. | Treating omitted alternatives as impossible. |
| Prediction resolution | Determine the observed outcome under the previously specified resolution rule. | Changing the target to match the forecast. |
| Prediction error | Determine the difference between forecast and outcome under a fixed error definition. | Revising the error measure after seeing comparative results. |
| Prediction calibration | Compare stated probabilities with observed frequencies over a specified admissible set of forecasts. | Inferring calibration from one successful forecast. |
| Prediction revision | Update the forecast using identified new information while preserving its prior version. | Overwriting the old forecast as if it had always predicted the new result. |

## Prediction claim

| Subject | Definition | Exclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Prediction claim expression | Express a claim about a specified future or unknown outcome with its scope and epistemic force explicit. | Having an unexpressed expectation, estimating a hidden current state, or preparing for an outcome. |
