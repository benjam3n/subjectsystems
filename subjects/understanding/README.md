# Understanding

Standing: Retained root; initial development. Scope and branches below are starting proposals, not an exhaustive theory.

Accounts and distinctions that make a subject more intelligible and permit further work with it.

## Starting hierarchy

- Distinctions.
- Relations.
- Causes.
- Mechanisms.
- Contexts.
- Alternative accounts.
- Transfer.

## Frontier

Which new inferences or interventions become possible when understanding improves?

Can a new frame improve understanding without adding facts? Which differences between two accounts produce different inferences or interventions?

## Next substantive work

Reconstruct one subject through two substantially different frames and identify a consequence available in only one account.

## Development standard

Develop the subject’s distinctions, accounts, methods, and evidence at the branch where they matter. A source method is one possible contribution. A theory of the activity, competent performance of it, and attainment of an associated outcome have separate standards. See [perfection](../../PERFECTION.md) and [the current contract](../../CURRENT.md).
