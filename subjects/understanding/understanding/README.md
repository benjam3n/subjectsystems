# Understanding

[Subject groups](../../README.md) / [Reasoning and understanding](../README.md)

Standing: Retained subject; initial development. Scope and branches below are starting proposals, not an exhaustive theory.

Accounts and distinctions that make a subject more intelligible and permit further work with it.

## Starting hierarchy

- Distinctions.
- Relations.
- Causes.
- Mechanisms.
- Contexts.
- Alternative accounts.
- Transfer.

## Frontier

Which new inferences or interventions become possible when understanding improves?

Can a new frame improve understanding without adding facts? Which differences between two accounts produce different inferences or interventions?

## Next substantive work

## Development standard

Develop the subject’s distinctions, accounts, methods, and evidence at the branch where they matter. A local system can retain, revise, combine, or replace source methods according to this subject’s purpose. A theory of the activity, competent performance of it, and attainment of an associated outcome have separate standards.
